# Growth Surface

Any channel where a business builds demand — LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email, outbound.

## Why it matters

Search is shifting from ten blue links to AI-synthesized answers. Understanding this term is part of staying visible in both.

## Common mistakes

- Treating it as a hack instead of building real, cite-worthy information
- Chasing volume over accuracy
- Ignoring the underlying search infrastructure it depends on
