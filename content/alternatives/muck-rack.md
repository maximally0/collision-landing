# Muck Rack alternatives

AI communications platform with a journalist database, media monitoring and AI agents (Media List Agent, pitch assistant) for smarter pitching.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Muck Rack — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Muck Rack does well

700K+ journalist profiles; AI media-list building agent; AI pitch drafting; placement tracking; monitoring alerts; coverage reporting

## What Muck Rack costs

- **Pricing:** Custom (annual) (Annual subscription)
- **Positioning:** Largest verified journalist database plus agentic media-list workflow (10% avg pitch open/click lift in beta)
- **Best for:** PR/comm teams, startups and agencies

_Pricing verified 2026-08-14._

## Where Muck Rack stops

PR-vertical only; no content, SEO or ad execution; opaque enterprise-leaning pricing; pitching still human-in-the-loop

Capabilities Muck Rack doesn't cover that Collision does: Lead generation, GEO, Sales, LinkedIn growth, PR, SEO.

## The best Muck Rack alternatives

- **[Meltwater](/alternatives/meltwater)** — Scale (27K orgs, 1.3B docs/day) plus AI features and an MCP connector bridging media intelligence into AI agents
- **[PRophet](/alternatives/prophet)** — Predictive (not just generative) AI for PR - forecasts journalist interest and sentiment pre-pitch
- **[Signal AI](/alternatives/signal-ai)** — AI-native (founded on NLP/ML) risk+reputation intelligence now majority-backed by Battery Ventures
- **[PressPulse AI](/alternatives/presspulse-ai)** — True AI agent for HARO-style PR lead-gen versus manual query sifting
- **[AdCreative.ai](/alternatives/adcreative-ai)** — Predictive conversion scoring of creatives before launch, trained on performance data
- **[Adzooma](/alternatives/adzooma)** — Free AI PPC optimization across three ad networks with two-click implementation

## Collision as a Muck Rack alternative

AI PR outreach and pitching overlaps Collision's PR and outbound agents; journalist targeting parallels Collision's media outreach for founders.

assessment: Collision bundles PR with content/SEO/ads execution; Muck Rack is PR-vertical only

## Who should choose Muck Rack — and who shouldn't

Choose Muck Rack if: PR/comm teams, startups and agencies matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
