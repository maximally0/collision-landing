# ChatFeatured alternatives

Agentic answer-engine-optimization (AEO) platform: analyzes what LLMs already cite, finds competitive and content gaps, builds the strategy, writes and publishes content, and tracks the resulting lift in citations/traffic across AI search platforms.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to ChatFeatured — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What ChatFeatured does well

Agentic AEO loop (analyze-strategize-write-publish-measure); works backward from sources LLMs cite; competitor gap identification; citation/traffic tracking across major AI search platforms; autonomous agent roadmap (proactively finds opportunities, recommends editorial plays, publishes)

## What ChatFeatured costs

- **Pricing:** Not publicly listed (SaaS subscription)
- **Positioning:** Execution-first AEO (writes and publishes content, not just measurement) with 40%+ MoM growth and customers in five countries within ~6 months of launch
- **Best for:** Marketing teams at SMB/mid-market companies in the US, Canada, Australia, UK, Germany

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where ChatFeatured stops

ChatFeatured ships the autonomous AEO agent Collision positions around, is growing fast, and is already revenue-generating across five countries (assessment)

Capabilities ChatFeatured doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, SEO, Outbound, Advertising.
