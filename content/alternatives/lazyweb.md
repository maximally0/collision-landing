# Lazyweb alternatives

AI Growth PM ('product growth on autopilot'): MCP tool that AI agents tap into to run product growth from 300k+ learnings of top products (founders from Discord, Duolingo, Dropbox, Photomath), plus a managed hands-off growth service. Backed by a16z Speedrun SR007.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Lazyweb — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Lazyweb does well

MCP tool for AI agents; 300k+ learnings from best products; landing grader with applied fixes; growth hypotheses; managed service option; a16z Speedrun SR007

## What Lazyweb costs

- **Pricing:** Not published (SaaS (MCP + managed))
- **Positioning:** Growth knowledge as an MCP layer AI agents can execute — founded by ex-Duolingo/Discord/Dropbox growth leaders (Ali Abouelatta of First 1000), a16z Speedrun-backed
- **Best for:** Founders and product teams doing AI-agent-driven growth; technical founders

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where Lazyweb stops

Its MCP-first design lets any AI agent (Claude, Hermes, Cursor) carry growth playbooks — it could become the knowledge layer inside DIY growth stacks Collision competes with (assessment).

Capabilities Lazyweb doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, AEO, SEO, Outbound.

## The best Lazyweb alternatives

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities Lazyweb doesn't cover.

- **[Tell if AI](/alternatives/tell-if-ai)** — Real-phone fleet makes activity indistinguishable from human creators; revenue share aligns incentives
- **[Mighty & True](/alternatives/mighty-true)** — Autonomous systems run on their own business first (their SEO page is optimized by an agent loop), with named, documente
- **[Mega](/alternatives/mega)** — Services delivered via software: full agency replacement with no prompting, tool management or agency overhead
- **[Brew](/alternatives/brew)** — Agent-native ESP (docs for OpenClaw/Claude/Lovable) with production-grade email rendering and no lock-in
- **[CharacterQuilt](/alternatives/characterquilt)** — Operates your existing stack instead of replacing it — 'sells the plumbing, not the chatbox'
- **[CMO Miko](/alternatives/cmo-miko)** — First 'AGI CMO' positioned explicitly for indie hackers/startups, built by a solo builder

## Collision as a Lazyweb alternative

Overlaps Collision's growth-strategy/execution wedge: an AI agent (or managed service) that runs product-growth experiments and landing optimization for the same founder buyers.

Collision executes the full marketing stack (SEO/AEO/GEO, content, outbound, LinkedIn); Lazyweb is product-growth/landing-experimentation focused and very early (assessment).

## Who should choose Lazyweb — and who shouldn't

Choose Lazyweb if: Founders and product teams doing AI-agent-driven growth; technical founders matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
