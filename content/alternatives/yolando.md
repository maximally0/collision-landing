# Yolando alternatives

Competitive intelligence + Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) platform that monitors brand visibility across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini, surfaces prioritized recommendations and generates on-brand content to close AI-visibility gaps

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Yolando — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Yolando does well

AI Visibility tracking across ChatGPT/Claude/Perplexity/Gemini (ranking, reputation, authority, blind spots); Competitor Radar (new competitor content, pricing moves, visibility gains); prioritized Recommendations engine; Marketing Studio with 40+ agents (research, competitive analysis, fact-checking, formatting); Knowledge Base; proprietary model trained on millions of webpages to identify patterns that drive LLM citation behavior

## What Yolando costs

- **Pricing:** Paid SaaS (demo-based; no public pricing) (Subscription)
- **Positioning:** Combines GEO measurement with competitive intelligence AND on-brand content generation in one platform; built and operated inside BirdseyePost/Birdseye Global, a live marketing business that uses it in production
- **Best for:** B2B SaaS, service businesses (legal, healthcare, financial services) and ecommerce marketing teams

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where Yolando stops

Yolando's competitor-radar plus AI-citation modeling plus content generation is the GEO-plus-intelligence loop Collision competes in, backed by $8.5M from Drive Capital (assessment)

Capabilities Yolando doesn't cover that Collision does: Social media, Automation, SEO, Outbound, Advertising, Email.
