# Nexad alternatives

AI-native marketing company (founded 2024, San Francisco) whose flagship Soku AI is an autonomous marketing agent: connects to a company's ad accounts and website, then runs the growth loop itself - market research, ad creative generation/iteration, campaign launch and optimization across Google Ads, Meta, TikTok and ChatGPT Ads, plus SEO landing pages and AI-search visibility. Backed by a16z Speedrun, Point72 and Prosus ($6M seed, Apr 2025). Launched its AI-native marketing product line Aug 21 2026.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Nexad — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Nexad does well

Self-serve at soku.ai with credit pricing (1,000-credit increments, $3 per 1,000 top-ups); observe-only to full-autopilot autonomy levels; works inside existing ad accounts (client keeps pixels/data/learnings); Chatbot Ad Preview (ads in conversational assistants); Seedance 2.0 creative generation; managed campaign scoping

## What Nexad costs

- **Pricing:** Free to start; credit-based plans (10k-100k credit increments, $3/1,000 top-ups); managed campaigns scoped to budget (Subscription + usage credits)
- **Positioning:** a16z/Point72/Prosus-backed autonomous marketing agent spanning paid (incl. ChatGPT Ads) plus SEO/AI-search visibility, with graduated autonomy and a full audit trail - software that executes inside the ad platforms rather than another dashboard
- **Best for:** Founders, growth teams and agencies running paid ads (Google/Meta/TikTok/ChatGPT Ads)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where Nexad stops

Soku has a16z/Point72/Prosus backing, a live self-serve product and real ad-platform execution incl. ChatGPT Ads - deeper paid-automation infrastructure than Collision offers (assessment)

Capabilities Nexad doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, AEO, SEO, Outbound.

## The best Nexad alternatives

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities Nexad doesn't cover.

- **[Adobe GenStudio](/alternatives/adobe-genstudio)** — Creative-production scale + brand governance + Adobe ecosystem (CDP, AEM, Workfront) — not autonomous strategy
- **[Hyper AI](/alternatives/hyper-ai)** — Prebuilt expert agents (not DIY automations) with deep ad-platform API execution and multi-client agency mode
- **[Jasper](/alternatives/jasper)** — Only major AI marketing platform with native GEO + SEO/AEO + agents + brand governance in one suite
- **[Persado](/alternatives/persado)** — Domain-specific motivation AI plus compliance engine for regulated verticals — clears UDAAP/TILA checks out of the box
- **[Simon AI](/alternatives/simon-ai)** — AI-first composable CDP + agents = enterprise-scale data-driven personalization, not content generation
- **[StoreClaw](/alternatives/storeclaw)** — First 'AI growth engine' purpose-built for store operators with a diagnostics-first agent loop

## Collision as a Nexad alternative

Direct overlap with Collision's full-stack pitch: autonomous marketing execution across paid, SEO landing pages and AI-search visibility for the same founder/growth-team buyer; ChatGPT Ads execution is a channel Collision does not emphasize

Collision is organic-first and channel-agnostic with deeper SEO/AEO/GEO, outbound, LinkedIn/personal branding and strategy; Soku is paid-ads-led with landing pages (assessment)

## Who should choose Nexad — and who shouldn't

Choose Nexad if: Founders, growth teams and agencies running paid ads (Google/Meta/TikTok/ChatGPT Ads) matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
