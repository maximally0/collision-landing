# Lindy alternatives

AI teammate platform: no-code agents that connect to your tools, know your company, and do real work across marketing, support, sales and ops.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Lindy — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Lindy does well

Dedicated marketing use cases (create ads, monitor ad spend, track competitors); 50+ templates; Slack/native UI; email/calendar/CRM actions

## What Lindy costs

- **Pricing:** 7-day free trial; freemium plans (Freemium)
- **Positioning:** Broad 'AI teammate for the whole company' scope with explicit marketing role templates
- **Best for:** SMB-to-enterprise teams including marketing departments

_Pricing verified 2026-08-14._

## Where Lindy stops

Well-funded, easy no-code UX, large template library spanning every function.

Capabilities Lindy doesn't cover that Collision does: Lead generation, GEO, Sales, LinkedIn growth, PR, SEO.

## The best Lindy alternatives

- **[Salesforce Agentforce](/alternatives/salesforce-agentforce)** — Agents natively grounded in the world's largest CRM data estate plus Data Cloud; enterprise trust and distribution
- **[Gumloop](/alternatives/gumloop)** — No-code agent builder for non-developers with enterprise security; Shopify is customer+investor (20M automated actions)
- **[n8n](/alternatives/n8n)** — Developer-loved open-source automation with explosive adoption and SAP strategic investment
- **[Tycoon AI](/alternatives/tycoon-ai)** — Pre-assembled AI company including a CMO, not an agent builder — you chat with a CEO who delegates
- **[11x](/alternatives/11x)** — Named 'digital workers' (Alice/Julian/Jordan) sold as employees replacing roles, not tools augmenting them
- **[Artisan](/alternatives/artisan)** — 'AI employees' positioning with autonomy levels (Level 2 sends outreach unassisted); 'Stop Hiring Humans' brand

## Collision as a Lindy alternative

Ships marketing-specific agents (ads, funnel, competitor tracking, dashboards) — a horizontal agent OS that can already execute pieces of Collision's marketing job.

Collision is outcome-led growth execution; Lindy is a DIY builder requiring setup and oversight. Assessment.

## Who should choose Lindy — and who shouldn't

Choose Lindy if: SMB-to-enterprise teams including marketing departments matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
