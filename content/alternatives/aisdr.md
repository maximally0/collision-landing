# AiSDR alternatives

AI SDR agent that finds prospects in real time, writes personalized email/LinkedIn/text outreach and books qualified meetings that show up.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to AiSDR — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What AiSDR does well

AI prospecting and qualification, personalized emails/LinkedIn/texts, campaign planning, performance monitoring, inbound handling, CRM sync.

## What AiSDR costs

- **Pricing:** From ~$250/mo; flat plans up to ~$900/mo (Subscription)
- **Positioning:** Pure-play AI SDR (YC-backed) with strong booked-meeting performance claims and HubSpot-native workflow.
- **Best for:** B2B SaaS growth teams (50-300 employees, $2M+ ARR)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-14._

## Where AiSDR stops

AiSDR is a proven, funded AI SDR with real booked-meeting results and fast 3-click campaign setup.

Capabilities AiSDR doesn't cover that Collision does: Advertising, Execution, Strategy, AI agents, Lead generation, Personal branding.

## The best AiSDR alternatives

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities AiSDR doesn't cover.

- **[Instantly](/alternatives/instantly)** — Owns cold-email deliverability infrastructure (unlimited accounts + warmup) and now ships AI SDR agents.
- **[Regie.ai](/alternatives/regie-ai)** — World's first AI-native sales engagement platform with patented agentic AI and 300% YoY ARR growth.
- **[Reply.io](/alternatives/reply-io)** — AI SDR agents (e.g., Jason AI) inside a mature multichannel engagement platform - automation-native, not bolted-on.
- **[Smartlead](/alternatives/smartlead)** — Deliverability-first cold email (unlimited mailboxes + AI warmup) that powers most outbound agencies and startups.
- **[11x](/alternatives/11x)** — Named 'digital workers' (Alice/Julian/Jordan) sold as employees replacing roles, not tools augmenting them
- **[Artisan](/alternatives/artisan)** — 'AI employees' positioning with autonomy levels (Level 2 sends outreach unassisted); 'Stop Hiring Humans' brand

## Collision as a AiSDR alternative

Direct overlap with Collision's outbound, SDR and lead-gen capability - same 'AI sales rep' value proposition.

Collision covers the full marketing stack, not just SDR; AiSDR is meeting-booking focused. assessment.

## Who should choose AiSDR — and who shouldn't

Choose AiSDR if: B2B SaaS growth teams (50-300 employees, $2M+ ARR) matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
