# SEO.AI alternatives

AI SEO agent that creates ranking content, publishes it to your site, builds backlinks via exchange network and grows Google/ChatGPT visibility 24/7.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to SEO.AI — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What SEO.AI does well

24/7 autopilot, fresh pages published weekly, automatic backlink exchange network, Google + ChatGPT citation focus, 10-minute install.

## What SEO.AI costs

- **Pricing:** Not publicly listed (Freemium)
- **Positioning:** Automated link building via proprietary exchange network plus hands-off publishing.
- **Best for:** Business owners/SMBs who don't want to think about SEO; agencies

_Pricing verified 2026-08-13._

## Where SEO.AI stops

SEO.AI adds automated backlink building and a 'set once, runs forever' simplicity Collision may not match.

Capabilities SEO.AI doesn't cover that Collision does: LinkedIn growth, Analytics, Personal branding, Automation, Outbound, Lead generation.

## The best SEO.AI alternatives

- **[Outrank](/alternatives/outrank)** — Full SEO loop (research-write-publish) as one autopilot agent.
- **[Surfer (Positive)](/alternatives/surfer-positive)** — Mature SEO data engine rebranded around AI-search visibility across engines.
- **[AdCreative.ai](/alternatives/adcreative-ai)** — Predictive conversion scoring of creatives before launch, trained on performance data
- **[Adzooma](/alternatives/adzooma)** — Free AI PPC optimization across three ad networks with two-click implementation
- **[AiSDR](/alternatives/aisdr)** — Pure-play AI SDR (YC-backed) with strong booked-meeting performance claims and HubSpot-native workflow.
- **[Apollo.io](/alternatives/apollo-io)** — Free-tier data + engagement + AI in one stack; 600K+ companies and a replace-five-tools pitch.

## Collision as a SEO.AI alternative

Full overlap with Collision's autonomous SEO capability: audit, content, publish and links.

Collision covers more of the growth stack (outbound, social, strategy); SEO.AI is SEO-only. assessment.

## Who should choose SEO.AI — and who shouldn't

Choose SEO.AI if: Business owners/SMBs who don't want to think about SEO; agencies matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
