# MAI alternatives

AI platform that puts performance marketing on autopilot: autonomous Google Ads agents (create, monitor, optimize 24/7) with ROAS guarantees, natively integrated with business data. Emerged from stealth Sep 2025.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to MAI — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What MAI does well

Autonomous campaign setup/monitoring/optimization; real-time budget, bid and creative tuning; plain-language explanation of every action; issue detection (broken discount codes, stockouts); handles thousands of products/creative variants; ROAS guarantee

## What MAI costs

- **Pricing:** Not publicly listed (SaaS subscription)
- **Positioning:** ROAS-guaranteed autonomous ad agents with deep platform-side DNA (founders ex-Google/Instacart), already managing millions in monthly ad spend for brands (Dreo, DrWoof, Patpat, Vivaia)
- **Best for:** Ecommerce and DTC brands, SMB to mid-market, lean teams wanting enterprise-level paid performance

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where MAI stops

MAI's autonomous ad optimization with a ROAS guarantee and Kleiner Perkins backing is deeper paid-media execution than anything Collision currently ships, and its stated roadmap expands into the rest of marketing (assessment)

Capabilities MAI doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, AEO, SEO, Outbound.

## The best MAI alternatives

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities MAI doesn't cover.

- **[AdCreative.ai](/alternatives/adcreative-ai)** — Predictive conversion scoring of creatives before launch, trained on performance data
- **[Adzooma](/alternatives/adzooma)** — Free AI PPC optimization across three ad networks with two-click implementation
- **[Madgicx](/alternatives/madgicx)** — First AI ad agent for Meta plus exclusive AI Bidding via Meta partnership
- **[Smartly.io](/alternatives/smartly-io)** — Only platform managing creative and media end-to-end for 700+ global brands at 300B creatives/year
- **[InstaAgent](/alternatives/instaagent)** — Hundreds of distinct audiences with distinct content from one brief — the anti-generic ad engine
- **[Sprites](/alternatives/sprites)** — Cross-channel ad execution (not just content) with ROAS/CPL goal optimization plus an SEO/AEO content engine - one agent

## Collision as a MAI alternative

Overlaps Collision's paid-ads execution and marketing-automation capability: autonomous agents that run and optimize campaigns end-to-end for the same lean-team buyer; KP explicitly frames MAI's roadmap extending into asset creation, channel selection, SEO and social

Collision covers the full organic stack (SEO/AEO/GEO, content, social, outbound, personal branding) and strategy; MAI is ads-only execution on Google today (assessment)

## Who should choose MAI — and who shouldn't

Choose MAI if: Ecommerce and DTC brands, SMB to mid-market, lean teams wanting enterprise-level paid performance matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
