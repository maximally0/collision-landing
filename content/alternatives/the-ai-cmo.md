The AI CMO, by ROGA AI Ltd (HQ: United Kingdom), is an agentic marketing operating system for founders, solo marketers, SMBs and agencies. It plans, produces and sends campaigns across email, ads, SMS and push — with an approval queue, a data warehouse and built-in SEO and link-building. Pricing starts at $299/month, and the full platform is on every plan. If you're evaluating it, you want the same thing we built Collision for: a system that runs your marketing function instead of handing you a pile of drafts.

## What The AI CMO does well

Honestly, this is one of the most complete "marketing OS" pitches in the AI CMO wave — our competitor database flags it as the broadest agentic scope of the cohort:

- **Real send-path infrastructure.** The AI CMO ships a customer 360 with a self-filling data warehouse, an append-only audit record and structural suppression. That's the difference between a content wrapper and something an operations person can trust with a live send.
- **A true approval queue.** Nothing ships without your sign-off. For a company sending email, ads, SMS and push, that is the correct default.
- **Campaign breadth.** Email, ads, SMS and push, plus behavior journeys and SEO and link-building — with 600+ connectors and throughput of 10,000+ campaigns per month.
- **Accessible pricing.** From $299/month with the full platform on every plan — no enterprise paywall on the core product.
- **Governance as a differentiator.** An append-only ledger plus structural suppression is enterprise-grade trust, built bootstrapped out of the UK.

## Where The AI CMO stops — and where Collision differs

The AI CMO is campaign-execution heavy (assessment). Its center of gravity is planning, producing and sending campaigns to audiences you already know — a real, hard problem, and it solves it well. Where it's quieter: founder-centric outbound, lead generation, SDR motion, personal branding and LinkedIn growth. That's exactly the half of growth that founders say eats their week.

Collision is built for that gap:

- **Outbound, email and lead gen are core, not adjacent.** Collision researches prospects, writes the outreach, runs the sequences and learns what converts — same intelligence, same memory.
- **One memory across every surface.** The strategy conversation from Tuesday shapes the LinkedIn posts on Friday, the SEO briefs and the outbound angle. The AI CMO's data warehouse is a record of sends; Collision's memory is a source of truth that compounds.
- **Approval gates on every action**, not just campaign sends — posts, outreach, publishes, all of it.
- **SEO/AEO/GEO depth plus personal branding** as a founder growth system, not a campaign channel.
