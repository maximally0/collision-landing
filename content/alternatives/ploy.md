# Ploy alternatives

AI marketing platform that turns a company's website into its growth engine: swarms of marketing and web-design agents design pages, write copy, launch campaigns (ABM, SEO, paid ads) and optimize, with built-in AEO. YC P26; $27M seed (Jun 2026). Founded by ex-Webflow co-founder/CTO Bryant Chou.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Ploy — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Ploy does well

Site slurp (design system/components/brand content); PloyBooks strategy templates; ABM page generation; programmatic SEO engines; AEO; visitor identification/enrichment; ad integrations; custom domains; GitHub code sync; free tier (800 credits/day)

## What Ploy costs

- **Pricing:** Free; Starter $50/mo; Pro/Enterprise (usage credits) (Freemium subscription (credits))
- **Positioning:** Founder pedigree (ex-Webflow CTO) + agent swarms operating on the website itself with AEO built in; $27M seed before YC demo day
- **Best for:** Founders and startups (13%+ of YC P26 batch), SaaS teams (Hex, Clay), small teams

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where Ploy stops

Ploy has $27M seed, an ex-Webflow CTO and deep YC-batch adoption - better resourced and faster-scaling than most Collision competitors in the founder wedge (assessment).

Capabilities Ploy doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, Outbound, Advertising, Email.

## The best Ploy alternatives

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities Ploy doesn't cover.

- **[Outrank](/alternatives/outrank)** — Full SEO loop (research-write-publish) as one autopilot agent.
- **[SEO.AI](/alternatives/seo-ai)** — Automated link building via proprietary exchange network plus hands-off publishing.
- **[Surfer (Positive)](/alternatives/surfer-positive)** — Mature SEO data engine rebranded around AI-search visibility across engines.
- **[Semrush](/alternatives/semrush)** — Deepest SEO data estate plus native GEO tooling, now inside Adobe's marketing stack — data and distribution no standalon
- **[Ahrefs Letaido](/alternatives/ahrefs-letaido)** — Only agent with unrestricted internal Ahrefs endpoint access (data beyond the public API/MCP, no API-unit cost) plus pur
- **[AutoBacklinks](/alternatives/autobacklinks)** — First dedicated AI agent for the full link-building loop (vs link marketplaces or agency services)

## Collision as a Ploy alternative

Overlaps Collision's SEO, AEO, content and campaign execution for founders - agents that proactively run website growth compete with Collision's growth strategy + execution wedge.

Collision spans the full funnel beyond the website (outbound, LinkedIn, social distribution, lead gen, personal branding); Ploy is website-centric (assessment).

## Who should choose Ploy — and who shouldn't

Choose Ploy if: Founders and startups (13%+ of YC P26 batch), SaaS teams (Hex, Clay), small teams matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
