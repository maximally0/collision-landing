# Mega alternatives

AI-powered growth engine that replaces marketing agencies for SMBs: a network of AI agents executes SEO, GEO, paid ads and website management end-to-end, continuously optimizing and reporting. ~$10M revenue in 10 months; $11.5M Series A (Mar 2026).

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Mega — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Mega does well

Zero-login operation; content maps + creation; real-time ad optimization; GEO; continuous plan-execute-optimize-report loop; enterprise-grade growth without agency overhead

## What Mega costs

- **Pricing:** From $699/mo (SEO), $1,399/mo (ads), $2,099/mo all-in-one (per Tam Radar) (Subscription)
- **Positioning:** Services delivered via software: full agency replacement with no prompting, tool management or agency overhead
- **Best for:** SMBs ($500K-$20M revenue) that would otherwise hire an agency

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where Mega stops

Mega has Goodwater/a16z backing, ~$10M revenue in 10 months and a proven zero-login 'never manages it' promise that out-executes Collision on hands-off SMB growth (assessment)

Capabilities Mega doesn't cover that Collision does: Social media, Automation, AEO, Outbound, Advertising, Email.

## The best Mega alternatives

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities Mega doesn't cover.

- **[Tell if AI](/alternatives/tell-if-ai)** — Real-phone fleet makes activity indistinguishable from human creators; revenue share aligns incentives
- **[Mighty & True](/alternatives/mighty-true)** — Autonomous systems run on their own business first (their SEO page is optimized by an agent loop), with named, documente
- **[Lazyweb](/alternatives/lazyweb)** — Growth knowledge as an MCP layer AI agents can execute — founded by ex-Duolingo/Discord/Dropbox growth leaders (Ali Abou
- **[Adobe GenStudio](/alternatives/adobe-genstudio)** — Creative-production scale + brand governance + Adobe ecosystem (CDP, AEM, Workfront) — not autonomous strategy
- **[AI CMO (tryaicmo.com)](/alternatives/ai-cmo-tryaicmo-com)** — Combines social listening with AI content distribution at SMB prices ($99 vs $79+/tool for Brand24 alone)
- **[Copy.ai](/alternatives/copy-ai)** — Autonomous GTM workflows spanning sales + marketing (not just copy) with a massive freemium user base

## Collision as a Mega alternative

Direct overlap with Collision's core: autonomous end-to-end execution of SEO, GEO, paid ads and website growth for SMBs — the same 'AI growth team in a box' value prop and ICP; roadmap explicitly adds email, outbound, organic social and lead qualification

Collision adds personal branding, LinkedIn growth, multi-platform outbound depth and founder strategy; Mega is SEO/ads/website-centric today (assessment)

## Who should choose Mega — and who shouldn't

Choose Mega if: SMBs ($500K-$20M revenue) that would otherwise hire an agency matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
