# Manus alternatives

General autonomous AI agent ('hands-on AI'): browses, researches, builds websites/slides, writes reports and executes multi-step tasks.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Manus — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Manus does well

Wide Research; browser operator; AI design/slides/music; Mail Manus; Slack integration; team plans; API; scheduled tasks

## What Manus costs

- **Pricing:** $19-$199/mo plans with credits (8k-40k credits) (Subscription)
- **Positioning:** Most-hyped general agent (viral Mar 2025 launch); produces finished deliverables like websites and research reports
- **Best for:** Individuals & teams needing general task automation

_Pricing verified 2026-08-13._

## Where Manus stops

Huge brand awareness, deep pockets, general-task autonomy improving quickly.

Capabilities Manus doesn't cover that Collision does: Content, LinkedIn growth, Analytics, Personal branding, Automation, Outbound.

## The best Manus alternatives

- **[Decagon](/alternatives/decagon)** — Category leader in AI support agents; $4.5B valuation; disciplined 'agent engineering' approach
- **[Sierra](/alternatives/sierra)** — Founder pedigree (ex-Salesforce co-CEO Bret Taylor), $4.5B valuation, enterprise CX focus
- **[11x](/alternatives/11x)** — Named 'digital workers' (Alice/Julian/Jordan) sold as employees replacing roles, not tools augmenting them
- **[Artisan](/alternatives/artisan)** — 'AI employees' positioning with autonomy levels (Level 2 sends outreach unassisted); 'Stop Hiring Humans' brand
- **[Dygentic](/alternatives/dygentic)** — Agency-grade autonomy on open tooling (n8n) with full handover — clients own the system
- **[Gumloop](/alternatives/gumloop)** — No-code agent builder for non-developers with enterprise security; Shopify is customer+investor (20M automated actions)

## Collision as a Manus alternative

A general agent that builds websites and runs research/content tasks could handle marketing execution; ownership chaos (blocked Meta deal) slows this path.

Collision is purpose-built for growth workflows with platform knowledge; Manus is generic and inconsistent. Assessment.

## Who should choose Manus — and who shouldn't

Choose Manus if: Individuals & teams needing general task automation matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
