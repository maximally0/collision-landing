# JustAI alternatives

AI-native marketing platform (YC Winter 2024) helping enterprises automate personalization, experimentation and decisioning at scale with four always-on agents (strategy, creative, decisioning, data); powered by reinforcement learning and agentic infrastructure.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to JustAI — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What JustAI does well

Strategy agent (segments, behavior, touchpoints); creative agent (email/in-app content); decisioning agent (campaign optimization within business guardrails); data agent (measurement + feedback); RL + agentic infrastructure; 600+ AI-delegated marketing decisions/mo; $100M+ customer revenue generated

## What JustAI costs

- **Pricing:** Not publicly listed (Enterprise SaaS)
- **Positioning:** Reinforcement-learning-powered agentic decisioning (not just content generation); YC W24 + $17M Base10-led Series A; 5X ARR growth; operator investors from Anthropic, Chime, HubSpot CTO, Eppo and Vapi founders
- **Best for:** Enterprise marketing teams (consumer, D2C, e-commerce, B2B)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where JustAI stops

JustAI's RL-driven decisioning at enterprise scale, $17M funding and operator network make it a credible enterprise-ward expansion threat (assessment)

Capabilities JustAI doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, AEO, SEO, Outbound.

## The best JustAI alternatives

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities JustAI doesn't cover.

- **[Adobe GenStudio](/alternatives/adobe-genstudio)** — Creative-production scale + brand governance + Adobe ecosystem (CDP, AEM, Workfront) — not autonomous strategy
- **[Hyper AI](/alternatives/hyper-ai)** — Prebuilt expert agents (not DIY automations) with deep ad-platform API execution and multi-client agency mode
- **[Jasper](/alternatives/jasper)** — Only major AI marketing platform with native GEO + SEO/AEO + agents + brand governance in one suite
- **[Persado](/alternatives/persado)** — Domain-specific motivation AI plus compliance engine for regulated verticals — clears UDAAP/TILA checks out of the box
- **[Simon AI](/alternatives/simon-ai)** — AI-first composable CDP + agents = enterprise-scale data-driven personalization, not content generation
- **[StoreClaw](/alternatives/storeclaw)** — First 'AI growth engine' purpose-built for store operators with a diagnostics-first agent loop

## Collision as a JustAI alternative

Overlaps Collision's marketing automation and execution capability: autonomous agents that plan, create and optimize campaigns — the 'AI runs marketing' value prop aimed at enterprise teams rather than founders

Collision serves founders/startups with organic growth (SEO/AEO/GEO, outbound, LinkedIn) and strategy; JustAI is enterprise personalization/experimentation on owned channels (assessment)

## Who should choose JustAI — and who shouldn't

Choose JustAI if: Enterprise marketing teams (consumer, D2C, e-commerce, B2B) matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
