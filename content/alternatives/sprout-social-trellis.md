# Sprout Social (Trellis) alternatives

AI-powered social intelligence platform (NASDAQ: SPT) with Trellis, a proprietary agentic AI layer now generally available across every plan - spans Listening, Publishing, Smart Inbox and Reporting; Trellis Studio lets teams build custom AI skill templates that turn recurring work into automated workflows.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Sprout Social (Trellis) — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Sprout Social (Trellis) does well

Trellis GA to every customer on every plan (Aug 2026); Trellis Studio (custom AI skill templates, automated recurring workflows); JetBlue/Ipsy evidence (reporting from hours to minutes); brand-risk detection before it spreads; cross-team routing of social feedback; sentiment/theme analysis

## What Sprout Social (Trellis) costs

- **Pricing:** Enterprise (custom, demo-based) (Subscription)
- **Positioning:** Only social-management incumbent with a single agentic layer spanning the whole platform plus user-built skills (Trellis Studio) - agentic social intelligence at enterprise scale
- **Best for:** Enterprise and mid-market social/marketing teams (JetBlue, Ipsy; 30,000+ brands)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where Sprout Social (Trellis) stops

Sprout's data scale (billions of data points), enterprise logos and public-company resources far exceed Collision's social tooling; Trellis Studio shows user-built agentic workflows (assessment)

Capabilities Sprout Social (Trellis) doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, AEO, SEO, Outbound.

## The best Sprout Social (Trellis) alternatives

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities Sprout Social (Trellis) doesn't cover.

- **[Buffer](/alternatives/buffer)** — Per-channel pricing and a genuinely free AI tier make it the default low-cost AI social tool for startups.
- **[FeedHive](/alternatives/feedhive)** — Workflow/condition-based automation plus AI agents inside a social scheduler.
- **[Hootsuite](/alternatives/hootsuite)** — OwlyGPT trained on real-time social trends plus 15+ years of social expertise and enterprise compliance tooling.
- **[Lately](/alternatives/lately)** — Statistical AI trained on a customer's own best posts predicts engaging words/ideas, not generic LLM copy.
- **[Postwise](/alternatives/postwise)** — Engagement-engineered AI trained on proven posts plus revenue tools (auto-DM, link plugging) inside a social scheduler.
- **[Predis.ai](/alternatives/predis-ai)** — Mass scale (6.4M users, 200M+ ads generated) and strong AI video-ad/UGC generation.

## Collision as a Sprout Social (Trellis) alternative

Overlaps Collision's social/content/distribution capability: agentic social intelligence and reporting for enterprise teams; the comparison-table incumbent in the AI social lane alongside Hootsuite

Collision is founder-priced, channel-agnostic growth execution (SEO/AEO/GEO, outbound, LinkedIn); Sprout is enterprise social intelligence/publishing (assessment)

## Who should choose Sprout Social (Trellis) — and who shouldn't

Choose Sprout Social (Trellis) if: Enterprise and mid-market social/marketing teams (JetBlue, Ipsy; 30,000+ brands) matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
