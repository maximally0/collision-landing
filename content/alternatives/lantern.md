# Lantern alternatives

E-commerce GEO platform (pivoted from Shopify loyalty in 2026): projects a brand's AI-shopping visibility forward and simulates how on-site and off-site changes move it, so ecommerce brands become the answer ChatGPT recommends.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Lantern — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Lantern does well

Predicted Visibility forecast; change simulation before committing; agentic-shopping focus (ACP/UCP commerce protocols); AI-recommendation tracking

## What Lantern costs

- **Pricing:** Undisclosed (SaaS subscription)
- **Positioning:** Forward-looking visibility forecasting with change simulation, purpose-built for e-commerce and agentic shopping - vs. backward-looking measurement tools.
- **Best for:** E-commerce / DTC brands (Shopify ecosystem)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where Lantern stops

E-commerce is where agentic-shopping dollars concentrate fastest (Walmart agent-led shopping, ACP/UCP protocols, Costco AI carousels) - Lantern owns the e-commerce GEO niche early (assessment).

Capabilities Lantern doesn't cover that Collision does: Social media, Automation, AEO, SEO, Outbound, Advertising.

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities Lantern doesn't cover.

## Collision as a Lantern alternative

Overlaps with Collision's GEO/AEO capability in the e-commerce segment - "be the brand AI recommends" is the same outcome Collision sells to founders.

Collision covers B2B/founder growth across channels beyond e-commerce AI-shopping visibility; Lantern is a single-vertical GEO tool (assessment).

## Who should choose Lantern — and who shouldn't

Choose Lantern if: E-commerce / DTC brands (Shopify ecosystem) matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
