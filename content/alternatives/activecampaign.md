# ActiveCampaign alternatives

Incumbent marketing automation platform (est. 2003) repositioned as an autonomous marketing platform: Active Intelligence AI agents plan, create, launch and optimize email, SMS and WhatsApp campaigns end-to-end

This page compares the realistic alternatives to ActiveCampaign — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What ActiveCampaign does well

Autonomous Marketing platform positioning; Wavelength (marketing AI tuned to each business's data, launched Aug 27 2026); results guarantee on autonomous marketing; Google Ads connector for AI-guided campaign creation (Jul 8 2026); first autonomous marketing connector for Anthropic Claude (Nov 2025); MCP server; Feedback Intelligence AI-evaluation acquisition (Feb 3 2026); billions of behavioral data points processed daily

## What ActiveCampaign costs

- **Pricing:** Tiered subscription (Plus/Pro/Enterprise) (Subscription)
- **Positioning:** Incumbent automation vendor with a large SMB install base, a results guarantee and billions of behavioral data points - autonomous marketing layered on an existing customer database rather than greenfield
- **Best for:** SMB and mid-market marketing teams (180K+ customer base)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where ActiveCampaign stops

ActiveCampaign's installed base, behavioral data scale and results guarantee make it the default autonomous-marketing choice for its existing customers - the HubSpot-Breeze pattern in the email lane (assessment)

Capabilities ActiveCampaign doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, AEO, SEO, Outbound.

## The best ActiveCampaign alternatives

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities ActiveCampaign doesn't cover.

- **[HubSpot Breeze](/alternatives/hubspot-breeze)** — Agents run natively on existing CRM data and workflows, distributed to HubSpot's large SMB install base
- **[Dygentic](/alternatives/dygentic)** — Agency-grade autonomy on open tooling (n8n) with full handover — clients own the system
- **[Make](/alternatives/make)** — Visual-first UX plus Celonis enterprise muscle; strong marketing-team adoption
- **[Zapier (Agents)](/alternatives/zapier-agents)** — Largest integration ecosystem plus massive SMB installed base entering agentic automation
- **[Brew](/alternatives/brew)** — Agent-native ESP (docs for OpenClaw/Claude/Lovable) with production-grade email rendering and no lock-in
- **[CharacterQuilt](/alternatives/characterquilt)** — Operates your existing stack instead of replacing it — 'sells the plumbing, not the chatbox'

## Collision as a ActiveCampaign alternative

Overlaps Collision's marketing automation/execution, content creation and campaign management for email/SMS/WhatsApp channels, with the same 'AI agents run your marketing' narrative

Collision spans SEO/GEO/AEO, outbound, LinkedIn, personal branding and strategy; ActiveCampaign is channel-bound to email/SMS/WhatsApp lifecycle marketing (assessment)

## Who should choose ActiveCampaign — and who shouldn't

Choose ActiveCampaign if: SMB and mid-market marketing teams (180K+ customer base) matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
