# Tec-Do alternatives

Chinese AI marketing technology company (Beijing, est. 2017): Navos Marketing Multi-Agent Platform driven by self-developed Tec-Chi marketing MLLMs, delivering end-to-end AI-native marketing (market intelligence, content generation, campaign delivery, performance optimization) for enterprises going global. Served 100,000+ advertisers in 2025. New financing round led by Huatai-General Atlantic (Aug 2026).

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Tec-Do — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Tec-Do does well

100,000+ advertisers served (2025); multi-agent marketing workflows; specialized marketing MLLMs (text-to-video, content deconstruction); strategic investment in agentic commerce, Agent-to-Agent collaboration and GEO

## What Tec-Do costs

- **Pricing:** Enterprise (not published) (Enterprise contracts)
- **Positioning:** China-scale marketing-specific MLLMs + multi-agent platform with global expansion capital (Huatai-General Atlantic's first RMB investment)
- **Best for:** Enterprise advertisers going global (ecommerce, gaming, entertainment, local commerce)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where Tec-Do stops

Tec-Do has 100k+ advertisers, proprietary marketing LLMs and new Huatai-General Atlantic capital — infrastructure scale Collision cannot match (assessment)

Capabilities Tec-Do doesn't cover that Collision does: Social media, Automation, AEO, SEO, Outbound, Advertising.

## The best Tec-Do alternatives

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities Tec-Do doesn't cover.

- **[Adobe GenStudio](/alternatives/adobe-genstudio)** — Creative-production scale + brand governance + Adobe ecosystem (CDP, AEM, Workfront) — not autonomous strategy
- **[Hyper AI](/alternatives/hyper-ai)** — Prebuilt expert agents (not DIY automations) with deep ad-platform API execution and multi-client agency mode
- **[Jasper](/alternatives/jasper)** — Only major AI marketing platform with native GEO + SEO/AEO + agents + brand governance in one suite
- **[Persado](/alternatives/persado)** — Domain-specific motivation AI plus compliance engine for regulated verticals — clears UDAAP/TILA checks out of the box
- **[Simon AI](/alternatives/simon-ai)** — AI-first composable CDP + agents = enterprise-scale data-driven personalization, not content generation
- **[StoreClaw](/alternatives/storeclaw)** — First 'AI growth engine' purpose-built for store operators with a diagnostics-first agent loop

## Collision as a Tec-Do alternative

Overlaps Collision's AI marketing automation/execution category at enterprise scale; its stated investment in GEO and agentic commerce targets the same AI-search lanes Collision plays

Collision is founder-priced, self-serve and Western-market growth with SEO/AEO/GEO depth; Tec-Do is enterprise ad-tech for Chinese brands going global (assessment)

## Who should choose Tec-Do — and who shouldn't

Choose Tec-Do if: Enterprise advertisers going global (ecommerce, gaming, entertainment, local commerce) matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
