# PeopleLinx alternatives

Revenue orchestration platform (LinkedIn social-selling heritage): data, AI-powered outreach automation and managed go-to-market execution for national brands with distributed teams. Completed its first acquisition (Aug 2026): SalesGeneration.ai's outbound-email business, plus a partnership giving clients access to SalesGeneration.ai's personalized video technology.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to PeopleLinx — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What PeopleLinx does well

2-4x reply-rate claims; 30-60 days to qualified conversations; AI-powered outreach automation; data + human research; premium BI sources (ZoomInfo); managed execution; personalized video outreach via SalesGeneration.ai partnership

## What PeopleLinx costs

- **Pricing:** Undisclosed (platform + services) (Subscription + managed services)
- **Positioning:** Revenue orchestration for local-first/distributed national brands combining AI automation with managed human GTM execution, now adding personalized-video outreach at scale
- **Best for:** National brands, franchise/local-first and relationship-driven industries (1,000+ teams)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where PeopleLinx stops

PeopleLinx has 1,000+ teams, a managed-execution layer and now an M&A track record in the outbound lane - deeper operational delivery than Collision offers (assessment)

Capabilities PeopleLinx doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, AEO, SEO, Advertising.

## The best PeopleLinx alternatives

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities PeopleLinx doesn't cover.

- **[AiSDR](/alternatives/aisdr)** — Pure-play AI SDR (YC-backed) with strong booked-meeting performance claims and HubSpot-native workflow.
- **[Instantly](/alternatives/instantly)** — Owns cold-email deliverability infrastructure (unlimited accounts + warmup) and now ships AI SDR agents.
- **[Regie.ai](/alternatives/regie-ai)** — World's first AI-native sales engagement platform with patented agentic AI and 300% YoY ARR growth.
- **[Reply.io](/alternatives/reply-io)** — AI SDR agents (e.g., Jason AI) inside a mature multichannel engagement platform - automation-native, not bolted-on.
- **[Smartlead](/alternatives/smartlead)** — Deliverability-first cold email (unlimited mailboxes + AI warmup) that powers most outbound agencies and startups.
- **[11x](/alternatives/11x)** — Named 'digital workers' (Alice/Julian/Jordan) sold as employees replacing roles, not tools augmenting them

## Collision as a PeopleLinx alternative

Overlaps Collision outbound and lead-generation capabilities: AI-powered personalized outreach automation and managed execution for the same B2B pipeline outcomes, aimed at mid-market/enterprise rather than founders

Collision is self-serve, founder-priced and channel-agnostic growth (SEO/AEO/GEO, LinkedIn, content); PeopleLinx is an enterprise platform-plus-services engagement for distributed sales teams (assessment)

## Who should choose PeopleLinx — and who shouldn't

Choose PeopleLinx if: National brands, franchise/local-first and relationship-driven industries (1,000+ teams) matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
