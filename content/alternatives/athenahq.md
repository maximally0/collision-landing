# AthenaHQ alternatives

Enterprise AI-search visibility platform ("Agents to Win on AI Search"): monitors what AI engines say about a brand, explains why, and turns insights into governed action across content, PR, commerce and optimization. 300+ global brands incl. Coinbase, SoFi, ZoomInfo, PagerDuty, Hearst.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to AthenaHQ — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What AthenaHQ does well

AI-search monitoring across engines; answer-shaping analysis; governed action workflows; agent-based execution; enterprise integrations; 300+ brand logos

## What AthenaHQ costs

- **Pricing:** Contact sales (Subscription)
- **Positioning:** Enterprise-grade AI-search action platform built by ex-Google Search/DeepMind and ex-ServiceNow leadership; 300+ brands and an agency channel (R/GA, Slalom, Ignite Visibility)
- **Best for:** Enterprise marketing/PR/content teams and agencies (Coinbase, SoFi, ZoomInfo, Hearst, R/GA, Slalom, Ignite Visibility)
