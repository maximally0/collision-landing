# Lately alternatives

AI content assistant that learns a brand's voice, auto-generates social posts from long-form assets and pre-vets messaging with audiences.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Lately — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Lately does well

Voice-trained AI (Lately DNA); long-form to social repurposing; engagement prediction; autopilot publishing; employee advocacy; Hootsuite/Canva integrations

## What Lately costs

- **Pricing:** Not publicly listed (subscription)
- **Positioning:** Statistical AI trained on a customer's own best posts predicts engaging words/ideas, not generic LLM copy.
- **Best for:** Marketing teams, brands and agencies automating social content

_Pricing verified 2026-08-14._

## Where Lately stops

Lately is enterprise-branded, expensive and aimed at marketing departments, not founder-led growth - weaker fit for Collision's core buyer.

Capabilities Lately doesn't cover that Collision does: Lead generation, GEO, Sales, LinkedIn growth, PR, SEO.

## The best Lately alternatives

- **[Buffer](/alternatives/buffer)** — Per-channel pricing and a genuinely free AI tier make it the default low-cost AI social tool for startups.
- **[FeedHive](/alternatives/feedhive)** — Workflow/condition-based automation plus AI agents inside a social scheduler.
- **[Hootsuite](/alternatives/hootsuite)** — OwlyGPT trained on real-time social trends plus 15+ years of social expertise and enterprise compliance tooling.
- **[Postwise](/alternatives/postwise)** — Engagement-engineered AI trained on proven posts plus revenue tools (auto-DM, link plugging) inside a social scheduler.
- **[Predis.ai](/alternatives/predis-ai)** — Mass scale (6.4M users, 200M+ ads generated) and strong AI video-ad/UGC generation.
- **[Typefully](/alternatives/typefully)** — Creator-first writing experience with native support for emerging networks (Bluesky, Threads) and 10,000+ customers.

## Collision as a Lately alternative

Competes on AI content creation and distribution: auto-generating and publishing social content from brand assets overlaps Collision's content engine.

assessment - Collision adds strategy, SEO/AEO and outbound lead gen; Lately focuses on social repurposing/autopilot for marketing teams

## Who should choose Lately — and who shouldn't

Choose Lately if: Marketing teams, brands and agencies automating social content matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
