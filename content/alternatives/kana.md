# Kana alternatives

Agentic marketing platform from Rapt/Krux founders: flexible AI agents for data analysis, audience targeting, campaign orchestration, customer engagement, media planning and AI-chatbot (LLM) optimization. $15M seed led by Mayfield (Feb 2026).

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Kana — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Kana does well

10 solution areas incl. LLM Command Center for AI-search/AI-chatbot visibility; Kana for Marketers + Kana for Publishers suites; agentic data platform; synthetic data generation; published research: 70% of enterprises already run AI agents in marketing (Jul 2026)

## What Kana costs

- **Pricing:** Enterprise (contact) (Enterprise SaaS)
- **Positioning:** Founders with 26 years of martech pedigree (Rapt->Microsoft, Krux->Salesforce $1B, super{set}->Habu/LiveRamp $200M exit); enterprise-grade agentic platform incl. LLM Command Center for AI-search presence
- **Best for:** Enterprise marketing teams and publishers

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where Kana stops

Kana's founder pedigree ($1B Krux acquisition), $15M Mayfield backing and solution breadth give it enterprise credibility and an AEO position Collision must match (assessment)

Capabilities Kana doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, AEO, SEO, Outbound.

## The best Kana alternatives

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities Kana doesn't cover.

- **[Adobe GenStudio](/alternatives/adobe-genstudio)** — Creative-production scale + brand governance + Adobe ecosystem (CDP, AEM, Workfront) — not autonomous strategy
- **[Hyper AI](/alternatives/hyper-ai)** — Prebuilt expert agents (not DIY automations) with deep ad-platform API execution and multi-client agency mode
- **[Jasper](/alternatives/jasper)** — Only major AI marketing platform with native GEO + SEO/AEO + agents + brand governance in one suite
- **[Persado](/alternatives/persado)** — Domain-specific motivation AI plus compliance engine for regulated verticals — clears UDAAP/TILA checks out of the box
- **[Simon AI](/alternatives/simon-ai)** — AI-first composable CDP + agents = enterprise-scale data-driven personalization, not content generation
- **[StoreClaw](/alternatives/storeclaw)** — First 'AI growth engine' purpose-built for store operators with a diagnostics-first agent loop

## Collision as a Kana alternative

Overlaps Collision on multi-function agentic marketing execution and on AEO/GEO via LLM Command Center (AI-chatbot optimization) — the enterprise-ward version of Collision’s autonomous marketing stack

Collision is founder-priced, self-serve and execution-first across SEO/AEO/GEO/outbound/LinkedIn; Kana is enterprise data/platform-centric with a services-leaning motion (assessment)

## Who should choose Kana — and who shouldn't

Choose Kana if: Enterprise marketing teams and publishers matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
