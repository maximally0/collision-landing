# ASO Agent alternatives

AI-powered platform for app publishers covering App Store Optimization (ASO), Apple Ads and AI visibility: tracks whether apps are mentioned, cited or recommended by ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overview, Claude and Perplexity at the individual-prompt level, with analysis of the sources (websites, PR, Reddit, LinkedIn, YouTube) shaping LLM answers; automates ASO metadata generation across 40+ locales and 155 markets.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to ASO Agent — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What ASO Agent does well

LLM AI Visibility dashboard; automated ASO metadata generation (titles, subtitles, keyword fields, descriptions); keyword/ranking/volume/difficulty monitoring across App Store + Google Play; competitor analysis; visual/creative analysis; 40+ locales, 155 markets; App Store Connect + Google Play Console integration; prioritized action roadmaps with attribution

## What ASO Agent costs

- **Pricing:** Contact sales (SaaS subscription (contact))
- **Positioning:** Founder ex-Uber ASO (130+ languages, ~500 metadata revisions); first platform combining ASO with LLM AI-visibility purpose-built for the app vertical
- **Best for:** App publishers - from single indie apps to portfolios of hundreds

## Where ASO Agent stops

Purpose-built app-store + AI-visibility depth (metadata automation, 155 markets, prompt-level source analysis) that Collision does not offer for apps (assessment)

Capabilities ASO Agent doesn't cover that Collision does: Social media, Automation, AEO, SEO, Outbound, Advertising.
