# Postwise alternatives

AI tool to write, schedule and grow on X, LinkedIn and Threads; generates viral-engineered posts and offers growth/DM automations.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Postwise — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Postwise does well

AI viral post & thread generation; one topic to many posts; multi-platform scheduling; GrowthTools follower suite; GhostWriter autopost; auto-send DMs; repost winning content

## What Postwise costs

- **Pricing:** 7-day free trial; Basic $37/mo; Unlimited $97/mo (annual) (subscription)
- **Positioning:** Engagement-engineered AI trained on proven posts plus revenue tools (auto-DM, link plugging) inside a social scheduler.
- **Best for:** Creators, founders and marketers growing personal brands on X/LinkedIn

_Pricing verified 2026-08-13._

## Where Postwise stops

Postwise is cheaper, fast to adopt, and its auto-DM/sales tools directly compete for founders who only want social growth.

Capabilities Postwise doesn't cover that Collision does: Content, Analytics, Personal branding, Automation, Outbound, Lead generation.

## The best Postwise alternatives

- **[Buffer](/alternatives/buffer)** — Per-channel pricing and a genuinely free AI tier make it the default low-cost AI social tool for startups.
- **[FeedHive](/alternatives/feedhive)** — Workflow/condition-based automation plus AI agents inside a social scheduler.
- **[Hootsuite](/alternatives/hootsuite)** — OwlyGPT trained on real-time social trends plus 15+ years of social expertise and enterprise compliance tooling.
- **[Lately](/alternatives/lately)** — Statistical AI trained on a customer's own best posts predicts engaging words/ideas, not generic LLM copy.
- **[Predis.ai](/alternatives/predis-ai)** — Mass scale (6.4M users, 200M+ ads generated) and strong AI video-ad/UGC generation.
- **[Typefully](/alternatives/typefully)** — Creator-first writing experience with native support for emerging networks (Bluesky, Threads) and 10,000+ customers.

## Collision as a Postwise alternative

Overlaps Collision's AI content creation and distribution on X/LinkedIn/Threads, including growth tools and DM lead capture.

assessment - Collision adds strategy, SEO/AEO and outbound across channels; Postwise is single-purpose social content, so Collision bundles deeper

## Who should choose Postwise — and who shouldn't

Choose Postwise if: Creators, founders and marketers growing personal brands on X/LinkedIn matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
