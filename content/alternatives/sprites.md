# Sprites alternatives

AI performance marketing agent (YC W22, San Francisco) that runs and optimizes paid ads across Meta, Google, LinkedIn and TikTok with ROAS/CPL goal optimization, generates ad creatives, and executes SEO/AEO (AI visibility) content, keyword research and competitor intelligence for entrepreneurs and growth teams.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Sprites — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Sprites does well

Goal optimization (ROAS/CPL) across all ad accounts; Meta/Google/LinkedIn/TikTok campaign management; AI ad generator; creative analytics (hooks/angles); competitor intelligence; AI visibility (GEO/AEO); AI SEO content; keyword research; SEO site audit; B2B ABM; market research

## What Sprites costs

- **Pricing:** Freemium; paid from ~$48/mo (per SaaSworthy) (Freemium subscription)
- **Positioning:** Cross-channel ad execution (not just content) with ROAS/CPL goal optimization plus an SEO/AEO content engine - one agent for paid + organic
- **Best for:** Entrepreneurs, growth teams, SMB/startup marketers, ecommerce, PLG SaaS

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where Sprites stops

Sprites has 4+ years of product maturity, YC backing and live ad-platform execution - deeper paid-ads tooling than Collision likely offers (assessment).

Capabilities Sprites doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, Outbound, Advertising, Email.

## Collision as a Sprites alternative

Overlaps Collision's paid-ads execution, SEO, AEO and content capabilities for the same founder/growth-team buyers - a cross-channel agent covering organic + paid.

Collision adds outbound/SDR, lead gen, LinkedIn growth and personal branding; Sprites is ads+search-centric (assessment).

## Who should choose Sprites — and who shouldn't

Choose Sprites if: Entrepreneurs, growth teams, SMB/startup marketers, ecommerce, PLG SaaS matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
