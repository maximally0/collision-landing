# Airspeed alternatives

AI agents for revenue teams (rebranded from Glyphic): call and revenue intelligence, CRM auto-fill, deal management, rep coaching, forecasting and pipeline generation - agents deployed on revenue tools that increase win rates and keep the CRM clean. 100s of revenue teams, 4x YoY revenue.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Airspeed — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Airspeed does well

Agent suite across the revenue motion; HubSpot + Salesforce native; SOC 2 Type II; pipeline-generation agent drafts outbound on champion changes; sub-agent orchestration; Customer 360 roadmap

## What Airspeed costs

- **Pricing:** Contact sales (Subscription (per-seat))
- **Positioning:** Ex-DeepMind founders (Adam Liska, Devang Agrawal); agents that both analyze (deal risk, win/loss, forecasts) and execute (outbound drafts, CRM updates) on revenue data; 4x YoY revenue growth
- **Best for:** Revenue/GTM teams at B2B companies (100s of teams: Fermat, Foleon, Pricefx, Persona, TripleTen, Qdrant, GetAccept)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where Airspeed stops

Airspeed has a $20M Series A (DN Capital, Atlassian Ventures), ex-DeepMind team and 4x YoY revenue - deeper AI-sales execution credibility and enterprise GTM traction (assessment).

Capabilities Airspeed doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, AEO, SEO, Advertising.

## The best Airspeed alternatives

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities Airspeed doesn't cover.

- **[AdCreative.ai](/alternatives/adcreative-ai)** — Predictive conversion scoring of creatives before launch, trained on performance data
- **[Adzooma](/alternatives/adzooma)** — Free AI PPC optimization across three ad networks with two-click implementation
- **[AiSDR](/alternatives/aisdr)** — Pure-play AI SDR (YC-backed) with strong booked-meeting performance claims and HubSpot-native workflow.
- **[Apollo.io](/alternatives/apollo-io)** — Free-tier data + engagement + AI in one stack; 600K+ companies and a replace-five-tools pitch.
- **[AuthoredUp](/alternatives/authoredup)** — Polished writing + analytics workflow marketed as the #1 LinkedIn content tool (Chrome Web Store).
- **[Buffer](/alternatives/buffer)** — Per-channel pricing and a genuinely free AI tier make it the default low-cost AI social tool for startups.

## Collision as a Airspeed alternative

Overlaps with Collision's outbound/SDR and lead-gen capabilities via the pipeline-generation agent (auto-drafted outbound on deal signals) and revenue-signal execution; competes for the same B2B GTM budget.

Collision covers the full marketing stack (SEO/AEO/GEO, content, social, personal branding) beyond revenue-team ops; Airspeed is sales/revenue-execution focused (assessment).

## Who should choose Airspeed — and who shouldn't

Choose Airspeed if: Revenue/GTM teams at B2B companies (100s of teams: Fermat, Foleon, Pricefx, Persona, TripleTen, Qdrant, GetAccept) matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
