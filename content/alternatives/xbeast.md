# XBeast alternatives

AI agent for X/Twitter that writes, schedules, retweets, replies and plugs content in the user's voice on autopilot.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to XBeast — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What XBeast does well

Voice-trained presets; weekly auto-generation with human review; auto-reply, auto-retweet, auto-plug (link/offer on viral posts); YouTube/TikTok to tweet; analytics

## What XBeast costs

- **Pricing:** 7-day free trial; Basic $12/mo; Pro $24/mo; Ultra $72/mo (subscription)
- **Positioning:** Positioned as an AI agent that fully executes X growth (writes, replies, retweets, plugs), not just a scheduler.
- **Best for:** Creators, founders, indie hackers growing an X presence

_Pricing verified 2026-08-14._

## Where XBeast stops

XBeast's hands-off engagement automation is deeper on X than Collision likely offers; cheap ($12/mo) and fast for solo founders to adopt.

Capabilities XBeast doesn't cover that Collision does: Advertising, Execution, Strategy, AI agents, Lead generation, Personal branding.

## The best XBeast alternatives

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities XBeast doesn't cover.

- **[Buffer](/alternatives/buffer)** — Per-channel pricing and a genuinely free AI tier make it the default low-cost AI social tool for startups.
- **[FeedHive](/alternatives/feedhive)** — Workflow/condition-based automation plus AI agents inside a social scheduler.
- **[Hootsuite](/alternatives/hootsuite)** — OwlyGPT trained on real-time social trends plus 15+ years of social expertise and enterprise compliance tooling.
- **[Lately](/alternatives/lately)** — Statistical AI trained on a customer's own best posts predicts engaging words/ideas, not generic LLM copy.
- **[Postwise](/alternatives/postwise)** — Engagement-engineered AI trained on proven posts plus revenue tools (auto-DM, link plugging) inside a social scheduler.
- **[Predis.ai](/alternatives/predis-ai)** — Mass scale (6.4M users, 200M+ ads generated) and strong AI video-ad/UGC generation.

## Collision as a XBeast alternative

Direct overlap with Collision's AI social/personal-branding execution for founders: autonomous content creation and engagement on X.

## Who should choose XBeast — and who shouldn't

Choose XBeast if: Creators, founders, indie hackers growing an X presence matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
