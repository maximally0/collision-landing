# Runable alternatives

General-purpose AI agent platform (Bengaluru) that pivoted from AI website/app building into 'Grow': AI agents that find customers, run ad campaigns, manage social media, handle SEO and optimize brand presence in AI chatbot results for small businesses. $21M Series A (Aug 2026); 1.7M registered users; ~$2M annualized revenue run-rate within 3 weeks of launching payments (Mar 2026).

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Runable — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Runable does well

Natural-language build (websites, apps, slides, videos, carousels); deployment + analytics handled; Grow automates bookings, FAQs, repeat orders, social posting/replies, ad campaigns, SEO and AI-chatbot presence; 1.7M registered users; US/UK/Japan/Brazil markets

## What Runable costs

- **Pricing:** Freemium; paid plans live (not fully published) (Freemium subscription)
- **Positioning:** Huge warm build-side user base (1.7M) + $21M to push the same agent into autonomous growth; the only 'grow' entrant this week with a built-in distribution funnel and India-scale economics
- **Best for:** Non-technical small business owners and solo founders (US, UK, Japan focus)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where Runable stops

Runable has $21M (Susquehanna + Nexus), 1.7M users and a built-in funnel from its build product; it cites general-purpose agents (Manus, Genspark) as rivals, meaning marketing depth will be bought, not built (assessment)

Capabilities Runable doesn't cover that Collision does: Social media, Automation, AEO, Outbound, Advertising, Email.

## The best Runable alternatives

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities Runable doesn't cover.

- **[Adobe GenStudio](/alternatives/adobe-genstudio)** — Creative-production scale + brand governance + Adobe ecosystem (CDP, AEM, Workfront) — not autonomous strategy
- **[Hyper AI](/alternatives/hyper-ai)** — Prebuilt expert agents (not DIY automations) with deep ad-platform API execution and multi-client agency mode
- **[Jasper](/alternatives/jasper)** — Only major AI marketing platform with native GEO + SEO/AEO + agents + brand governance in one suite
- **[Persado](/alternatives/persado)** — Domain-specific motivation AI plus compliance engine for regulated verticals — clears UDAAP/TILA checks out of the box
- **[Simon AI](/alternatives/simon-ai)** — AI-first composable CDP + agents = enterprise-scale data-driven personalization, not content generation
- **[StoreClaw](/alternatives/storeclaw)** — First 'AI growth engine' purpose-built for store operators with a diagnostics-first agent loop

## Collision as a Runable alternative

Overlaps Collision's core: autonomous customer acquisition, ads, social, SEO and GEO/AI-search presence for SMBs - the same agency-replacement buyer and growth lanes as Collision's execution system

Collision has channel depth (SEO/AEO/GEO, LinkedIn growth, multi-platform outbound) and strategy-led execution; Runable's Grow arm is day-one and shares one general-purpose agent with its build product (assessment)

## Who should choose Runable — and who shouldn't

Choose Runable if: Non-technical small business owners and solo founders (US, UK, Japan focus) matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
