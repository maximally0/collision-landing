# Typefully alternatives

AI-powered writing and scheduling tool for X, LinkedIn, Bluesky and Threads; distraction-free editor with AI writing assistance and analytics.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Typefully — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Typefully does well

AI ideas & rewrites; distraction-free composer; real-time platform previews; cross-platform scheduling; analytics; social blog repurposing; team plans

## What Typefully costs

- **Pricing:** Free plan; Starter ~$14/mo; Team ~$29/mo; Agency/Enterprise up to $199/mo (subscription)
- **Positioning:** Creator-first writing experience with native support for emerging networks (Bluesky, Threads) and 10,000+ customers.
- **Best for:** Individual creators, founders and small teams building audiences

_Pricing verified 2026-08-14._

## Where Typefully stops

Typefully is beloved, affordable and sticky for solo creators; its platform-native formatting and social blog are hard to match.

Capabilities Typefully doesn't cover that Collision does: Lead generation, GEO, Sales, PR, SEO, Social media.

## The best Typefully alternatives

- **[Buffer](/alternatives/buffer)** — Per-channel pricing and a genuinely free AI tier make it the default low-cost AI social tool for startups.
- **[FeedHive](/alternatives/feedhive)** — Workflow/condition-based automation plus AI agents inside a social scheduler.
- **[Hootsuite](/alternatives/hootsuite)** — OwlyGPT trained on real-time social trends plus 15+ years of social expertise and enterprise compliance tooling.
- **[Lately](/alternatives/lately)** — Statistical AI trained on a customer's own best posts predicts engaging words/ideas, not generic LLM copy.
- **[Postwise](/alternatives/postwise)** — Engagement-engineered AI trained on proven posts plus revenue tools (auto-DM, link plugging) inside a social scheduler.
- **[Predis.ai](/alternatives/predis-ai)** — Mass scale (6.4M users, 200M+ ads generated) and strong AI video-ad/UGC generation.

## Collision as a Typefully alternative

Competes for Collision's content creation and distribution on X/LinkedIn/Bluesky/Threads and founder personal-brand consistency.

assessment - Collision pairs publishing with strategy, SEO/AEO and outbound; Typefully is a focused writing/scheduling layer Collision can bundle above

## Who should choose Typefully — and who shouldn't

Choose Typefully if: Individual creators, founders and small teams building audiences matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
