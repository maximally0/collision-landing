HubSpot Breeze (hubspot.com/products/artificial-intelligence, Cambridge, MA) is the AI layer inside HubSpot's CRM: a Breeze Copilot plus 20+ agents for content, social, prospecting and customer work that run marketing, sales and service on the customer data already living in HubSpot. It's included with Marketing, Sales and Service Hub Pro+ and runs on HubSpot credits, with outcome-based pricing for some agents since April 2026. If you're evaluating it, you're a HubSpot customer wondering whether you need anything else. Fair question.

## What HubSpot Breeze does well

- **Distribution, not just capability.** Breeze's agents reach HubSpot's enormous SMB install base — the database row calls it a scale Collision can't match (assessment). Millions of companies already trust HubSpot.
- **Native CRM grounding.** Agents act on the customer data where it already lives — no new system, no data migration, no new stack to learn.
- **Breadth out of the box.** 20+ agents across content (blogs, podcasts, case studies), multi-channel social strategies, prospecting and outreach — marketing, sales and service in one place.
- **Flexible pricing.** Included with Hub Pro+ tiers, credit-based for routine work, outcome-based for key agents since April 2026 — aligned to results where it matters.

## Where Breeze stops — and where Collision differs

Breeze lives inside the HubSpot ecosystem. Its agents run on HubSpot data, in HubSpot workflows, for HubSpot customers. That's its strength and its ceiling: growth that happens outside the CRM — SEO and AI search, LinkedIn personal branding, cold outbound, multi-surface distribution — isn't where Breeze lives (assessment).

Collision is built for that outside:

- **Channel-agnostic by design.** Collision's intelligence works across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews), email and outbound — no CRM required, no ecosystem lock-in.
- **Outbound and lead gen as core.** Breeze can draft prospecting touches inside HubSpot; Collision runs the full outbound loop — research, sequences, learning — as one system.
- **One memory.** Breeze grounds in your CRM records; Collision keeps one memory across every surface, so what it learns posting on LinkedIn makes next week's outbound smarter, and vice versa.
- **Approval gates on every action.** Breeze inherits HubSpot's workflow permissions; Collision puts an approval gate on every post, send and publish by design, with every action attributable.
- **Founder-shaped.** Breeze is priced and built for HubSpot's SMB-to-enterprise customer base; Collision is built for the founder who is the marketing team.

## Who should choose HubSpot Breeze

- You already run Marketing or Sales Hub Pro+ and want AI teammates inside the CRM you live in.
- Your growth motion is inbound, CRM-led and content-heavy — and HubSpot is your source of truth.
- You want outcome-based pricing on key agents and credits for the rest.

## Who should choose Collision instead

- Your growth happens outside the CRM: LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, cold outbound.
- You want one intelligence with one memory across every surface, not twenty agents inside one platform.
- You want outbound and lead gen as core capabilities, with an approval gate on every action.

## The bigger picture

Breeze is the right answer to a specific question: how do HubSpot's millions of SMBs get AI without leaving the CRM? Collision answers a different one: how does a founder run growth across every surface with one intelligence, one memory and approval on every action? If you're a HubSpot shop whose growth is mostly inside the hub, Breeze makes sense. If growth means getting in front of people who don't know you exist, that's Collision's loop.
