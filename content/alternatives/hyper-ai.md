Hyper AI (hyperfx.ai) is an AI-native marketing agent platform: CMO, paid-ads, social, SEO/AI-search and analytics agents that connect 200+ tools, run 24/7, and include multi-client agency workspaces. It targets marketing teams, founders and agencies, with a free tier ($10 credits) and paid plans whose tiers were unverified at time of research. Our competitor database calls it the most credible full-stack agentic marketing platform in its cohort — so this page takes it seriously.

## What Hyper AI does well

- **Real agent specialization.** Prebuilt expert agents — CMO, paid ads, social, SEO — rather than DIY workflows. You hire a specialist, not a config project.
- **Deep ad-platform execution.** Autonomous paid ads via Meta, Google, TikTok and LinkedIn APIs, with 80+ ad and marketing connectors. That's execution depth most of the AI CMO wave lacks — and it's something Collision doesn't match today (assessment).
- **200+ integrations and 24/7 operation.** Recurring hourly, daily and weekly tasks, Slack/Teams/iMessage surfaces, and MCP for external agents — it lives inside how a team already works.
- **Agency mode.** Multi-client workspaces make it genuinely useful for agencies running several brands.
- **Fast shipping.** The database row notes Hyper claims 1,000+ teams, ships quickly and has momentum behind it.

## Where Hyper AI stops — and where Collision differs

Hyper is a platform of specialist agents; Collision is one intelligence. The database row's own assessment: Collision's founder-focused SEO/AEO/GEO and personal-branding execution is more specialized than Hyper's generalist agents (assessment). And like most agent platforms, its center of gravity is execution across connected tools — not founder outbound.

Collision differs on the parts of growth that don't fit a connector:

- **Outbound, email and lead gen.** Hyper connects to outreach tools; Collision runs the outreach — research, sequences, replies, learning what converts.
- **One memory, not twenty agents.** Hyper's brand voice context shapes each agent's work; Collision keeps one memory across every surface, so the same intelligence that plans SEO also writes outreach and learns from both.
- **Approval gates on every action.** Hyper runs on scheduled recurring tasks; Collision's autonomy passes an approval gate on every post, send and publish, with every action attributable.
- **Founder-shaped depth.** SEO/AEO/GEO and LinkedIn personal branding as a system — not generalist agents covering the same ground at shallower depth (assessment).

## Who should choose Hyper AI

- You're a team or agency running paid ads and want an agent that executes across 200+ tools around the clock.
- You want prebuilt specialist agents over DIY workflows, with an agency workspace.
- Ad-platform API execution is the capability you're missing today.

## Who should choose Collision instead

- You're a founder whose growth is organic-first: LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound.
- You want one intelligence and one memory, not a panel of agents to manage.
- You want every action to pass an approval gate — posts, outreach, publishes — with a single source of truth behind them.

## The bigger picture

Hyper AI is the benchmark for agentic depth in this cohort, and the database row says so plainly — including the paid-ads execution Collision lacks (assessment). Collision's counter isn't more agents; it's fewer, deeper: one intelligence that owns the founder growth loop end to end, with outbound, one memory and approval gates where Hyper ships specialists and connectors. If you're hiring a platform of marketers, Hyper is credible. If you're hiring one intelligence that learns and runs your growth, that's Collision.
