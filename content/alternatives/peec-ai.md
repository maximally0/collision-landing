# Peec AI alternatives

GEO platform for the AI-search era: real-time brand visibility, source influence and sentiment tracking across ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and AI Overviews, with optimization workflows. Fastest-growing GEO vendor ($10M+ ARR by May 2026).

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Peec AI — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Peec AI does well

Multi-engine tracking (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude/Perplexity/AI Overviews); proprietary real-time data pipeline mapping source influence; prompt-level insights; 1,300+ brands and agencies (300+ new customers/mo); NYC office

## What Peec AI costs

- **Pricing:** Contact sales (demo) (Subscription)
- **Positioning:** Proprietary data pipeline mapping source influence/visibility/sentiment across AI engines in real time; first GEO vendor to cross $10M ARR
- **Best for:** Marketing teams at mid-market and enterprise brands (n8n, Attio, ElevenLabs, Chanel, TUI, Axel Springer, DEPT)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-14._

## Where Peec AI stops

Peec has category-leading GEO data infrastructure, $29M funding and 2.5x ARR growth in 6 months — well ahead of Collision's GEO tooling (assessment)

Capabilities Peec AI doesn't cover that Collision does: Advertising, Execution, Strategy, AI agents, Lead generation, Personal branding.

## Who should choose Peec AI — and who shouldn't
