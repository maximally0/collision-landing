# Turgo alternatives

Autonomous marketing execution platform ('autonomous GTM platform', product of Growstack Inc.) that deploys 5 AI employees - AI Inbound Marketer, AI Outbound Rep, AI Calling Agent, AI Media Buyer, AI Marketing Ops - to run the full B2B revenue cycle from first lead signal to booked meeting across email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, voice, paid media and CRM

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Turgo — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Turgo does well

5 AI employees (Inbound Marketer handles SEO blogs/AI search visibility/social selling; Outbound Rep runs cold email + LinkedIn automation; Calling Agent makes human-like voice calls; Media Buyer runs competitor ad analysis + retargeting; Marketing Ops handles lead scoring/reporting/CRM); multi-channel execution (email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, voice, Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, WordPress); 150+ pre-built workflow templates; 25+ integrations; 60M company / 300M contact proprietary database; ISO 42001:2023 and ISO 27001:2022 certified

## What Turgo costs

- **Pricing:** Credit-based plans (500 free credits); self-serve and enterprise tiers (Usage-based credits + subscription)
- **Positioning:** Complete-marketing-department positioning with voice calling included; founded by a Fortune 500-trained CMO (Pallav Tamaskar); ISO-certified; India-built cost structure; markets itself directly against Apollo, Clay and 11x
- **Best for:** B2B growth and marketing teams, SMB to enterprise

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where Turgo stops

Turgo's 5-AI-employee full-funnel loop including voice calling and paid media covers execution breadth Collision must match; India cost base and ISO certifications help it sell into enterprise (assessment)

Capabilities Turgo doesn't cover that Collision does: Automation, AEO, Advertising, Email, Strategy, Personal branding.
