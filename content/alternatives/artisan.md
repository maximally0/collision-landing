# Artisan alternatives

AI employee 'Ava' (AI BDR) finds leads, enriches contacts, sends hyper-personalized email/social outreach and books meetings autonomously.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Artisan — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Artisan does well

AI prospect ID; enriched B2B contacts; Web Visitor ID; Watchtower signal campaigns; CRM nurture; multi-agent engine; roadmap of more AI employees (Aaron, Aria)

## What Artisan costs

- **Pricing:** Custom (demo-based) (Subscription)
- **Positioning:** 'AI employees' positioning with autonomy levels (Level 2 sends outreach unassisted); 'Stop Hiring Humans' brand
- **Best for:** Startup/SMB/mid-market B2B sales teams

_Pricing verified 2026-08-13._

## Where Artisan stops

Viral brand, HubSpot/Sequoia backing, formal autonomy framework ahead of most rivals.

Capabilities Artisan doesn't cover that Collision does: Content, LinkedIn growth, Analytics, Personal branding, Automation, Lead generation.

## The best Artisan alternatives

- **[AiSDR](/alternatives/aisdr)** — Pure-play AI SDR (YC-backed) with strong booked-meeting performance claims and HubSpot-native workflow.
- **[Instantly](/alternatives/instantly)** — Owns cold-email deliverability infrastructure (unlimited accounts + warmup) and now ships AI SDR agents.
- **[Regie.ai](/alternatives/regie-ai)** — World's first AI-native sales engagement platform with patented agentic AI and 300% YoY ARR growth.
- **[Reply.io](/alternatives/reply-io)** — AI SDR agents (e.g., Jason AI) inside a mature multichannel engagement platform - automation-native, not bolted-on.
- **[Smartlead](/alternatives/smartlead)** — Deliverability-first cold email (unlimited mailboxes + AI warmup) that powers most outbound agencies and startups.
- **[11x](/alternatives/11x)** — Named 'digital workers' (Alice/Julian/Jordan) sold as employees replacing roles, not tools augmenting them

## Collision as a Artisan alternative

Explicit multi-product 'Artisan workforce' strategy (BDR, inbound, meetings) — marketing employees are the obvious next role, colliding with Collision's outbound + growth remit.

Collision adds SEO/content/social/personal branding to outbound; Artisan sells sales roles only. Assessment.

## Who should choose Artisan — and who shouldn't

Choose Artisan if: Startup/SMB/mid-market B2B sales teams matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
