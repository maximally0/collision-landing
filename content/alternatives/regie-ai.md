# Regie.ai alternatives

AI-native sales engagement platform: AI agents source leads, write personalized outreach and sequence follow-ups; RegieOne unifies it.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Regie.ai — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Regie.ai does well

AI Agents; audience builder; personalized content generation; signal-based prioritization; AI dialer (9-line parallel); RegieOne SEP; enterprise integrations

## What Regie.ai costs

- **Pricing:** AI Agents packages from $35K/yr; RegieOne free with annual contract (subscription (annual))
- **Positioning:** World's first AI-native sales engagement platform with patented agentic AI and 300% YoY ARR growth.
- **Best for:** B2B sales teams and SDR orgs (mid-market/enterprise)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-13._

## Where Regie.ai stops

Deep enterprise AI-SDR capability ($50.8M raised) sets the bar for outbound automation quality Collision is measured against.

Capabilities Regie.ai doesn't cover that Collision does: Content, LinkedIn growth, Analytics, Personal branding, Automation, Outbound.

## The best Regie.ai alternatives

- **[AiSDR](/alternatives/aisdr)** — Pure-play AI SDR (YC-backed) with strong booked-meeting performance claims and HubSpot-native workflow.
- **[Instantly](/alternatives/instantly)** — Owns cold-email deliverability infrastructure (unlimited accounts + warmup) and now ships AI SDR agents.
- **[Reply.io](/alternatives/reply-io)** — AI SDR agents (e.g., Jason AI) inside a mature multichannel engagement platform - automation-native, not bolted-on.
- **[Smartlead](/alternatives/smartlead)** — Deliverability-first cold email (unlimited mailboxes + AI warmup) that powers most outbound agencies and startups.
- **[11x](/alternatives/11x)** — Named 'digital workers' (Alice/Julian/Jordan) sold as employees replacing roles, not tools augmenting them
- **[Artisan](/alternatives/artisan)** — 'AI employees' positioning with autonomy levels (Level 2 sends outreach unassisted); 'Stop Hiring Humans' brand

## Collision as a Regie.ai alternative

Overlaps Collision's outbound/SDR execution: AI-sourced leads, personalized email/LinkedIn outreach and follow-up automation.

assessment - Collision bundles content, SEO/AEO and social; Regie is enterprise-priced outbound automation for sales teams, not founder-led marketing

## Who should choose Regie.ai — and who shouldn't

Choose Regie.ai if: B2B sales teams and SDR orgs (mid-market/enterprise) matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
