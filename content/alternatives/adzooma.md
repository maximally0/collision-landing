# Adzooma alternatives

AI-powered PPC platform that audits, optimizes and scales Google, Microsoft and Meta campaigns with automated, one-click recommendations.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Adzooma — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Adzooma does well

24/7 machine-learning analysis; one-click recommendation application; performance scorecards; automated alerts; AI ad copy; multi-channel reporting

## What Adzooma costs

- **Pricing:** Free; Pro from ~£69/mo (Freemium subscription)
- **Positioning:** Free AI PPC optimization across three ad networks with two-click implementation
- **Best for:** SMBs, agencies, freelancers and founders running paid ads

_Pricing verified 2026-08-14._

## Where Adzooma stops

Recommendations rather than full agentic execution; no content, PR or outbound; smaller brand and traction than Madgicx

Capabilities Adzooma doesn't cover that Collision does: SEO, LinkedIn growth, GEO, Email, AI agents, Strategy.

## The best Adzooma alternatives

- **[AdCreative.ai](/alternatives/adcreative-ai)** — Predictive conversion scoring of creatives before launch, trained on performance data
- **[Madgicx](/alternatives/madgicx)** — First AI ad agent for Meta plus exclusive AI Bidding via Meta partnership
- **[Smartly.io](/alternatives/smartly-io)** — Only platform managing creative and media end-to-end for 700+ global brands at 300B creatives/year
- **[InstaAgent](/alternatives/instaagent)** — Hundreds of distinct audiences with distinct content from one brief — the anti-generic ad engine
- **[AiSDR](/alternatives/aisdr)** — Pure-play AI SDR (YC-backed) with strong booked-meeting performance claims and HubSpot-native workflow.
- **[Apollo.io](/alternatives/apollo-io)** — Free-tier data + engagement + AI in one stack; 600K+ companies and a replace-five-tools pitch.

## Collision as a Adzooma alternative

Paid-ad optimization and automation overlaps Collision's ad execution; SEO/website audits parallel Collision's growth audit capability for founders.

assessment: Collision executes autonomously end-to-end; Adzooma still requires user clicks to apply most recommendations

## Who should choose Adzooma — and who shouldn't

Choose Adzooma if: SMBs, agencies, freelancers and founders running paid ads matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
