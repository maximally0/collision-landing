# Protaigé alternatives

Autonomous AI marketing platform (Singapore): Maia, 'world's first AI Account Director', briefs, plans and executes campaigns from the channels marketers already use (email, WhatsApp, Slack, video meetings), orchestrating ~200 tools across 30 categories with proprietary Brand DNA governance. Public launch May 2026 after Nov 2025 beta; 1,000+ brands onboarded.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Protaigé — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Protaigé does well

Maia via Email, Meetings (joins video calls to capture briefs) and WhatsApp; Brand DNA (strategy, voice, identity, governance); orchestration of ~200 tools across 30 categories; performance-data loop optimizing future output; localized multi-market asset production

## What Protaigé costs

- **Pricing:** Free tier ('Get Started Free'); paid plans not published (demo-based) (SaaS subscription)
- **Positioning:** Channel-native AI Account Director (operates inside existing communication tools, no new platform to learn) with Brand DNA governance; 1,000+ brands since beta; Best Newcomer Global Startup Awards + top-5 Product Hunt ranking
- **Best for:** Marketing teams at SMB/mid-market brands (1,000+ brands onboarded since beta)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where Protaigé stops

Protaigé's channel-native delivery (email/WhatsApp/Slack) and 1,000+ onboarded brands give it fast adoption momentum, especially in APAC markets Collision may not reach (assessment)

Capabilities Protaigé doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, AEO, SEO, Outbound.

## The best Protaigé alternatives

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities Protaigé doesn't cover.

- **[Adobe GenStudio](/alternatives/adobe-genstudio)** — Creative-production scale + brand governance + Adobe ecosystem (CDP, AEM, Workfront) — not autonomous strategy
- **[Hyper AI](/alternatives/hyper-ai)** — Prebuilt expert agents (not DIY automations) with deep ad-platform API execution and multi-client agency mode
- **[Jasper](/alternatives/jasper)** — Only major AI marketing platform with native GEO + SEO/AEO + agents + brand governance in one suite
- **[Persado](/alternatives/persado)** — Domain-specific motivation AI plus compliance engine for regulated verticals — clears UDAAP/TILA checks out of the box
- **[Simon AI](/alternatives/simon-ai)** — AI-first composable CDP + agents = enterprise-scale data-driven personalization, not content generation
- **[StoreClaw](/alternatives/storeclaw)** — First 'AI growth engine' purpose-built for store operators with a diagnostics-first agent loop

## Collision as a Protaigé alternative

Direct overlap with Collision's autonomous marketing execution: brief-to-strategy-to-assets-to-execution across channels is Collision's core loop, sold to marketing teams (SMB/mid-market) rather than founders

Collision covers SEO/AEO/GEO, outbound, lead gen and personal branding with founder-priced productization; Protaigé is campaign-asset execution for existing marketing teams with no organic-search or outbound stack (assessment)

## Who should choose Protaigé — and who shouldn't

Choose Protaigé if: Marketing teams at SMB/mid-market brands (1,000+ brands onboarded since beta) matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
