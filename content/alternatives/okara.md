Okara is one of the closest things to Collision on the market: an AI CMO for founders that runs ten-plus marketing agents across SEO, GEO, Reddit, LinkedIn and X, 24/7. It has a free tier, a $66/month paid plan, and claims north of 100,000 users. If you're evaluating it, you're probably asking the right question: *can software actually run my growth?*

The honest answer is that Okara answers that question for the **drafting half** of growth — and stops there.

## What Okara does well

Okara's real strengths are clear:

- **GEO-first positioning.** It audits whether your brand shows up in ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini answers, and it drafts content designed to get you cited. It was early here, and it shows in the product.
- **A genuinely useful free tier.** 20 free credits, no credit card, real drafts out. For a founder testing whether AI marketing is for them, the barrier to try is close to zero.
- **Community-channel depth.** Its Reddit and X agents are built around where founders and early adopters actually talk. Most AI marketing products don't touch community channels at all.
- **Approval workflow.** Nothing ships without your sign-off, which is the right default for anything that posts as your company.

## Where Okara stops

Okara is a **draft-and-approve** system. It generates assets — posts, articles, GEO-targeted content — and you (or someone on your team) still have to take those drafts somewhere: publish them, run the outbound, build the email sequences, chase the replies, compile what worked. That's the part of growth that actually costs founders their week.

There is no outbound engine in Okara. No email, no SDR motion, no lead generation pipeline. The system drafts; the human executes.

## Who should choose Okara

- You want to spend $66/month to see what AI drafts look like before committing to anything.
- Your growth motion is *only* content drafting, and you're happy doing distribution yourself.
- You specifically need GEO/Reddit coverage and already have outbound handled elsewhere.

## Who should choose Collision instead

- You don't want a stack — you want one intelligence that owns the whole loop, including the parts Okara doesn't touch: outbound, email, lead gen, distribution.
- You care about what the system *learns*. Collision keeps one memory across every surface, so the strategy conversation from Tuesday actually shapes the LinkedIn posts on Friday.
- You want a Head of Growth, not a draft generator.

## The bigger picture

Okara and Collision aren't really in the same category. Okara is an AI drafting layer for content channels. Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — and that difference shows up in the parts of your week each one takes off your plate.
