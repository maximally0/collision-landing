# DOJO AI alternatives

Intelligent marketing system for challenger brands: a living knowledge graph (DOJO Graph) models the brand's marketing reality; specialized AI agents act on it across paid, organic, SEO and content, feeding every outcome back into the model.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to DOJO AI — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What DOJO AI does well

DOJO Graph living knowledge graph; proactive agentic workflows (not prompt-driven); brand/demand/creative intelligence; unlimited competitive monitoring; revenue intelligence; queryable dashboards; 20% MoM growth; 100+ brands

## What DOJO AI costs

- **Pricing:** From $499/mo; 7-day free trial; Starter/Growth/Enterprise tiers (Subscription)
- **Positioning:** Knowledge-graph-first marketing intelligence where agents execute (not just report) and every outcome feeds back into the model — 'most AI resets, DOJO compounds'
- **Best for:** Challenger brands and mid-market marketing teams (CoinDesk, MorningStar, PensionBee, Broadvoice, Refine Labs)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where DOJO AI stops

DOJO has $6M funding, 100+ named customers, 20% MoM growth and a closed-loop learning architecture — a well-resourced full-stack rival to Collision's execution story (assessment).

Capabilities DOJO AI doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, AEO, Outbound, Advertising.

## Collision as a DOJO AI alternative

Overlaps Collision's marketing intelligence, SEO, content and campaign execution: DOJO agents autonomously run paid/organic/SEO/content workflows for the same mid-market buyers Collision targets.

Collision is founder-priced and self-serve with outbound, LinkedIn/personal branding and lead gen; DOJO is intelligence-plus-execution aimed at established challenger brands at $499+/mo (assessment).

## Who should choose DOJO AI — and who shouldn't

Choose DOJO AI if: Challenger brands and mid-market marketing teams (CoinDesk, MorningStar, PensionBee, Broadvoice, Refine Labs) matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
