NoimosAI (noimosai.com, Japan/USA) is an all-in-one AI marketing agent that plans, creates and executes marketing on its own, 24/7, learning your brand DNA from outcomes. It is bootstrapped, priced at $99/$249/$499 per month (credit-based), and aimed at content creators, freelancers, startups and SMBs. Its pitch — the output of a $3k–$25k/mo marketing team at $99–$499/mo — is the same pitch Collision makes, which is exactly why you're reading this page.

## What NoimosAI does well

- **Proactive autonomy.** NoimosAI takes initiative; it doesn't wait to be told. A proactive 24/7 mode is a genuinely different product posture from request-driven assistants (assessment).
- **Real platform integrations.** X, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Google Analytics, Google Search Console and Slack — the surfaces where SMB growth actually happens.
- **Privacy as a feature.** Encrypted data and no third-party AI training is a real selling point for founders who don't want their brand material feeding someone else's model.
- **Published proof.** The database row notes published case studies, including claims of 10x ROI and 20M+ monthly reach (assessment) — live customer evidence most competitors lack.
- **Clear pricing.** Pro $99/user/mo, Team $249, Advanced $499, credit-based, with 30k–160k credits per month depending on plan.

## Where NoimosAI stops — and where Collision differs

The database row is specific: Collision is stronger on SEO/AEO/GEO, outbound and lead gen, SDR, personal branding and LinkedIn growth depth (assessment). NoimosAI's center of gravity is social and analytics: listening, content, posting and learning from what performs. Outbound and lead gen are not its headline motion, and its SEO depth doesn't reach AEO/GEO territory.

Collision differs where founders actually lose weeks:

- **Outbound, email and lead gen as a first-class pillar.** NoimosAI grows what you publish; Collision also runs the outreach that turns strangers into pipeline.
- **SEO/AEO/GEO depth.** Collision's intelligence researches and executes across search and AI search — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews — as a core loop, not an add-on.
- **One memory.** NoimosAI learns brand DNA from outcomes; Collision keeps one memory across every surface, so learning from a campaign send, a LinkedIn post and an SEO brief all compound into the same source of truth.
- **Approval gates on every action.** Autonomy is great until it ships something you regret. Collision's autonomy comes with approval gates on every post, send and publish — the control NoimosAI's "full control" pitch promises, but Collision enforces by design.

- Data privacy — no third-party training — is a hard requirement for your brand.

## Who should choose Collision instead

- You need outbound, email and lead gen — not just publishing.
