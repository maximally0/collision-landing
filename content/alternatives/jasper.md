Jasper (jasper.ai, Austin, TX) is the AI marketing platform that grew out of the AI copywriting boom: it has raised $131M total — including a $125M Series A in 2022 at a $1.5B valuation — and now ships purpose-built agents for campaigns, content pipelines, and GEO, SEO and AEO work. If you're evaluating it, you already know it writes well; the question is whether it runs growth. Collision's answer to that question is what this page is about.

## What Jasper does well

- **Depth on the content side.** Jasper is a category-defining writer: brand voice, style guides and knowledge-base grounding keep long-form content on-brand at scale.
- **GEO/SEO/AEO in one suite.** Jasper is the only major AI marketing platform with native GEO citation monitoring plus SEO and AEO content, per our competitor database — a genuinely differentiated bundle.
- **A real agents library.** Product-launch and social-campaign agents execute end-to-end workflows, and APIs plus MCP keep it embeddable in an existing marketing stack.
- **Scale and trust.** $131M raised, an enterprise customer base, a Semrush integration and HubSpot Ventures on the cap table. It is not a risky buy.

## Where Jasper stops — and where Collision differs

Jasper's center of gravity is content and campaign production for marketing teams (assessment). The database row is blunt: Jasper is content and campaign creation, not growth execution. It doesn't run outbound, SDR or lead gen. It doesn't build a founder's LinkedIn presence as a system. Distribution — who sees what you wrote — is mostly left to the humans.

Collision differs on exactly those axes:

- **Outbound, email and lead gen are core.** Jasper writes; Collision researches prospects, writes outreach, runs sequences and learns what converts.
- **One memory that compounds.** Jasper's knowledge base grounds content in what you tell it. Collision's memory learns from outcomes across every surface — so the same intelligence that runs LinkedIn also runs SEO, email and outbound, and gets smarter each week.
- **Distribution is owned, not bolted on.** Publishing, posting, outreach and learning are part of Collision's loop, with approval gates on every action.
- **Founder-first, not team-first.** Jasper is built for marketing teams with budgets; Collision is built for founders who are the marketing team.

## Who should choose Jasper

- Your team produces a high volume of on-brand long-form content and needs GEO/SEO/AEO coverage with governance.
- You have a marketing team and want a writing-and-campaign layer they can direct.
- Enterprise brand safety — style guides and knowledge grounding — is a hard requirement.

## Who should choose Collision instead

- You're a founder who needs growth execution — outbound, lead gen, distribution — not just production.
- You want one intelligence that owns the loop and one memory across every surface.
- You'd rather hire the AI that runs growth than subscribe to the AI that writes assets.

## The bigger picture

Jasper is the incumbent with the war chest — and the database row says its content and GEO depth outmatch Collision there (assessment). But growth isn't a writing problem; it's a loop problem. Jasper owns the writing half of the loop at enterprise scale. Collision owns the whole loop for founders: research, plan, write, distribute, learn — with approval gates and one memory.
