# Autumn AI alternatives

Real-time signal intelligence for GTM teams (YC Winter 2026): monitors posts, commits, blogs and announcements to surface buying signals the moment they appear; delivers a condensed, real-time feed filtered by intent against a defined ICP.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Autumn AI — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Autumn AI does well

Real-time signal monitoring; ICP definition; intent filtering; condensed feed; YC W26 batch

## What Autumn AI costs

- **Pricing:** Undisclosed (SaaS subscription)
- **Positioning:** Real-time (not batch) signal intelligence across posts, commits, blogs and announcements - signals delivered the moment they appear.
- **Best for:** B2B GTM teams (founders, SDR/AE teams)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where Autumn AI stops

YC-backed real-time intent data could become the input layer other outbound tools - and Collision's customers - plug into, commoditizing signal sourcing (assessment).

Capabilities Autumn AI doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, AEO, SEO, Advertising.

## The best Autumn AI alternatives

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities Autumn AI doesn't cover.

- **[Apollo.io](/alternatives/apollo-io)** — Free-tier data + engagement + AI in one stack; 600K+ companies and a replace-five-tools pitch.
- **[Clay](/alternatives/clay)** — The go-to AI GTM data layer with best-in-class enrichment and a powerful agentic workflow builder.
- **[Snov.io](/alternatives/snov-io)** — One affordable toolkit spanning find, verify, warmup, send and CRM, with AI Studio and MCP integration.
- **[Brew](/alternatives/brew)** — Agent-native ESP (docs for OpenClaw/Claude/Lovable) with production-grade email rendering and no lock-in
- **[CharacterQuilt](/alternatives/characterquilt)** — Operates your existing stack instead of replacing it — 'sells the plumbing, not the chatbox'
- **[CMO Miko](/alternatives/cmo-miko)** — First 'AGI CMO' positioned explicitly for indie hackers/startups, built by a solo builder

## Collision as a Autumn AI alternative

Overlaps with Collision's lead-gen and outbound capabilities: buying-signal detection feeding outreach sequences is part of Collision's growth stack.

Collision executes the full loop (signal to outreach to content to SEO); Autumn is a signal feed that still needs an execution layer (assessment).

## Who should choose Autumn AI — and who shouldn't

Choose Autumn AI if: B2B GTM teams (founders, SDR/AE teams) matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
