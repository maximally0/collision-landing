# Zeta Global alternatives

Public enterprise marketing cloud (NYSE: ZETA, founded 2007): CDP, omnichannel activation, ESP/messaging, loyalty and analytics for Fortune 500 marketers; launched Athena by Zeta (GA Mar 2026), an agent positioned as "the first superintelligent agent built for marketers" that plans, activates and optimizes across the Zeta Marketing Platform.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Zeta Global — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Zeta Global does well

Athena agent GA (Mar 24 2026); Palantir strategic partnership; 18th straight beat-and-raise quarter (record FY2025 revenue); proprietary data + identity graph; AI orchestration; agency-facing Athena tier

## What Zeta Global costs

- **Pricing:** Undisclosed (enterprise) (Enterprise subscription)
- **Positioning:** Superintelligent-agent positioning (Athena) on top of a full enterprise marketing cloud with proprietary identity data - an incumbent with distribution, budgets and measurement stepping into agentic marketing
- **Best for:** Enterprise brands and agencies (Fortune 500 marketers)

## Where Zeta Global stops

Zeta's proprietary identity data, public-company balance sheet, record growth and Palantir partnership give it enterprise credibility and distribution Collision cannot match in that segment (assessment)

Capabilities Zeta Global doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, AEO, SEO, Outbound.

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities Zeta Global doesn't cover.
