# Tell if AI alternatives

AI-run performance marketing agency (YC F25): autonomous agents on real-phone fleets create, post, grow content across YouTube, TikTok, X, Reddit

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Tell if AI — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Tell if AI does well

Fleet of real phones (human-indistinguishable); ideation-to-video-to-posting-to-engagement pipeline; parallel account operation; revenue-share pricing; private beta

## What Tell if AI costs

- **Pricing:** Revenue share (aligned incentives) (Service retainer)
- **Positioning:** Real-phone fleet makes activity indistinguishable from human creators; revenue share aligns incentives
- **Best for:** Brands hiring growth agencies (revenue-share)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where Tell if AI stops

Physical phone-fleet approach and creator-grade content could outperform software-only social growth; revenue share lowers client risk (assessment)

Capabilities Tell if AI doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, AEO, SEO, Outbound.

## The best Tell if AI alternatives

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities Tell if AI doesn't cover.

- **[Mighty & True](/alternatives/mighty-true)** — Autonomous systems run on their own business first (their SEO page is optimized by an agent loop), with named, documente
- **[Lazyweb](/alternatives/lazyweb)** — Growth knowledge as an MCP layer AI agents can execute — founded by ex-Duolingo/Discord/Dropbox growth leaders (Ali Abou
- **[Mega](/alternatives/mega)** — Services delivered via software: full agency replacement with no prompting, tool management or agency overhead
- **[Adobe GenStudio](/alternatives/adobe-genstudio)** — Creative-production scale + brand governance + Adobe ecosystem (CDP, AEM, Workfront) — not autonomous strategy
- **[AI CMO (tryaicmo.com)](/alternatives/ai-cmo-tryaicmo-com)** — Combines social listening with AI content distribution at SMB prices ($99 vs $79+/tool for Brand24 alone)
- **[Copy.ai](/alternatives/copy-ai)** — Autonomous GTM workflows spanning sales + marketing (not just copy) with a massive freemium user base

## Collision as a Tell if AI alternative

Overlaps Collision content creation/distribution, social media growth and engagement on short-video platforms

Collision covers SEO/AEO/GEO, outbound, email and LinkedIn branding; Tell if AI is social content growth only (assessment)

## Who should choose Tell if AI — and who shouldn't

Choose Tell if AI if: Brands hiring growth agencies (revenue-share) matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
