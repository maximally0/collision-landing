# CMO Miko alternatives

AI CMO product for indie hackers & startups: SEO monitoring, content strategy, growth analytics, backlink building. Waitlist only, launching Q1 2026.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to CMO Miko — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What CMO Miko does well

SEO monitoring; content strategy; growth analytics; backlink building; free tools + blog funnel; waitlist access

## What CMO Miko costs

- **Pricing:** Not publicly listed (subscription)
- **Positioning:** First 'AGI CMO' positioned explicitly for indie hackers/startups, built by a solo builder
- **Best for:** Indie hackers and startup founders

_Pricing verified 2026-08-14._

## Where CMO Miko stops

CMO Miko's simplicity and free-tools funnel could win price-sensitive indie hackers first; Collision must prove depth against the AGI-CMO narrative

Capabilities CMO Miko doesn't cover that Collision does: Lead generation, GEO, Sales, LinkedIn growth, PR, SEO.

- **[AI CMO (tryaicmo.com)](/alternatives/ai-cmo-tryaicmo-com)** — Combines social listening with AI content distribution at SMB prices ($99 vs $79+/tool for Brand24 alone)

Same ICP (founders/startups) and same wedge (autonomous marketing intelligence incl. SEO). If it ships content+distribution it becomes a direct full-stack rival; Collision delivers this today

Collision is live with a shipped product; CMO Miko is waitlist-only until Q1 2026 (assessment)

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
