# Laurence alternatives

Autonomous performance marketing for e-commerce (YC Winter 2026): automates advertising the way quant firms trade markets, starting with Amazon PPC where it autonomously executes tens of millions of dollars of ad spend with 15-40% net-profit improvements within a month of onboarding; closed beta expanding to ChatGPT, Google, Meta, TikTok and Walmart.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Laurence — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Laurence does well

15-40% net profit improvement within ~30 days (company-reported); tens of millions of dollars of Amazon ad spend under management; systematic portfolio-style allocation; instant free audit

## What Laurence costs

- **Pricing:** Undisclosed (free audit) (Automation on ad spend)
- **Positioning:** 'Hedge fund for ads' positioning (YC W26): quant-style systematic ad allocation optimizing profit after margin, seeded by Susa/Box Group with OpenAI and Amazon angels; expanding beyond Amazon into AI-chat and social ad surfaces
- **Best for:** E-commerce brands of all sizes (health supplements, pet electronics, home cleaning products)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where Laurence stops

Laurence's quant-style profit optimization on live ad spend and its founder-friendly 'no agencies, no wasted clicks' pitch are credible, well-funded (Susa/Box/YC; OpenAI and Amazon angels) and already deployed on real DTC budgets (assessment)

Capabilities Laurence doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, AEO, SEO, Outbound.

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities Laurence doesn't cover.
