# tday alternatives

YC P26 startup that turns a software product + git repo into on-brand marketing creative (launch videos, social graphics, ads), then publishes and measures it across channels.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to tday — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What tday does well

GitHub integration; brand-consistent creative; publish + measure across social and ad channels

## What tday costs

- **Pricing:** Not publicly listed (subscription)
- **Positioning:** Product-source-of-truth creative engine — content generated from actual shipped features
- **Best for:** Software companies and technical founders

_Pricing verified 2026-08-14._

## Where tday stops

Automated launch videos/ads are a fast-growing need; tday could capture the 'launch content' slice first

Capabilities tday doesn't cover that Collision does: Advertising, Execution, Strategy, AI agents, Lead generation, Personal branding.

## The best tday alternatives

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities tday doesn't cover.

- **[MarketMuse](/alternatives/marketmuse)** — Decade of NLP topical modeling for content intelligence (now inside Siteimprove)
- **[StoryChief](/alternatives/storychief)** — Distribution-first content platform pairing AI with publishing automation to every channel
- **[Typeface](/alternatives/typeface)** — $1B-valued brand-intelligence-first AI content platform with Salesforce/Google partnerships
- **[Writer.com](/alternatives/writer-com)** — Own enterprise-grade LLMs plus GraphRAG grounding, built for regulated enterprises
- **[Writesonic](/alternatives/writesonic)** — First mover executing AEO/GEO fixes agentically, not just tracking visibility
- **[Memoir](/alternatives/memoir)** — Starts from the product source of truth (git/ship data), not a prompt — 'a marketer with the soul of an engineer'

## Collision as a tday alternative

Content creation + distribution for product-led founders overlaps Collision's content pipeline; tday focuses on launch creative while Collision adds SEO/AEO/GEO strategy and outbound

Collision's intelligence layer (SEO/AEO/GEO strategy, multi-platform outbound) goes beyond creative generation (assessment)

## Who should choose tday — and who shouldn't

Choose tday if: Software companies and technical founders matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
