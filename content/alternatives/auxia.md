# Auxia alternatives

Agentic marketing platform for enterprise B2C lifecycle teams: Auxia Decisioning personalizes/orchestrates every customer interaction in real time (200B+ decisions delivered, 500M+/day), and Auxia Agent Studio is a command center where AI agents plan, build and launch campaigns across the team's existing ESP/CDP stack

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Auxia — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Auxia does well

500M+ decisions/day; marketing context graph (brand voice, campaign history); natural-language agentic workflows across ESPs/CDPs; collaborative approval workspace; localized campaign generation at global scale; model-driven experimentation replacing manual A/B testing

## What Auxia costs

- **Pricing:** Enterprise (demo/early-access waitlist) (Enterprise subscription)
- **Positioning:** Proven decisioning scale (200B+ decisions; customers Atlassian, Comcast, Docomo) plus a new agentic execution layer - enterprise-grade autonomous marketing backed by real personalization data
- **Best for:** Enterprise B2C lifecycle marketing teams (Fortune 500)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where Auxia stops

Auxia has enterprise logos, VMG-led capital and 500M decisions/day infrastructure - execution scale and trust far beyond Collision (assessment)

Capabilities Auxia doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, AEO, SEO, Outbound.

## The best Auxia alternatives

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities Auxia doesn't cover.

- **[Adobe GenStudio](/alternatives/adobe-genstudio)** — Creative-production scale + brand governance + Adobe ecosystem (CDP, AEM, Workfront) — not autonomous strategy
- **[Hyper AI](/alternatives/hyper-ai)** — Prebuilt expert agents (not DIY automations) with deep ad-platform API execution and multi-client agency mode
- **[Jasper](/alternatives/jasper)** — Only major AI marketing platform with native GEO + SEO/AEO + agents + brand governance in one suite
- **[Persado](/alternatives/persado)** — Domain-specific motivation AI plus compliance engine for regulated verticals — clears UDAAP/TILA checks out of the box
- **[Simon AI](/alternatives/simon-ai)** — AI-first composable CDP + agents = enterprise-scale data-driven personalization, not content generation
- **[StoreClaw](/alternatives/storeclaw)** — First 'AI growth engine' purpose-built for store operators with a diagnostics-first agent loop

## Collision as a Auxia alternative

Overlaps Collision in autonomous marketing execution and marketing-OS ambition, but from enterprise lifecycle/personalization rather than founder/SMB growth

Collision is stronger on founder-centric SEO/AEO/GEO, outbound, personal branding and LinkedIn growth; Auxia targets enterprise lifecycle personalization (assessment)

## Who should choose Auxia — and who shouldn't

Choose Auxia if: Enterprise B2C lifecycle marketing teams (Fortune 500) matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
