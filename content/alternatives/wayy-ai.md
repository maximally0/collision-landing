# Wayy.ai alternatives

AI co-founder that sells for you: autonomous sales workflow for solopreneurs/SMBs — analyzes the company and LinkedIn profile, identifies prospects via a multi-level scoring/qualification engine, drafts personalized outreach, requests approval before contact, and self-refines targeting every 3 days.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Wayy.ai — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Wayy.ai does well

Multi-level ICP scoring/qualification; autonomous identification of prospects/investors/partners; personalized outreach drafts; user approval before contact; 3-day self-refinement loop; ~3% response rate claim; compliance-capped daily outreach; $49-$149/mo

## What Wayy.ai costs

- **Pricing:** $49/$99/$149 per month (Subscription)
- **Positioning:** Positioned as an 'AI co-founder' (not a tool) for solopreneurs, with an autonomous qualification engine and self-refining loops at $49-$149/mo
- **Best for:** Solopreneurs, founders and small business owners (70+ companies using)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where Wayy.ai stops

Wayy's $49 entry price, solopreneur targeting and self-refining outreach loop are a cheap, focused alternative for Collision's outbound-only buyers (assessment).

Capabilities Wayy.ai doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, AEO, SEO, Outbound.

## The best Wayy.ai alternatives

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities Wayy.ai doesn't cover.

- **[AiSDR](/alternatives/aisdr)** — Pure-play AI SDR (YC-backed) with strong booked-meeting performance claims and HubSpot-native workflow.
- **[Instantly](/alternatives/instantly)** — Owns cold-email deliverability infrastructure (unlimited accounts + warmup) and now ships AI SDR agents.
- **[Regie.ai](/alternatives/regie-ai)** — World's first AI-native sales engagement platform with patented agentic AI and 300% YoY ARR growth.
- **[Reply.io](/alternatives/reply-io)** — AI SDR agents (e.g., Jason AI) inside a mature multichannel engagement platform - automation-native, not bolted-on.
- **[Smartlead](/alternatives/smartlead)** — Deliverability-first cold email (unlimited mailboxes + AI warmup) that powers most outbound agencies and startups.
- **[11x](/alternatives/11x)** — Named 'digital workers' (Alice/Julian/Jordan) sold as employees replacing roles, not tools augmenting them

## Collision as a Wayy.ai alternative

Overlaps Collision's outbound and lead-gen pillar: autonomous prospecting, scoring and personalized outreach for founders — the same 'AI does outbound for you' value prop at founder prices.

Collision bundles outbound with SEO/AEO/GEO, content, LinkedIn and strategy; Wayy is sales-outreach-only (assessment).

## Who should choose Wayy.ai — and who shouldn't

Choose Wayy.ai if: Solopreneurs, founders and small business owners (70+ companies using) matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
