# Lapis alternatives

Self-improving ads engine (YC Fall 2025): AI agents create, launch and optimize paid campaigns end-to-end — ChatSense (ChatGPT-native ads), OmniSense (Google/Meta/LinkedIn) and RapidDomain (self-optimizing landing pages).

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Lapis — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Lapis does well

End-to-end campaign lifecycle; ChatGPT sponsored-result ads; AI ad copy + static creative; per-ad custom landing pages; self-improving loop from real interaction data; brand-from-site learning; A/B testing at scale; competitor ad tracking; Brand Radar 10,000+ ad library; 10 languages; 20x performance claims

## What Lapis costs

- **Pricing:** Tiered plans with credits (Starter/Growth/Enterprise managed tier); pricing not fully published (Subscription + credits)
- **Positioning:** YC F25 self-improving ads engine covering the full lifecycle incl. ChatGPT-native ads (ChatSense) and self-optimizing landing pages (RapidDomain); 1,500+ teams, 30+ enterprises
- **Best for:** DTC and SaaS marketing teams (1,500+ teams; 30+ enterprises incl. Fortune 500)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where Lapis stops

Lapis' full-cycle paid execution with ChatGPT-ads distribution and self-improving creative is deeper ad tooling than Collision ships, at founder-friendly entry (assessment)

Capabilities Lapis doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, AEO, SEO, Outbound.

## The best Lapis alternatives

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities Lapis doesn't cover.

- **[AdCreative.ai](/alternatives/adcreative-ai)** — Predictive conversion scoring of creatives before launch, trained on performance data
- **[Adzooma](/alternatives/adzooma)** — Free AI PPC optimization across three ad networks with two-click implementation
- **[Madgicx](/alternatives/madgicx)** — First AI ad agent for Meta plus exclusive AI Bidding via Meta partnership
- **[Smartly.io](/alternatives/smartly-io)** — Only platform managing creative and media end-to-end for 700+ global brands at 300B creatives/year
- **[InstaAgent](/alternatives/instaagent)** — Hundreds of distinct audiences with distinct content from one brief — the anti-generic ad engine
- **[Sprites](/alternatives/sprites)** — Cross-channel ad execution (not just content) with ROAS/CPL goal optimization plus an SEO/AEO content engine - one agent

## Collision as a Lapis alternative

Overlaps Collision's paid-ads and content-creation capabilities: AI agents that produce ad creative, landing pages and run campaigns end-to-end for the same founder/SMB growth teams

Collision spans organic growth (SEO/AEO/GEO), outbound, LinkedIn and strategy; Lapis is paid-ads execution plus landing pages (assessment)

## Who should choose Lapis — and who shouldn't

Choose Lapis if: DTC and SaaS marketing teams (1,500+ teams; 30+ enterprises incl. Fortune 500) matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
