This page compares the realistic alternatives to Surfer (Positive) — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Surfer (Positive) does well

Content scoring, AI Overview/Gemini/ChatGPT visibility tracking, keyword research, SERP analyzer, AI writing, site audits.

## What Surfer (Positive) costs

- **Pricing:** Plans from ~$120/mo (annual tiers up to ~$720/mo) (Subscription)
- **Positioning:** Mature SEO data engine rebranded around AI-search visibility across engines.
- **Best for:** SMB to enterprise marketing/SEO teams
