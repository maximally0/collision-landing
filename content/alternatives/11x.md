# 11x alternatives

AI digital workers for GTM teams: Alice (outbound SDR), Julian (inbound), Jordan (voice) prospect, personalize outreach and book meetings.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to 11x — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What 11x does well

Real-time company/lead DB; live web research; AI personalization; event/nurture campaigns; phone agent; Slack integration; deep research

## What 11x costs

- **Pricing:** Custom (demo-based) (Subscription)
- **Positioning:** Named 'digital workers' (Alice/Julian/Jordan) sold as employees replacing roles, not tools augmenting them
- **Best for:** B2B sales/revenue teams, startups to enterprises

_Pricing verified 2026-08-14._

## Where 11x stops

Much deeper funding ($76M a16z/Benchmark), ~$10M ARR, category-defining brand in AI SDR.

Capabilities 11x doesn't cover that Collision does: Lead generation, GEO, Sales, LinkedIn growth, PR, SEO.

## The best 11x alternatives

- **[AiSDR](/alternatives/aisdr)** — Pure-play AI SDR (YC-backed) with strong booked-meeting performance claims and HubSpot-native workflow.
- **[Instantly](/alternatives/instantly)** — Owns cold-email deliverability infrastructure (unlimited accounts + warmup) and now ships AI SDR agents.
- **[Regie.ai](/alternatives/regie-ai)** — World's first AI-native sales engagement platform with patented agentic AI and 300% YoY ARR growth.
- **[Reply.io](/alternatives/reply-io)** — AI SDR agents (e.g., Jason AI) inside a mature multichannel engagement platform - automation-native, not bolted-on.
- **[Smartlead](/alternatives/smartlead)** — Deliverability-first cold email (unlimited mailboxes + AI warmup) that powers most outbound agencies and startups.
- **[Artisan](/alternatives/artisan)** — 'AI employees' positioning with autonomy levels (Level 2 sends outreach unassisted); 'Stop Hiring Humans' brand

## Collision as a 11x alternative

Owns the outbound SDR capability Collision offers and is expanding into lead gen, nurture, reactivation and expansion motions — a natural corridor into broader growth/marketing execution.

Collision bundles SEO/AEO/content/social with outbound for founders; 11x is sales-only. Assessment.

## Who should choose 11x — and who shouldn't

Choose 11x if: B2B sales/revenue teams, startups to enterprises matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
