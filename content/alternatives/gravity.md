# Gravity alternatives

Full-stack ad network for AI (founded 2025): demand platform for advertisers, supply platform for AI developers, and an exchange connecting both - placing sponsored, context-matched ads inside ChatGPT and other AI assistants; forward-looking agent-to-agent product feeds product catalogs to AI shopping agents.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Gravity — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Gravity does well

Millions of ads/day served (company-reported); CPM billing; first-party tracking pixels (no cross-site data sharing); placement in ChatGPT, Codebuff, EcoGPT, Daimon, Magneta, Runable, CTO.new; specialized 1B-param model cutting ad-serving p99 latency ~5.7x and inference cost ~10x (Inference.net case study)

## What Gravity costs

- **Pricing:** CPM (rates undisclosed) (Ad network / CPM)
- **Positioning:** Independent exchange connecting AI demand and supply with real-time auction and first-party measurement, plus first-mover positioning on agent-to-agent commerce (paid information entering agent recommendations, eventually approved transactions)
- **Best for:** Advertisers (Best Buy, Target, Vercel, MongoDB) and AI platform developers seeking monetization

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where Gravity stops

Gravity has early access to premium AI surfaces (ChatGPT and others), named enterprise advertisers (Best Buy, Target, Vercel, MongoDB), $30.5M in fresh capital and a first-mover position in AI-chat ad infrastructure (assessment)

Capabilities Gravity doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, AEO, SEO, Outbound.

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities Gravity doesn't cover.

- **[AdCreative.ai](/alternatives/adcreative-ai)** — Predictive conversion scoring of creatives before launch, trained on performance data

## Collision as a Gravity alternative

Overlaps with Collision's AEO/GEO wedge: Gravity owns the paid layer of AI-search/AI-chat visibility - sponsored placement inside the same AI answers Collision optimizes for organically - and its agent-to-agent product pursues the same 'get recommended by AI agents' outcome.

Collision delivers organic AEO/GEO execution that is channel-agnostic and does not depend on ad budgets or platform-supply deals; Gravity requires paid placement spend and supply-side relationships (assessment)

## Who should choose Gravity — and who shouldn't

Choose Gravity if: Advertisers (Best Buy, Target, Vercel, MongoDB) and AI platform developers seeking monetization matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
