# AutoBacklinks alternatives

AI agent for link building (launched Aug 26 2026, Berlin): discovers relevant sites from the user's website and competitors, filters by SEO metrics, finds and verifies contact emails, and sends personalized outreach sequences — replacing the manual Ahrefs+Hunter+Instantly stack.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to AutoBacklinks — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What AutoBacklinks does well

Six prospect scan types; AI relevance scoring and SEO filters (DR, referring domains, traffic); verified email finding; review-before-send or scheduled autopilot (weekly/biweekly/monthly); reply tracking and deal board

## What AutoBacklinks costs

- **Pricing:** Free trial; paid from $79/mo (agency/high-volume plans above) (Subscription + monthly credits)
- **Positioning:** First dedicated AI agent for the full link-building loop (vs link marketplaces or agency services)
- **Best for:** In-house SEO teams, agencies and founders running their own search growth

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where AutoBacklinks stops

Day-one product with no traction or funding; link-building agents face deliverability ceilings and quality-control limits that agencies solve with relationships (assessment)

Capabilities AutoBacklinks doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, AEO, SEO, Outbound.

## The best AutoBacklinks alternatives

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities AutoBacklinks doesn't cover.

- **[Outrank](/alternatives/outrank)** — Full SEO loop (research-write-publish) as one autopilot agent.
- **[SEO.AI](/alternatives/seo-ai)** — Automated link building via proprietary exchange network plus hands-off publishing.
- **[Surfer (Positive)](/alternatives/surfer-positive)** — Mature SEO data engine rebranded around AI-search visibility across engines.
