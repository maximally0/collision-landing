# Featured alternatives

AI co-pilot for PR and AI visibility (Scottsdale AZ): helps comms professionals and experts find journalist requests, podcasts, bylined articles, speaking opportunities and awards; owns and operates HARO and Connectively (100,000+ experts, 2,500+ publishers). Launched GEO Audit (Aug 2026) showing how brands appear in ChatGPT/Claude/Perplexity/Gemini answers and which publications to pitch to change them.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Featured — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Featured does well

GEO Audit (runs prompts across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini; shows brand appearance, missing prompts, competing brands and cited publications; ranks PR targets and drafts pitches); GEO visibility monitoring; journalist requests, podcasts, speaking, awards, bylined articles; 100,000+ expert network; 2,500+ publishers

## What Featured costs

- **Pricing:** Free tier (1 GEO audit/day); Lite from $29/mo, Pro from $79/mo (annual billing) (Freemium, usage-based)
- **Positioning:** Owns HARO + Connectively: the 'act' side of GEO (earned media) is native rather than bolted on - no other GEO vendor can pitch the publications shaping AI answers from inside the same product
- **Best for:** PR/communications/SEO teams; subject-matter experts and founders building AI visibility

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where Featured stops

Featured's HARO ownership gives it the largest journalist-request distribution in the industry - an earned-media path to shaping AI citations that Collision and GEO rivals cannot match (assessment)

Capabilities Featured doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, AEO, SEO, Outbound.
