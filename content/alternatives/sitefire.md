# Sitefire alternatives

Marketing suite for the agentic web (YC Winter 2026): helps brands get discovered and used by AI agents (ChatGPT, OpenClaw) - identifies which content drives AI visibility, automatically creates it, and executes on-page and off-page actions for measurable growth.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Sitefire — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Sitefire does well

AI-visibility content identification; automatic content creation; on-page and off-page execution; focus on measurable top-line growth; YC W26 batch

## What Sitefire costs

- **Pricing:** Undisclosed (SaaS subscription)
- **Positioning:** YC W26 positioning as "the marketing suite for the agentic web" - explicitly selling marketing-to-agents (ChatGPT, OpenClaw) rather than humans, the next wave of the AEO/GEO category
- **Best for:** Brands and startups wanting to be discovered and recommended by AI agents
