# Semrush alternatives

Online visibility management platform (SEO, content, advertising, social, competitive research) with native GEO (LLM Optimizer, AI Overview tracking). Acquired by Adobe for $1.9B (closed Apr 2026) as its brand-visibility engine for the agentic AI era. ~$443.6M revenue (2025).

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Semrush — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Semrush does well

25+ tools; LLM Optimizer and AI Overview/ChatGPT/Claude/Copilot/Grok/Perplexity tracking; position tracking; keyword/backlink/site audits; content marketing suite; social management; API; enterprise segment 33% YoY ARR growth

## What Semrush costs

- **Pricing:** Freemium; Pro from ~$139.95/mo; enterprise custom (Subscription)
- **Positioning:** Deepest SEO data estate plus native GEO tooling, now inside Adobe's marketing stack — data and distribution no standalone GEO vendor matches
- **Best for:** Marketing teams, SEO professionals, agencies, SMB to enterprise (Amazon, JPMorganChase, TikTok)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where Semrush stops

Semrush's data depth, ~1,600-person scale and Adobe's enterprise reach are unassailable for a startup; buyers default to it for SEO/GEO workflows (assessment)

Capabilities Semrush doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, AEO, Outbound, Advertising.
