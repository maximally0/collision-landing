# Treasure AI alternatives

Enterprise customer-data and agentic-experience platform (formerly Treasure Data, acquired by SoftBank 2018): Intelligent CDP plus AI Marketing Cloud; launched Marketing Super Agent (Jan 2026), a multi-agent orchestration system pitched as "the first enterprise AI that operates like a marketing department" - strategy, creative, activation and real-time optimization in one governed workspace.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Treasure AI — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

Super Agent Orchestrator; in-session memory layer; explainable reasoning chains; CDP-native audience activation; brand governance/guardrails; platform-agnostic martech integration; AI suites (Engagement, Personalization, Creative, Paid Media, Service); rebranded Treasure Data to Treasure AI
