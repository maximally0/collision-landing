# Zapier (Agents) alternatives

No-code automation platform (9,000+ apps) adding AI agents ('AI teammates') that plan, reason and execute multi-step work.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Zapier (Agents) — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Zapier (Agents) does well

Agents with tools (email, sheets, CRM); Chatbots; Canvas; Tables; 9,000+ integrations; MCP/SDK for AI coding agents

## What Zapier (Agents) costs

- **Pricing:** Free plan; paid from ~$19.99/mo; Agents on paid plans (Freemium)
- **Positioning:** Largest integration ecosystem plus massive SMB installed base entering agentic automation
- **Best for:** SMBs, marketers, ops teams

_Pricing verified 2026-08-13._

## Where Zapier (Agents) stops

Ubiquity, brand trust, ecosystem moat and fast agentic feature velocity.

Capabilities Zapier (Agents) doesn't cover that Collision does: Content, LinkedIn growth, Analytics, Personal branding, Automation, Outbound.

## The best Zapier (Agents) alternatives

- **[HubSpot Breeze](/alternatives/hubspot-breeze)** — Agents run natively on existing CRM data and workflows, distributed to HubSpot's large SMB install base
- **[Dygentic](/alternatives/dygentic)** — Agency-grade autonomy on open tooling (n8n) with full handover — clients own the system
- **[Make](/alternatives/make)** — Visual-first UX plus Celonis enterprise muscle; strong marketing-team adoption
- **[Brew](/alternatives/brew)** — Agent-native ESP (docs for OpenClaw/Claude/Lovable) with production-grade email rendering and no lock-in
- **[CharacterQuilt](/alternatives/characterquilt)** — Operates your existing stack instead of replacing it — 'sells the plumbing, not the chatbox'
- **[11x](/alternatives/11x)** — Named 'digital workers' (Alice/Julian/Jordan) sold as employees replacing roles, not tools augmenting them

## Collision as a Zapier (Agents) alternative

Marketing teams already use Zapier for lead routing and content ops; Agents can now execute marketing workflows end-to-end — a DIY substitute for Collision.

Collision executes specialized growth (SEO/AEO/GEO, content quality) vs Zapier's generic automation. Assessment.

## Who should choose Zapier (Agents) — and who shouldn't

Choose Zapier (Agents) if: SMBs, marketers, ops teams matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
