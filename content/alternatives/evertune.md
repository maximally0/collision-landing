# Evertune alternatives

GEO platform covering the full AI customer journey: statistically significant AI-visibility measurement (100x prompt sampling across 11+ models), consumer-intent panel, content activation, ChatGPT Ads and programmatic AI retargeting

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Evertune — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Evertune does well

100x prompt sampling per model for statistical significance; 11+ models (ChatGPT, ChatGPT Search, Gemini, AI Mode, AI Overviews, Meta AI, Claude, Copilot, Perplexity, DeepSeek); EverPanel 150M+ real consumer conversations; base-model vs consumer-app separation; ChatGPT Ads + programmatic AI retargeting; affiliate citation partners; source-level influence mapping

## What Evertune costs

- **Pricing:** Contact sales (enterprise) (Subscription (enterprise))
- **Positioning:** Only GEO platform combining rigorous 100x/sample measurement with closed-loop action (content workflows, ChatGPT Ads, affiliate citations, retargeting); founded by ex-The Trade Desk team
- **Best for:** Enterprise brands and advertisers

Capabilities Evertune doesn't cover that Collision does: Social media, Automation, SEO, Outbound, Advertising, Email.
