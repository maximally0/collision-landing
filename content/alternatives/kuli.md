# Kuli alternatives

AI co-worker for marketing teams at large consumer brands (YC Spring 2026): an agent that watches videos across social media, learns the brand context, and turns signals from billions of social videos into winning campaigns - creator scouting, trend/competitor analysis and campaign-performance analysis without adding headcount.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Kuli — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Kuli does well

Video-watching agent across social platforms; creator scouting and brand-safety checks; trend and competitor analysis; campaign performance analysis; free engagement/influencer pricing calculators; FR/EN site

## What Kuli costs

- **Pricing:** Contact (demo-based) (SaaS subscription)
- **Positioning:** The agent actually watches the videos (billions of social videos) rather than giving marketers a searchable database - YC-backed with enterprise consumer-brand logos
- **Best for:** Enterprise consumer brands and agencies (Disney, Microsoft, Supercell, PostHog, Armani Beauty, Havas Play, Monks, Roland Garros)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where Kuli stops

Kuli has YC backing, named enterprise logos (Disney, Microsoft, Supercell) and a differentiated video-watching agent loop - deeper creator-intelligence infrastructure than Collision offers (assessment)

Capabilities Kuli doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, AEO, SEO, Outbound.

## The best Kuli alternatives

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities Kuli doesn't cover.

- **[Buffer](/alternatives/buffer)** — Per-channel pricing and a genuinely free AI tier make it the default low-cost AI social tool for startups.
- **[FeedHive](/alternatives/feedhive)** — Workflow/condition-based automation plus AI agents inside a social scheduler.
- **[Hootsuite](/alternatives/hootsuite)** — OwlyGPT trained on real-time social trends plus 15+ years of social expertise and enterprise compliance tooling.
- **[Lately](/alternatives/lately)** — Statistical AI trained on a customer's own best posts predicts engaging words/ideas, not generic LLM copy.
- **[Postwise](/alternatives/postwise)** — Engagement-engineered AI trained on proven posts plus revenue tools (auto-DM, link plugging) inside a social scheduler.
- **[Predis.ai](/alternatives/predis-ai)** — Mass scale (6.4M users, 200M+ ads generated) and strong AI video-ad/UGC generation.

## Collision as a Kuli alternative

Overlaps Collision social-media and content capabilities: AI-driven creator/influencer campaign execution and social intelligence, aimed at enterprise consumer brands rather than founders

Collision serves founder-led B2B growth (SEO/AEO/GEO, outbound, LinkedIn, personal branding) and is channel-agnostic; Kuli is influencer/social-video intelligence for enterprise consumer teams (assessment)

## Who should choose Kuli — and who shouldn't

Choose Kuli if: Enterprise consumer brands and agencies (Disney, Microsoft, Supercell, PostHog, Armani Beauty, Havas Play, Monks, Roland Garros) matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
