# Revnu alternatives

AI growth agent (YC P26, launched May 2026): runs SEO, ads, outbound, social and A/B tests overnight on one shared intelligence - posts drafted in the founder's voice built to rank on Google and get cited by ChatGPT/Claude/Perplexity, ad variants scaled or killed automatically, and outbound that finds the right person, writes in your voice and books the call.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Revnu — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Revnu does well

Shared intelligence across channels (learnings from one channel improve all others); voice-matched content built to rank in Google + AI answers; parallel ad variants with winner scaling; automated outreach and meeting booking; iMessage/Slack delivery; YC P26 launch

## What Revnu costs

- **Pricing:** Not publicly listed (subscription)
- **Positioning:** Single-agent full-stack growth (SEO+ads+outbound+social on one shared intelligence) delivered as chat-native outcomes - closest 'AI growth hire' positioning to Collision's
- **Best for:** Founders and small teams who want a growth team without hiring one

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where Revnu stops

Capabilities Revnu doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Automation, AEO, Advertising, Email, LinkedIn growth.
