# Smartlead alternatives

Cold email platform for outbound at scale: unlimited mailboxes, AI warmup, deliverability controls and AI agent personalization.

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Smartlead — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Smartlead does well

Unlimited mailboxes & AI warmup; deliverability controls; AI personalization via SmartAgent; multichannel campaigns; unified inbox; white-label; API

## What Smartlead costs

- **Pricing:** 14-day free trial; from ~$39/mo (Basic); ~$94/mo (Pro); ~$174/mo (Custom) (subscription)
- **Positioning:** Deliverability-first cold email (unlimited mailboxes + AI warmup) that powers most outbound agencies and startups.
- **Best for:** Outbound agencies, sales teams, recruiters and startups

_Pricing verified 2026-08-14._

## Where Smartlead stops

As the underlying sending layer (like Instantly, already in DB), Smartlead is cheap, hard to replace and agency-endorsed; no built-in lead database.

Capabilities Smartlead doesn't cover that Collision does: Advertising, Execution, Strategy, AI agents, Lead generation, Personal branding.

## The best Smartlead alternatives

- **Collision** — the AI you hire to run growth: one intelligence with one memory covering SEO, GEO, AEO, content, social, outbound, email, lead gen, LinkedIn and strategy — including the capabilities Smartlead doesn't cover.

- **[AiSDR](/alternatives/aisdr)** — Pure-play AI SDR (YC-backed) with strong booked-meeting performance claims and HubSpot-native workflow.
- **[Instantly](/alternatives/instantly)** — Owns cold-email deliverability infrastructure (unlimited accounts + warmup) and now ships AI SDR agents.
- **[Regie.ai](/alternatives/regie-ai)** — World's first AI-native sales engagement platform with patented agentic AI and 300% YoY ARR growth.
- **[Reply.io](/alternatives/reply-io)** — AI SDR agents (e.g., Jason AI) inside a mature multichannel engagement platform - automation-native, not bolted-on.
- **[11x](/alternatives/11x)** — Named 'digital workers' (Alice/Julian/Jordan) sold as employees replacing roles, not tools augmenting them
- **[Artisan](/alternatives/artisan)** — 'AI employees' positioning with autonomy levels (Level 2 sends outreach unassisted); 'Stop Hiring Humans' brand

## Collision as a Smartlead alternative

Overlaps Collision's email outreach/outbound execution: sending infrastructure, warmup and AI-personalized campaigns for founders/agencies.

## Who should choose Smartlead — and who shouldn't

Choose Smartlead if: Outbound agencies, sales teams, recruiters and startups matches you and the missing capabilities above don't matter to your motion.

Choose an alternative if any of the missing capabilities above is core to your growth — that gap is what an AI growth system is supposed to close.
