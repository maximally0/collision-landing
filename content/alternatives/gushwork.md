# Gushwork alternatives

AI-search-era growth platform: 'AI Feeds' infrastructure identifies market topics, models what buyers ask AI engines (ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini/Perplexity), creates and publishes content that wins both Google and AI search, and tracks leads. $9M seed led by Susquehanna (Feb 2026).

This page compares the realistic alternatives to Gushwork — what the product actually does, what it costs, where it stops, and who each alternative is genuinely better for.

## What Gushwork does well

AI Feeds infrastructure; multi-engine coverage (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini); lead tracking from AI search; one-system loop: topic -> content -> publish -> leads

## What Gushwork costs

- **Pricing:** Unknown (subscription; enterprise quotes) (Subscription (assumed))
- **Positioning:** AI-search-driven lead acquisition (GEO as pipeline, not vanity citations) with enterprise backing (Susquehanna, Lightspeed, B Capital)
- **Best for:** B2B businesses (lean teams to enterprise) that want pipeline from AI search

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## Where Gushwork stops

Gushwork has $9M from Susquehanna/Lightspeed/B Capital and a productized AI-search lead-gen loop — a well-funded single-capability rival to Collision’s GEO wedge (assessment)

Capabilities Gushwork doesn't cover that Collision does: GEO, Social media, Automation, AEO, SEO, Outbound.
