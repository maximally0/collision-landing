Every AI growth tool eventually has to answer the same question: how much do you let it do on its own?

The tempting answer is "everything." Full autonomy, zero friction, content flowing to every channel without a human in the loop. It's also how you end up with an off-brand post live on your company LinkedIn for six hours before anyone notices.

## Speed without a checkpoint isn't leverage

We built Collision the other way. Every piece of writing, every campaign, every distribution decision surfaces for approval before it goes live. Not because we don't trust the system to be capable — because growth work compounds, and a single bad message sent at scale compounds too, just in the wrong direction.

Approval-based doesn't mean slow. Collision still does the research, drafts the copy, sequences the campaign, and prepares the send — the work that used to take a team days happens in the background. What changes is the last step: you see it, you approve it (or you don't), and only then does it ship.

## What this buys you

- **A brand voice that stays yours.** Collision learns how you write and think, but you're always the final filter before anything public happens.
- **A shorter feedback loop.** Approving or editing a draft teaches Collision faster than letting it publish and hoping the metrics tell the story later.
- **Fewer 2am surprises.** Nothing goes out that a human didn't see first.

The goal was never to remove humans from growth. It was to remove the busywork that keeps humans from doing the parts only they can do — deciding what's actually worth saying, and to whom.
