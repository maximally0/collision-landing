# Apollo.io vs Snov.io

**Apollo.io:** AI sales platform combining a large B2B contact database, engagement sequences, dialer and AI Assistant; free tier, used by 600K+ companies.

**Snov.io:** All-in-one lead gen and outreach toolkit: email finder/verifier, B2B database, drip campaigns, LinkedIn automation, AI email writer and CRM.

## At a glance

| | Apollo.io | Snov.io |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_LEAD_GENERATION | AI_LEAD_GENERATION |
| Pricing | Free plan; paid from ~$49/user/mo; custom enterprise | Free trial; Starter ~$29/mo up to Ultra ~$554/mo (25% off annual) |
| Best for | Founders, startups, SMBs and enterprise sales teams | Bootstrapped founders, SMBs and agencies |
| Differentiator | Free-tier data + engagement + AI in one stack; 600K+ companies and a replace-five-tools pitch. | One affordable toolkit spanning find, verify, warmup, send and CRM, with AI Studio and MCP integration. |

## What Apollo.io does

B2B database & search; enrichment; email/LinkedIn/call sequences; AI Assistant & AI research; Apollo MCP; workflow automation; Chrome extension; free plan

## What Snov.io does

Email finder/verifier; B2B database; multichannel drip campaigns; LinkedIn automation; AI writer + AI Studio; warmup; CRM; MCP for Claude/ChatGPT

## How to choose

Choose **Apollo.io** if: Founders, startups, SMBs and enterprise sales teams.

Choose **Snov.io** if: Bootstrapped founders, SMBs and agencies.
