# Adobe GenStudio vs Nexad

**Adobe GenStudio:** Generative AI app for performance marketing: on-brand copy/image/video variants at scale, activated to paid social/display/CTV with brand governance

**Nexad:** AI-native marketing company (founded 2024, San Francisco) whose flagship Soku AI is an autonomous marketing agent: connects to a company's ad accounts and website, then runs the growth loop itself - market research, ad creative generation/iteration, campaign launch and optimization across Google Ads, Meta, TikTok and ChatGPT Ads, plus SEO landing pages and AI-search visibility. Backed by a16z Speedrun, Point72 and Prosus ($6M seed, Apr 2025). Launched its AI-native marketing product line Aug 21 2026.

## At a glance

| | Adobe GenStudio | Nexad |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM |
| Pricing | Enterprise quote (Adobe Experience Cloud) | Free to start; credit-based plans (10k-100k credit increments, $3/1,000 top-ups); managed campaigns scoped to budget |
| Best for | Enterprise marketing teams (retail, ecommerce, financial services, telecom, CPG) | Founders, growth teams and agencies running paid ads (Google/Meta/TikTok/ChatGPT Ads) |

ChatGPT Ads) plus SEO/AI-search visibility, with graduated autonomy and a full audit trail - software that executes inside the ad platforms rather than another dashboard |

## What Adobe GenStudio does

Firefly/Azure OpenAI/Veo models; on-brand copy+image+video variants; activation to paid social/display/CTV; A/B testing; Workfront/AEM/Real-Time CDP integrations

## What Nexad does

Self-serve at soku.ai with credit pricing (1,000-credit increments, $3 per 1,000 top-ups); observe-only to full-autopilot autonomy levels; works inside existing ad accounts (client keeps pixels/data/learnings); Chatbot Ad Preview (ads in conversational assistants); Seedance 2.0 creative generation; managed campaign scoping

## How to choose

Choose **Adobe GenStudio** if: Enterprise marketing teams (retail, ecommerce, financial services, telecom, CPG).

Choose **Nexad** if: Founders, growth teams and agencies running paid ads (Google/Meta/TikTok/ChatGPT Ads).
