# Collision vs Buffer

Social media scheduling and publishing platform with built-in AI Assistant (OpenAI) for content ideas, drafting and repurposing; per-channel pricing.

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where Buffer genuinely wins

Buffer's brand and price ($0-12/mo) make it a reflexive default for founders; its API explicitly invites agent workflows, commoditizing distribution.

## Where Collision genuinely wins

assessment - Collision adds strategy, SEO/AEO and outbound; Buffer is publishing infrastructure, so Collision can position as the brain above Buffer

## Pricing reality

- **Buffer:** Free; Essentials $6/channel/mo; Team $12/channel/mo (subscription (per channel))
- **Collision:** Launch $500–1,000/mo · Growth $2,000–6,000/mo · Scale $8,000–15,000/mo (pre-launch hypothesis pricing)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-14. Collision pricing is pre-launch positioning, not market-validated._

## How to decide

Choose **Buffer** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Buffer doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
