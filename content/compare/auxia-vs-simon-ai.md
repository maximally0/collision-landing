# Auxia vs Simon AI

**Auxia:** Agentic marketing platform for enterprise B2C lifecycle teams: Auxia Decisioning personalizes/orchestrates every customer interaction in real time (200B+ decisions delivered, 500M+/day), and Auxia Agent Studio is a command center where AI agents plan, build and launch campaigns across the team's existing ESP/CDP stack

**Simon AI:** Agentic marketing platform (ex-Simon Data): marketers set goals, agents turn live customer + real-world signals into adaptive campaigns

## At a glance

| | Auxia | Simon AI |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM |
| Pricing | Enterprise (demo/early-access waitlist) | Enterprise (contact sales) |
| Best for | Enterprise B2C lifecycle marketing teams (Fortune 500) | Mid-market to enterprise brands (ASOS, SeatGeek, Travel + Leisure, Equinox) |
| Differentiator | Proven decisioning scale (200B+ decisions; customers Atlassian, Comcast, Docomo) plus a new agentic execution layer - enterprise-grade autonomous marketing backed by real personalization data | AI-first composable CDP + agents = enterprise-scale data-driven personalization, not content generation |

## What Auxia does

500M+ decisions/day; marketing context graph (brand voice, campaign history); natural-language agentic workflows across ESPs/CDPs; collaborative approval workspace; localized campaign generation at global scale; model-driven experimentation replacing manual A/B testing

## What Simon AI does

Personalization Studio; AI Fields & AI Moments; Insight Agents; Blueprints; adaptive segments; composable CDP on Snowflake; weather/trend/inventory triggers

## How to choose

Choose **Auxia** if: Enterprise B2C lifecycle marketing teams (Fortune 500).

Choose **Simon AI** if: Mid-market to enterprise brands (ASOS, SeatGeek, Travel + Leisure, Equinox).
