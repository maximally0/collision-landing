# Collision vs Auxia

Agentic marketing platform for enterprise B2C lifecycle teams: Auxia Decisioning personalizes/orchestrates every customer interaction in real time (200B+ decisions delivered, 500M+/day), and Auxia Agent Studio is a command center where AI agents plan, build and launch campaigns across the team's existing ESP/CDP stack

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where Auxia genuinely wins

Auxia has enterprise logos, VMG-led capital and 500M decisions/day infrastructure - execution scale and trust far beyond Collision (assessment)

## Where Collision genuinely wins

Collision is stronger on founder-centric SEO/AEO/GEO, outbound, personal branding and LinkedIn growth; Auxia targets enterprise lifecycle personalization (assessment)

## Pricing reality

- **Auxia:** Enterprise (demo/early-access waitlist) (Enterprise subscription)
- **Collision:** Launch $500–1,000/mo · Growth $2,000–6,000/mo · Scale $8,000–15,000/mo

## How to decide

Choose **Auxia** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Auxia doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
