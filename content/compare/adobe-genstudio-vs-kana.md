# Adobe GenStudio vs Kana

**Adobe GenStudio:** Generative AI app for performance marketing: on-brand copy/image/video variants at scale, activated to paid social/display/CTV with brand governance

**Kana:** Agentic marketing platform from Rapt/Krux founders: flexible AI agents for data analysis, audience targeting, campaign orchestration, customer engagement, media planning and AI-chatbot (LLM) optimization. $15M seed led by Mayfield (Feb 2026).

## At a glance

| | Adobe GenStudio | Kana |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM |
| Pricing | Enterprise quote (Adobe Experience Cloud) | Enterprise (contact) |
| Best for | Enterprise marketing teams (retail, ecommerce, financial services, telecom, CPG) | Enterprise marketing teams and publishers |
| Differentiator | Creative-production scale + brand governance + Adobe ecosystem (CDP, AEM, Workfront) — not autonomous strategy | Founders with 26 years of martech pedigree (Rapt->Microsoft, Krux->Salesforce $1B, super{set}->Habu/LiveRamp $200M exit); enterprise-grade agentic platform incl. LLM Command Center for AI-search presence |

## What Adobe GenStudio does

Firefly/Azure OpenAI/Veo models; on-brand copy+image+video variants; activation to paid social/display/CTV; A/B testing; Workfront/AEM/Real-Time CDP integrations

## What Kana does

10 solution areas incl. LLM Command Center for AI-search/AI-chatbot visibility; Kana for Marketers + Kana for Publishers suites; agentic data platform; synthetic data generation; published research: 70% of enterprises already run AI agents in marketing (Jul 2026)

## How to choose

Choose **Adobe GenStudio** if: Enterprise marketing teams (retail, ecommerce, financial services, telecom, CPG).

Choose **Kana** if: Enterprise marketing teams and publishers.
