# Jasper vs Simon AI

**Jasper:** AI marketing platform (ex-AI copywriter): purpose-built agents execute end-to-end workflows - campaigns, GEO/SEO/AEO optimization, content pipelines

**Simon AI:** Agentic marketing platform (ex-Simon Data): marketers set goals, agents turn live customer + real-world signals into adaptive campaigns

## At a glance

| | Jasper | Simon AI |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM |
| Pricing | Tiered subscription; free trial (Creator/Pro/Business plans) | Enterprise (contact sales) |
| Best for | Marketing teams, SMB to enterprise | Mid-market to enterprise brands (ASOS, SeatGeek, Travel + Leisure, Equinox) |
| Differentiator | Only major AI marketing platform with native GEO + SEO/AEO + agents + brand governance in one suite | AI-first composable CDP + agents = enterprise-scale data-driven personalization, not content generation |

## What Jasper does

Agents library (product launch, social campaigns); GEO citation monitoring; SEO & AEO content; Brand Voice/Style Guide governance; Knowledge Base grounding; APIs/MCP; Semrush integration

## What Simon AI does

Personalization Studio; AI Fields & AI Moments; Insight Agents; Blueprints; adaptive segments; composable CDP on Snowflake; weather/trend/inventory triggers

## How to choose

Choose **Jasper** if: Marketing teams, SMB to enterprise.

Choose **Simon AI** if: Mid-market to enterprise brands (ASOS, SeatGeek, Travel + Leisure, Equinox).
