# AiSDR vs Reply.io

**AiSDR:** AI SDR agent that finds prospects in real time, writes personalized email/LinkedIn/text outreach and books qualified meetings that show up.

**Reply.io:** AI sales outreach platform: multichannel sequences, email/LinkedIn automation, 1B+ contact data and AI SDR agents that run prospecting.

## At a glance

| | AiSDR | Reply.io |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_OUTBOUND | AI_OUTBOUND |
| Pricing | From ~$250/mo; flat plans up to ~$900/mo | Free trial; from ~$60/user/mo |
| Best for | B2B SaaS growth teams (50-300 employees, $2M+ ARR) | Startups, growth teams and agencies running outbound |
| Differentiator | Pure-play AI SDR (YC-backed) with strong booked-meeting performance claims and HubSpot-native workflow. | AI SDR agents (e.g., Jason AI) inside a mature multichannel engagement platform - automation-native, not bolted-on. |

## What AiSDR does

AI prospecting and qualification, personalized emails/LinkedIn/texts, campaign planning, performance monitoring, inbound handling, CRM sync.

## What Reply.io does

Multichannel sequences; AI SDR agents; audience suggestions; email/LinkedIn automation; Findy Chrome extension; data enrichment; deliverability tools

## How to choose

Choose **AiSDR** if: B2B SaaS growth teams (50-300 employees, $2M+ ARR).

Choose **Reply.io** if: Startups, growth teams and agencies running outbound.
