# Collision vs Uplane

Full-funnel AI marketing automation platform (YC Fall 2025): market intelligence, high-velocity personalized ad + landing-page production, automated A/B testing, algorithmic campaign management and real-time analytics; positioned to replace marketing agencies.

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where Uplane genuinely wins

Uplane's end-to-end paid funnel with algorithmic budget steering and enterprise deployment (ISO27001, on-prem) is deeper paid execution than Collision ships, with a direct 'replace agencies' message to the same buyer (assessment)

## Where Collision genuinely wins

Collision adds organic growth (SEO/AEO/GEO, content, outbound, LinkedIn, personal branding) and founder-priced self-serve; Uplane is paid-ads/landing-page centric (assessment)

## Pricing reality

- **Uplane:** Not publicly listed (SaaS subscription)
- **Collision:** Launch $500–1,000/mo · Growth $2,000–6,000/mo · Scale $8,000–15,000/mo

## How to decide

Choose **Uplane** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Uplane doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
