# Collision vs Wayy.ai

AI co-founder that sells for you: autonomous sales workflow for solopreneurs/SMBs — analyzes the company and LinkedIn profile, identifies prospects via a multi-level scoring/qualification engine, drafts personalized outreach, requests approval before contact, and self-refines targeting every 3 days.

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where Wayy.ai genuinely wins

Wayy's $49 entry price, solopreneur targeting and self-refining outreach loop are a cheap, focused alternative for Collision's outbound-only buyers (assessment).

## Where Collision genuinely wins

Collision bundles outbound with SEO/AEO/GEO, content, LinkedIn and strategy; Wayy is sales-outreach-only (assessment).

- **Wayy.ai:** $49/$99/$149 per month (Subscription)

## How to decide

Choose **Wayy.ai** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Wayy.ai doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
