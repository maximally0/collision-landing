# Adobe GenStudio vs Persado

**Adobe GenStudio:** Generative AI app for performance marketing: on-brand copy/image/video variants at scale, activated to paid social/display/CTV with brand governance

**Persado:** AI message-generation platform for regulated brands: generates, scores, compliance-checks and deploys campaign copy across email/SMS/push/ads

## At a glance

| | Adobe GenStudio | Persado |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM |
| Pricing | Enterprise quote (Adobe Experience Cloud) | Enterprise (demo/quote) |
| Best for | Enterprise marketing teams (retail, ecommerce, financial services, telecom, CPG) | Enterprise regulated brands (banks, insurers, telecoms, retail) |
| Differentiator | Creative-production scale + brand governance + Adobe ecosystem (CDP, AEM, Workfront) — not autonomous strategy | Domain-specific motivation AI plus compliance engine for regulated verticals — clears UDAAP/TILA checks out of the box |

## What Adobe GenStudio does

Firefly/Azure OpenAI/Veo models; on-brand copy+image+video variants; activation to paid social/display/CTV; A/B testing; Workfront/AEM/Real-Time CDP integrations

## What Persado does

Brief-Generate-Score-Comply-Deploy-Learn loop; performance score vs 1M+ A/B tests; regulatory checks (UDAAP, TILA, CAN-SPAM); deploys to Salesforce MC, Braze, Adobe Target

## How to choose

Choose **Adobe GenStudio** if: Enterprise marketing teams (retail, ecommerce, financial services, telecom, CPG).

Choose **Persado** if: Enterprise regulated brands (banks, insurers, telecoms, retail).
