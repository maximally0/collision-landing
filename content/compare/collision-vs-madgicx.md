# Collision vs Madgicx

Agentic Meta ads management platform: AI Marketer agent audits accounts, auto-reallocates budgets, generates creatives and automates campaigns.

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where Madgicx genuinely wins

Meta-only (no Google/TikTok/LinkedIn); some optimizations remain insights-driven; DTC/ads-specific with no content strategy, PR or lead-gen breadth

## Where Collision genuinely wins

assessment: Collision covers cross-channel (not Meta-only) and adds SEO/PR/outbound layers Madgicx does not

## Pricing reality

- **Madgicx:** ~$49-$499+/mo tiered by ad spend (Subscription by ad spend tier)
- **Collision:** Launch $500–1,000/mo · Growth $2,000–6,000/mo · Scale $8,000–15,000/mo (pre-launch hypothesis pricing)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-13. Collision pricing is pre-launch positioning, not market-validated._

## How to decide

Choose **Madgicx** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Madgicx doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
