# Adobe GenStudio vs Hyper AI

**Adobe GenStudio:** Generative AI app for performance marketing: on-brand copy/image/video variants at scale, activated to paid social/display/CTV with brand governance

**Hyper AI:** AI-native marketing agent platform: CMO, paid-ads, social, SEO/AI-search and analytics agents that connect 200+ tools and run 24/7, with multi-client agency workspaces.

## At a glance

| | Adobe GenStudio | Hyper AI |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM |
| Pricing | Enterprise quote (Adobe Experience Cloud) | Free tier ($10 credits); paid plans (tiers unverified) |
| Best for | Enterprise marketing teams (retail, ecommerce, financial services, telecom, CPG) | Marketing teams, founders and agencies |
| Differentiator | Creative-production scale + brand governance + Adobe ecosystem (CDP, AEM, Workfront) — not autonomous strategy | Prebuilt expert agents (not DIY automations) with deep ad-platform API execution and multi-client agency mode |

## What Adobe GenStudio does

Firefly/Azure OpenAI/Veo models; on-brand copy+image+video variants; activation to paid social/display/CTV; A/B testing; Workfront/AEM/Real-Time CDP integrations

## What Hyper AI does

80+ ad/marketing connectors; hourly/daily/weekly recurring tasks; brand voice + positioning context; multi-client agency mode; MCP for external agents; $10 free credits

## How to choose

Choose **Adobe GenStudio** if: Enterprise marketing teams (retail, ecommerce, financial services, telecom, CPG).

Choose **Hyper AI** if: Marketing teams, founders and agencies.
