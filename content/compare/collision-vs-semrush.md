# Collision vs Semrush

Online visibility management platform (SEO, content, advertising, social, competitive research) with native GEO (LLM Optimizer, AI Overview tracking). Acquired by Adobe for $1.9B (closed Apr 2026) as its brand-visibility engine for the agentic AI era. ~$443.6M revenue (2025).

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where Semrush genuinely wins

Semrush's data depth, ~1,600-person scale and Adobe's enterprise reach are unassailable for a startup; buyers default to it for SEO/GEO workflows (assessment)

## Where Collision genuinely wins

Collision executes autonomously (agents producing and distributing growth work); Semrush is a user-operated toolkit, not an executor (assessment)

## Pricing reality

- **Semrush:** Freemium; Pro from ~$139.95/mo; enterprise custom (Subscription)
- **Collision:** Launch $500–1,000/mo · Growth $2,000–6,000/mo · Scale $8,000–15,000/mo

_Pricing verified 2026-08-14._

## How to decide

Choose **Semrush** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Semrush doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
