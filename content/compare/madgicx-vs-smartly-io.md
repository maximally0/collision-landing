# Madgicx vs Smartly.io

**Madgicx:** Agentic Meta ads management platform: AI Marketer agent audits accounts, auto-reallocates budgets, generates creatives and automates campaigns.

**Smartly.io:** AI advertising platform unifying creative production, media buying and intelligence across Meta, TikTok, Snap, Pinterest, Google and CTV.

## At a glance

| | Madgicx | Smartly.io |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_ADVERTISING | AI_ADVERTISING |
| Pricing | ~$49-$499+/mo tiered by ad spend | Custom enterprise (~$5K+/mo) |
| Best for | DTC ecommerce brands, agencies and media buyers spending $5K+/mo on Meta | Enterprise brands and performance teams (700+ brands, $5B ad spend) |
| Differentiator | First AI ad agent for Meta plus exclusive AI Bidding via Meta partnership | Only platform managing creative and media end-to-end for 700+ global brands at 300B creatives/year |

## What Madgicx does

Daily AI account audits; autonomous budget reallocation across ad sets; AI creative generation with automated launch; real-time protection rules; first-party server tracking

## What Smartly.io does

AI creative production at scale (300B creatives/yr); automated campaign wizardry; cross-platform orchestration; real-time optimization; Brand Pulse measurement

## How to choose

Choose **Madgicx** if: DTC ecommerce brands, agencies and media buyers spending $5K+/mo on Meta.

Choose **Smartly.io** if: Enterprise brands and performance teams (700+ brands, $5B ad spend).
