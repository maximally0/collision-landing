# Alta vs Instantly

**Alta:** AI GTM system of actions: self-optimizing agents execute the full top-of-funnel (prospecting, personalized multi-channel outreach, inbound qualification, AI calling, ad audience sync, pipeline analytics) from first signal to booked meeting

**Instantly:** Sales engagement + lead intelligence: AI sales/reply/lead-finder agents, lead database, email warmup/deliverability, outreach and CRM.

## At a glance

| | Alta | Instantly |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_OUTBOUND | AI_OUTBOUND |
| Pricing | Contact sales (not public) | From $47/mo (tiers to ~$358/mo) |
| Best for | Mid-market and enterprise GTM teams | Founders, SMBs and agencies running cold email/outbound |
| Differentiator | Full top-of-funnel coverage (outbound + inbound + calling + paid alignment + pipeline intelligence) as one self-optimizing system, with enterprise trust certs and logos (Snowflake, Deel, Riverside) | Owns cold-email deliverability infrastructure (unlimited accounts + warmup) and now ships AI SDR agents. |

## What Alta does

Self-optimizing agents; personalized email/LinkedIn/call outreach; human-quality AI conversations; automatic ad audience sync to Google Ads/Meta; 50+ data source integrations; Slack + CRM integrations; SOC 2 + ISO certified

## What Instantly does

AI agents (sales/reply/lead-gen), lead database, email verification, warmup, deliverability, outreach automations, website visitor ID.

## How to choose

Choose **Alta** if: Mid-market and enterprise GTM teams.

Choose **Instantly** if: Founders, SMBs and agencies running cold email/outbound.
