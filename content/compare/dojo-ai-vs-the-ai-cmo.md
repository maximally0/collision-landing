# DOJO AI vs The AI CMO

**DOJO AI:** Intelligent marketing system for challenger brands: a living knowledge graph (DOJO Graph) models the brand's marketing reality; specialized AI agents act on it across paid, organic, SEO and content, feeding every outcome back into the model.

**The AI CMO:** Agentic marketing OS by ROGA AI Ltd: AI specialists plan, produce and send campaigns (email, ads, SMS, push) with approval queue, CDW and built-in SEO/link-building

## At a glance

| | DOJO AI | The AI CMO |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_MARKETING_OS | AI_MARKETING_OS |
| Pricing | From $499/mo; 7-day free trial; Starter/Growth/Enterprise tiers | From $299/mo; full platform on every plan |
| Best for | Challenger brands and mid-market marketing teams (CoinDesk, MorningStar, PensionBee, Broadvoice, Refine Labs) | Founders, solo marketers, SMBs, agencies |
| Differentiator | Knowledge-graph-first marketing intelligence where agents execute (not just report) and every outcome feeds back into the model — 'most AI resets, DOJO compounds' | Append-only audit ledger, structural suppression and dedicated warehouse = enterprise trust in an agentic marketing OS |

## What DOJO AI does

DOJO Graph living knowledge graph; proactive agentic workflows (not prompt-driven); brand/demand/creative intelligence; unlimited competitive monitoring; revenue intelligence; queryable dashboards; 20% MoM growth; 100+ brands

## What The AI CMO does

10k+ campaigns/mo; customer 360 + self-filling data warehouse; studios (ads, email, pages, video); behavior journeys; append-only record; structural suppression; 600+ connectors

## How to choose

Choose **DOJO AI** if: Challenger brands and mid-market marketing teams (CoinDesk, MorningStar, PensionBee, Broadvoice, Refine Labs).

Choose **The AI CMO** if: Founders, solo marketers, SMBs, agencies.
