# Ahrefs Letaido vs Ploy

**Ahrefs Letaido:** AI marketing agent workspace built by Ahrefs (bootstrapped SEO data incumbent): builds and runs marketing tools, reports, dashboards, scheduled automations and apps with unrestricted access to Ahrefs data endpoints. Launched Aug 12 2026.

**Ploy:** AI marketing platform that turns a company's website into its growth engine: swarms of marketing and web-design agents design pages, write copy, launch campaigns (ABM, SEO, paid ads) and optimize, with built-in AEO. YC P26; $27M seed (Jun 2026). Founded by ex-Webflow co-founder/CTO Bryant Chou.

## At a glance

| | Ahrefs Letaido | Ploy |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_SEO | AI_SEO |
| Pricing | $99/mo flat incl $50 AI credits (top-ups as used); separate Ahrefs plan required for full data access | Free; Starter $50/mo; Pro/Enterprise (usage credits) |
| Best for | Marketing teams, SEO/content/growth teams, agencies, freelancers, ecommerce, SaaS | Founders and startups (13%+ of YC P26 batch), SaaS teams (Hex, Clay), small teams |
| Differentiator | Only agent with unrestricted internal Ahrefs endpoint access (data beyond the public API/MCP, no API-unit cost) plus purpose-built Ahrefs expertise and a durable cloud workspace | Founder pedigree (ex-Webflow CTO) + agent swarms operating on the website itself with AEO built in; $27M seed before YC demo day |

## What Ahrefs Letaido does

$99/mo incl $50 AI credits (75% passes through to model providers); 300+ models (Claude/GPT/Gemini); pre-built marketing skills maintained by Ahrefs; 30+ connectors (Slack, HubSpot, Notion, WordPress, Semrush, Mailchimp, Stripe, Gong); scheduled agent runs/webhooks/triggers; persistent workspace + DB + Console + public site; zero Ahrefs API-unit consumption; unlimited team seats

## What Ploy does

Site slurp (design system/components/brand content); PloyBooks strategy templates; ABM page generation; programmatic SEO engines; AEO; visitor identification/enrichment; ad integrations; custom domains; GitHub code sync; free tier (800 credits/day)

## How to choose

Choose **Ahrefs Letaido** if: Marketing teams, SEO/content/growth teams, agencies, freelancers, ecommerce, SaaS.

Choose **Ploy** if: Founders and startups (13%+ of YC P26 batch), SaaS teams (Hex, Clay), small teams.
