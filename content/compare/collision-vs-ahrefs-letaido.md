# Collision vs Ahrefs Letaido

AI marketing agent workspace built by Ahrefs (bootstrapped SEO data incumbent): builds and runs marketing tools, reports, dashboards, scheduled automations and apps with unrestricted access to Ahrefs data endpoints. Launched Aug 12 2026.

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where Ahrefs Letaido genuinely wins

Ahrefs' data moat, brand trust and huge SEO customer base make Letaido the default AI-agent layer for SEO work - Collision's SEO wedge now competes with an incumbent's stand-alone agent (assessment).

## Where Collision genuinely wins

Collision is strategy-led full-funnel growth (outbound, LinkedIn, personal branding, lead gen, distribution); Letaido is a research/reporting/automation workspace that does not execute multi-channel growth campaigns (assessment).

## Pricing reality

- **Ahrefs Letaido:** $99/mo flat incl $50 AI credits (top-ups as used); separate Ahrefs plan required for full data access (Subscription (flat + usage credits))
- **Collision:** Launch $500–1,000/mo · Growth $2,000–6,000/mo · Scale $8,000–15,000/mo

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## How to decide

Choose **Ahrefs Letaido** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Ahrefs Letaido doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
