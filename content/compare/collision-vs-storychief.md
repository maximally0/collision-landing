# Collision vs StoryChief

Content marketing platform combining AI writing, multi-channel distribution and publishing automation from one workspace.

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

| Capability | Collision | StoryChief |

| Content | Yes | No |
| Social | Yes | No |

| Lead generation | Yes | No |
| One memory across surfaces | Yes | Varies |
| Human approval on every action | Yes | Varies |

## Where StoryChief genuinely wins

No autonomous agents - AI assist and scheduling, not execution; shallow SEO depth vs MarketMuse; small team

## Where Collision genuinely wins

assessment: Collision adds autonomous agents and growth execution (SEO, PR, outbound) around distribution

## Pricing reality

- **StoryChief:** Free; paid from EUR 19/mo (Freemium SaaS)
- **Collision:** Launch $500–1,000/mo · Growth $2,000–6,000/mo · Scale $8,000–15,000/mo (pre-launch hypothesis pricing)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-13. Collision pricing is pre-launch positioning, not market-validated._

## How to decide

Choose **StoryChief** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts StoryChief doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
