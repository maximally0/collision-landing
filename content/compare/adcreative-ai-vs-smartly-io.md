# AdCreative.ai vs Smartly.io

**AdCreative.ai:** AI ad creative generator producing high-converting banners, video ads and copy with predictive conversion scoring, wired into ad platforms.

**Smartly.io:** AI advertising platform unifying creative production, media buying and intelligence across Meta, TikTok, Snap, Pinterest, Google and CTV.

## At a glance

| | AdCreative.ai | Smartly.io |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_ADVERTISING | AI_ADVERTISING |
| Pricing | $39-$999/mo (Starter/Professional/Ultimate) | Custom enterprise (~$5K+/mo) |
| Best for | Founders, startups, SMBs and agencies running paid ads | Enterprise brands and performance teams (700+ brands, $5B ad spend) |
| Differentiator | Predictive conversion scoring of creatives before launch, trained on performance data | Only platform managing creative and media end-to-end for 700+ global brands at 300B creatives/year |

## What AdCreative.ai does

Banner/video ad generation; AI copy; pre-launch conversion score prediction; brand kit training; 20+ platform formats; creative refresh suggestions

## What Smartly.io does

AI creative production at scale (300B creatives/yr); automated campaign wizardry; cross-platform orchestration; real-time optimization; Brand Pulse measurement

## How to choose

Choose **AdCreative.ai** if: Founders, startups, SMBs and agencies running paid ads.

Choose **Smartly.io** if: Enterprise brands and performance teams (700+ brands, $5B ad spend).
