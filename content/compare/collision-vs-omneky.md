# Collision vs Omneky

Autonomous AI advertising platform that writes, designs, launches and optimizes ads across Meta, Google, LinkedIn, TikTok and Reddit — now including ChatGPT (OpenAI self-serve Ads Manager). Manages $1B+ annualized ad spend.

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where Omneky genuinely wins

Omneky's 8-year head start, $1B+ managed ad spend and ChatGPT-ad distribution reach are a deep moat in paid media Collision doesn't serve (assessment).

## Where Collision genuinely wins

Collision covers organic growth (SEO/AEO/GEO), outbound, LinkedIn and strategy; Omneky is ads-execution-only (assessment).

## Pricing reality

- **Omneky:** 7-day free trial; plans not published (contact) (Subscription)
- **Collision:** Launch $500–1,000/mo · Growth $2,000–6,000/mo · Scale $8,000–15,000/mo

## How to decide

Choose **Omneky** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Omneky doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
