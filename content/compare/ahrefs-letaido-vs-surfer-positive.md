# Ahrefs Letaido vs Surfer (Positive)

**Ahrefs Letaido:** AI marketing agent workspace built by Ahrefs (bootstrapped SEO data incumbent): builds and runs marketing tools, reports, dashboards, scheduled automations and apps with unrestricted access to Ahrefs data endpoints. Launched Aug 12 2026.

**Surfer (Positive):** AI visibility platform (formerly Surfer SEO, under Positive Group) for ranking in Google, AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity.

## At a glance

| | Ahrefs Letaido | Surfer (Positive) |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_SEO | AI_SEO |
| Pricing | $99/mo flat incl $50 AI credits (top-ups as used); separate Ahrefs plan required for full data access | Plans from ~$120/mo (annual tiers up to ~$720/mo) |
| Best for | Marketing teams, SEO/content/growth teams, agencies, freelancers, ecommerce, SaaS | SMB to enterprise marketing/SEO teams |
| Differentiator | Only agent with unrestricted internal Ahrefs endpoint access (data beyond the public API/MCP, no API-unit cost) plus purpose-built Ahrefs expertise and a durable cloud workspace | Mature SEO data engine rebranded around AI-search visibility across engines. |

## What Ahrefs Letaido does

$99/mo incl $50 AI credits (75% passes through to model providers); 300+ models (Claude/GPT/Gemini); pre-built marketing skills maintained by Ahrefs; 30+ connectors (Slack, HubSpot, Notion, WordPress, Semrush, Mailchimp, Stripe, Gong); scheduled agent runs/webhooks/triggers; persistent workspace + DB + Console + public site; zero Ahrefs API-unit consumption; unlimited team seats

## What Surfer (Positive) does

Content scoring, AI Overview/Gemini/ChatGPT visibility tracking, keyword research, SERP analyzer, AI writing, site audits.

## How to choose

Choose **Ahrefs Letaido** if: Marketing teams, SEO/content/growth teams, agencies, freelancers, ecommerce, SaaS.

Choose **Surfer (Positive)** if: SMB to enterprise marketing/SEO teams.
