# Collision vs Manus

General autonomous AI agent ('hands-on AI'): browses, researches, builds websites/slides, writes reports and executes multi-step tasks.

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where Manus genuinely wins

Huge brand awareness, deep pockets, general-task autonomy improving quickly.

## Where Collision genuinely wins

Collision is purpose-built for growth workflows with platform knowledge; Manus is generic and inconsistent. Assessment.

- **Manus:** $19-$199/mo plans with credits (8k-40k credits) (Subscription)
- **Collision:** Launch $500–1,000/mo · Growth $2,000–6,000/mo · Scale $8,000–15,000/mo (pre-launch hypothesis pricing)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-14. Collision pricing is pre-launch positioning, not market-validated._

## How to decide

Choose **Manus** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Manus doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
