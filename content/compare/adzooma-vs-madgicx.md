# Adzooma vs Madgicx

**Adzooma:** AI-powered PPC platform that audits, optimizes and scales Google, Microsoft and Meta campaigns with automated, one-click recommendations.

**Madgicx:** Agentic Meta ads management platform: AI Marketer agent audits accounts, auto-reallocates budgets, generates creatives and automates campaigns.

## At a glance

| | Adzooma | Madgicx |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_ADVERTISING | AI_ADVERTISING |
| Pricing | Free; Pro from ~£69/mo | ~$49-$499+/mo tiered by ad spend |
| Best for | SMBs, agencies, freelancers and founders running paid ads | DTC ecommerce brands, agencies and media buyers spending $5K+/mo on Meta |
| Differentiator | Free AI PPC optimization across three ad networks with two-click implementation | First AI ad agent for Meta plus exclusive AI Bidding via Meta partnership |

## What Adzooma does

24/7 machine-learning analysis; one-click recommendation application; performance scorecards; automated alerts; AI ad copy; multi-channel reporting

## What Madgicx does

Daily AI account audits; autonomous budget reallocation across ad sets; AI creative generation with automated launch; real-time protection rules; first-party server tracking

## How to choose

Choose **Adzooma** if: SMBs, agencies, freelancers and founders running paid ads.

Choose **Madgicx** if: DTC ecommerce brands, agencies and media buyers spending $5K+/mo on Meta.
