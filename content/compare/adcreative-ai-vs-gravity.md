# AdCreative.ai vs Gravity

**AdCreative.ai:** AI ad creative generator producing high-converting banners, video ads and copy with predictive conversion scoring, wired into ad platforms.

**Gravity:** Full-stack ad network for AI (founded 2025): demand platform for advertisers, supply platform for AI developers, and an exchange connecting both - placing sponsored, context-matched ads inside ChatGPT and other AI assistants; forward-looking agent-to-agent product feeds product catalogs to AI shopping agents.

## At a glance

| | AdCreative.ai | Gravity |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_ADVERTISING | AI_ADVERTISING |
| Pricing | $39-$999/mo (Starter/Professional/Ultimate) | CPM (rates undisclosed) |
| Best for | Founders, startups, SMBs and agencies running paid ads | Advertisers (Best Buy, Target, Vercel, MongoDB) and AI platform developers seeking monetization |
| Differentiator | Predictive conversion scoring of creatives before launch, trained on performance data | Independent exchange connecting AI demand and supply with real-time auction and first-party measurement, plus first-mover positioning on agent-to-agent commerce (paid information entering agent recommendations, eventually approved transactions) |

## What AdCreative.ai does

Banner/video ad generation; AI copy; pre-launch conversion score prediction; brand kit training; 20+ platform formats; creative refresh suggestions

## What Gravity does

Millions of ads/day served (company-reported); CPM billing; first-party tracking pixels (no cross-site data sharing); placement in ChatGPT, Codebuff, EcoGPT, Daimon, Magneta, Runable, CTO.new; specialized 1B-param model cutting ad-serving p99 latency ~5.7x and inference cost ~10x (Inference.net case study)

## How to choose

Choose **AdCreative.ai** if: Founders, startups, SMBs and agencies running paid ads.

Choose **Gravity** if: Advertisers (Best Buy, Target, Vercel, MongoDB) and AI platform developers seeking monetization.
