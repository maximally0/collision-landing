# AiSDR vs Alta

**AiSDR:** AI SDR agent that finds prospects in real time, writes personalized email/LinkedIn/text outreach and books qualified meetings that show up.

**Alta:** AI GTM system of actions: self-optimizing agents execute the full top-of-funnel (prospecting, personalized multi-channel outreach, inbound qualification, AI calling, ad audience sync, pipeline analytics) from first signal to booked meeting

## At a glance

| | AiSDR | Alta |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_OUTBOUND | AI_OUTBOUND |
| Pricing | From ~$250/mo; flat plans up to ~$900/mo | Contact sales (not public) |
| Best for | B2B SaaS growth teams (50-300 employees, $2M+ ARR) | Mid-market and enterprise GTM teams |
| Differentiator | Pure-play AI SDR (YC-backed) with strong booked-meeting performance claims and HubSpot-native workflow. | Full top-of-funnel coverage (outbound + inbound + calling + paid alignment + pipeline intelligence) as one self-optimizing system, with enterprise trust certs and logos (Snowflake, Deel, Riverside) |

## What AiSDR does

AI prospecting and qualification, personalized emails/LinkedIn/texts, campaign planning, performance monitoring, inbound handling, CRM sync.

## What Alta does

Self-optimizing agents; personalized email/LinkedIn/call outreach; human-quality AI conversations; automatic ad audience sync to Google Ads/Meta; 50+ data source integrations; Slack + CRM integrations; SOC 2 + ISO certified

## How to choose

Choose **AiSDR** if: B2B SaaS growth teams (50-300 employees, $2M+ ARR).

Choose **Alta** if: Mid-market and enterprise GTM teams.
