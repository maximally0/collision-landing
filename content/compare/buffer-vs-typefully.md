# Buffer vs Typefully

**Buffer:** Social media scheduling and publishing platform with built-in AI Assistant (OpenAI) for content ideas, drafting and repurposing; per-channel pricing.

**Typefully:** AI-powered writing and scheduling tool for X, LinkedIn, Bluesky and Threads; distraction-free editor with AI writing assistance and analytics.

## At a glance

| | Buffer | Typefully |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_SOCIAL | AI_SOCIAL |
| Pricing | Free; Essentials $6/channel/mo; Team $12/channel/mo | Free plan; Starter ~$14/mo; Team ~$29/mo; Agency/Enterprise up to $199/mo |
| Best for | Founders, creators, small marketing teams and agencies | Individual creators, founders and small teams building audiences |
| Differentiator | Per-channel pricing and a genuinely free AI tier make it the default low-cost AI social tool for startups. | Creator-first writing experience with native support for emerging networks (Bluesky, Threads) and 10,000+ customers. |

## What Buffer does

AI Assistant (OpenAI); content idea library; reply suggestions; scheduling across 11+ networks incl. Bluesky/Threads; analytics; approval workflows; API

## What Typefully does

AI ideas & rewrites; distraction-free composer; real-time platform previews; cross-platform scheduling; analytics; social blog repurposing; team plans

## How to choose

Choose **Buffer** if: Founders, creators, small marketing teams and agencies.

Choose **Typefully** if: Individual creators, founders and small teams building audiences.
