# Collision vs Kuli

AI co-worker for marketing teams at large consumer brands (YC Spring 2026): an agent that watches videos across social media, learns the brand context, and turns signals from billions of social videos into winning campaigns - creator scouting, trend/competitor analysis and campaign-performance analysis without adding headcount.

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where Kuli genuinely wins

Kuli has YC backing, named enterprise logos (Disney, Microsoft, Supercell) and a differentiated video-watching agent loop - deeper creator-intelligence infrastructure than Collision offers (assessment)

## Where Collision genuinely wins

Collision serves founder-led B2B growth (SEO/AEO/GEO, outbound, LinkedIn, personal branding) and is channel-agnostic; Kuli is influencer/social-video intelligence for enterprise consumer teams (assessment)

## How to decide

Choose **Kuli** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Kuli doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
