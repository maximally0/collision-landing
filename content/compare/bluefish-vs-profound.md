# Bluefish vs Profound

**Bluefish:** Agentic Marketing Platform (AMP) for Fortune 500 brands: visibility, influence and measurement of how brands are portrayed by AI tools (ChatGPT, Google AI, Claude, Perplexity, Amazon Rufus). ~10% of the Fortune 500 are customers.

**Profound:** Full-stack AEO platform: answer engine insights, prompt volumes, autonomous content agents, AI-crawler analytics across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini.

## At a glance

| | Bluefish | Profound |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_AEO | AI_AEO |
| Pricing | Enterprise custom | Starter $99/mo; Growth $399/mo |
| Best for | Fortune 500 marketing leaders (Adidas, American Express, Hearst, LVMH, Ulta; 12+ verticals incl. financial services, pharma, beauty, luxury, CPG) | Marketers, AEO/content/PR teams; startups to enterprises |
| Differentiator | Enterprise agentic marketing platform for brand portrayal and influence in AI answers - not a monitoring dashboard; Salesforce Ventures and Amex Ventures backed | Best-funded AEO platform (Sequoia-led) with proprietary prompt-volume data and autonomous marketing agents. |

## What Bluefish does

Millions of AI prompts/responses processed daily; multi-LLM monitoring (ChatGPT, Google AI, Claude, Perplexity, Amazon Rufus); brand portrayal control; enterprise governance; 80-90 employees

## What Profound does

Answer Engine Insights, Prompt Volumes (200M+ prompts), autonomous Agents (content/demand/brand), Agent Analytics, Workflows, Aim prioritization, MCP server.

## How to choose

Choose **Bluefish** if: Fortune 500 marketing leaders (Adidas, American Express, Hearst, LVMH, Ulta; 12+ verticals incl. financial services, pharma, beauty, luxury, CPG).

Choose **Profound** if: Marketers, AEO/content/PR teams; startups to enterprises.
