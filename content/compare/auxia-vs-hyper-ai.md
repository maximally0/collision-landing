# Auxia vs Hyper AI

**Auxia:** Agentic marketing platform for enterprise B2C lifecycle teams: Auxia Decisioning personalizes/orchestrates every customer interaction in real time (200B+ decisions delivered, 500M+/day), and Auxia Agent Studio is a command center where AI agents plan, build and launch campaigns across the team's existing ESP/CDP stack

**Hyper AI:** AI-native marketing agent platform: CMO, paid-ads, social, SEO/AI-search and analytics agents that connect 200+ tools and run 24/7, with multi-client agency workspaces.

## At a glance

| | Auxia | Hyper AI |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM |
| Pricing | Enterprise (demo/early-access waitlist) | Free tier ($10 credits); paid plans (tiers unverified) |
| Best for | Enterprise B2C lifecycle marketing teams (Fortune 500) | Marketing teams, founders and agencies |
| Differentiator | Proven decisioning scale (200B+ decisions; customers Atlassian, Comcast, Docomo) plus a new agentic execution layer - enterprise-grade autonomous marketing backed by real personalization data | Prebuilt expert agents (not DIY automations) with deep ad-platform API execution and multi-client agency mode |

## What Auxia does

500M+ decisions/day; marketing context graph (brand voice, campaign history); natural-language agentic workflows across ESPs/CDPs; collaborative approval workspace; localized campaign generation at global scale; model-driven experimentation replacing manual A/B testing

## What Hyper AI does

80+ ad/marketing connectors; hourly/daily/weekly recurring tasks; brand voice + positioning context; multi-client agency mode; MCP for external agents; $10 free credits

## How to choose

Choose **Auxia** if: Enterprise B2C lifecycle marketing teams (Fortune 500).

Choose **Hyper AI** if: Marketing teams, founders and agencies.
