# Collision vs The AI CMO

## What each one is

**The AI CMO** is an agentic marketing operating system from ROGA AI Ltd (UK). Its AI specialists plan, produce and send campaigns across email, ads, SMS and push, backed by an approval queue, a customer data warehouse and built-in SEO and link-building. It runs the whole marketing function as one governed system, 24/7 — and nothing ships without your approval.

**Collision** is AI-native growth intelligence for founders and startups: one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews), email and outbound — approval gates on every action, one memory across every surface. It's the AI you hire to run growth.

## Where The AI CMO genuinely wins

**Send infrastructure.** This is the real difference. The AI CMO runs an actual delivery path for email, SMS and push — 10,000+ campaigns a month — with the machinery that makes sending safe at volume: a self-filling data warehouse, structural suppression, and an append-only record of every action. That's enterprise-grade trust in an agentic system, plus 600+ connectors and a full ads studio. For a founder who lives or dies by deliverability and compliance, this is the thing to buy (assessment: these are exactly the enterprise features Collision doesn't yet claim).

**Price.** The full platform on every plan, from $299/mo, is the cheapest serious agentic marketing system in this cohort. Pricing verified Aug 2026.

## Where Collision genuinely wins

**The surfaces The AI CMO doesn't touch.** Collision's core loop — outbound, lead gen, personal branding, LinkedIn and X growth, and deep SEO/AEO/GEO work — sits outside The AI CMO's campaign-execution focus (assessment). If your growth depends on founder brand, outbound pipeline, or being cited in AI search, Collision owns those surfaces.

**One memory.** The AI CMO holds your data in a warehouse; Collision holds your strategy, voice and learnings in one memory that carries across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound. Your source of truth follows the work, not the campaign.

**Approval-based by design.** Collision puts approval gates on every action across every surface — you see and own each move before it ships.

## Pricing reality

The AI CMO: from $299/mo, full platform on every plan. Collision: pre-launch hypothesis of $500–1,000/mo (Launch), $2,000–6,000/mo (Growth), $8,000–15,000/mo (Scale) — priced as a growth hire, not a campaign system. Pricing verified Aug 2026.

## How to decide in 5 minutes

- Need a governed campaign engine with warehouse-grade sending today, under $300/mo → The AI CMO is the honest pick.
- Need founder-led growth across LinkedIn, X, outbound, SEO and AI search, with one memory and approval gates → Collision.
- Need both → Collision's email and outbound surfaces are built to own the full loop; start with the surface that hurts most.
