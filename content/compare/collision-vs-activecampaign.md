# Collision vs ActiveCampaign

Incumbent marketing automation platform (est. 2003) repositioned as an autonomous marketing platform: Active Intelligence AI agents plan, create, launch and optimize email, SMS and WhatsApp campaigns end-to-end

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where ActiveCampaign genuinely wins

ActiveCampaign's installed base, behavioral data scale and results guarantee make it the default autonomous-marketing choice for its existing customers - the HubSpot-Breeze pattern in the email lane (assessment)

## Where Collision genuinely wins

Collision spans SEO/GEO/AEO, outbound, LinkedIn, personal branding and strategy; ActiveCampaign is channel-bound to email/SMS/WhatsApp lifecycle marketing (assessment)

## Pricing reality

- **ActiveCampaign:** Tiered subscription (Plus/Pro/Enterprise) (Subscription)
- **Collision:** Launch $500–1,000/mo · Growth $2,000–6,000/mo · Scale $8,000–15,000/mo

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## How to decide

Choose **ActiveCampaign** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts ActiveCampaign doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
