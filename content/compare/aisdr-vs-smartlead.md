# AiSDR vs Smartlead

**AiSDR:** AI SDR agent that finds prospects in real time, writes personalized email/LinkedIn/text outreach and books qualified meetings that show up.

**Smartlead:** Cold email platform for outbound at scale: unlimited mailboxes, AI warmup, deliverability controls and AI agent personalization.

## At a glance

| | AiSDR | Smartlead |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_OUTBOUND | AI_OUTBOUND |
| Pricing | From ~$250/mo; flat plans up to ~$900/mo | 14-day free trial; from ~$39/mo (Basic); ~$94/mo (Pro); ~$174/mo (Custom) |
| Best for | B2B SaaS growth teams (50-300 employees, $2M+ ARR) | Outbound agencies, sales teams, recruiters and startups |
| Differentiator | Pure-play AI SDR (YC-backed) with strong booked-meeting performance claims and HubSpot-native workflow. | Deliverability-first cold email (unlimited mailboxes + AI warmup) that powers most outbound agencies and startups. |

## What AiSDR does

AI prospecting and qualification, personalized emails/LinkedIn/texts, campaign planning, performance monitoring, inbound handling, CRM sync.

## What Smartlead does

Unlimited mailboxes & AI warmup; deliverability controls; AI personalization via SmartAgent; multichannel campaigns; unified inbox; white-label; API

## How to choose

Choose **AiSDR** if: B2B SaaS growth teams (50-300 employees, $2M+ ARR).

Choose **Smartlead** if: Outbound agencies, sales teams, recruiters and startups.
