# Gradial vs HubSpot Breeze

**Gradial:** Agentic AI operating system ('system of work') for enterprise marketing: AI agents run the full marketing operations workflow - authoring, QA, brand compliance, accessibility, asset tagging, assembly - across existing stacks (Adobe, Salesforce, ServiceNow, Databricks), with GEO-driven execution that ships fixes (not just recommendations) when AI-search gaps surface. $65M Series C (Jun 2026) at $675M valuation.

**HubSpot Breeze:** AI layer inside HubSpot CRM: Breeze Copilot plus 20+ agents (content, social, prospecting, customer) automating marketing, sales and service

## At a glance

| | Gradial | HubSpot Breeze |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_MARKETING_AUTOMATION | AI_MARKETING_AUTOMATION |
| Pricing | Enterprise (demo/quote) | Included with Marketing/Sales/Service Hub Pro+; runs on HubSpot credits; outcome-based for some agents |
| Best for | Enterprise marketing and digital operations teams (AWS, T-Mobile, Kaiser Foundation Health Plan, U.S. Bank, Prudential, Vanguard) | SMB to enterprise HubSpot customers (Marketing/Sales/Service Hubs) |
| Differentiator | Only agentic marketing platform that ships fixes (not recommendations) when GEO data shows AI-search gaps; compliance-first design for regulated enterprises; $675M valuation with Fortune-500 roster (AWS, T-Mobile, Kaiser, U.S. Bank) | Agents run natively on existing CRM data and workflows, distributed to HubSpot's large SMB install base |

## What Gradial does

AI agents running authoring-to-assembly workflows; brand-compliance and QA enforcement; GEO-driven execution (surfaces AI-search gaps and ships fixes directly); HubSpot email operations; Slack + GEO Sentiment; customer-journey simulation; accessibility/translation/localization automation; campaign execution time reduced 80-90% per T-Mobile

## What HubSpot Breeze does

20+ agents in Breeze Studio; content across blogs/podcasts/case studies; multi-channel social strategies; prospecting/outreach; credits (10/prompt); outcome-based pricing for key agents since Apr 2026

## How to choose

Choose **Gradial** if: Enterprise marketing and digital operations teams (AWS, T-Mobile, Kaiser Foundation Health Plan, U.S. Bank, Prudential, Vanguard).

Choose **HubSpot Breeze** if: SMB to enterprise HubSpot customers (Marketing/Sales/Service Hubs).
