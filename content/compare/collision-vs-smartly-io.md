# Collision vs Smartly.io

AI advertising platform unifying creative production, media buying and intelligence across Meta, TikTok, Snap, Pinterest, Google and CTV.

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where Smartly.io genuinely wins

Enterprise-only (min ~$50K spend, 8-12 week onboarding); not founder-accessible; no SEO, PR, outbound or personal-branding coverage

## Where Collision genuinely wins

assessment: Collision serves founders/SMBs with self-serve autonomous agents; Smartly requires enterprise spend and onboarding

## Pricing reality

- **Smartly.io:** Custom enterprise (~$5K+/mo) (Enterprise annual contracts)
- **Collision:** Launch $500–1,000/mo · Growth $2,000–6,000/mo · Scale $8,000–15,000/mo (pre-launch hypothesis pricing)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-14. Collision pricing is pre-launch positioning, not market-validated._

## How to decide

Choose **Smartly.io** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Smartly.io doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
