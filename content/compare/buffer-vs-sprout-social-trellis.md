# Buffer vs Sprout Social (Trellis)

**Buffer:** Social media scheduling and publishing platform with built-in AI Assistant (OpenAI) for content ideas, drafting and repurposing; per-channel pricing.

**Sprout Social (Trellis):** AI-powered social intelligence platform (NASDAQ: SPT) with Trellis, a proprietary agentic AI layer now generally available across every plan - spans Listening, Publishing, Smart Inbox and Reporting; Trellis Studio lets teams build custom AI skill templates that turn recurring work into automated workflows.

## At a glance

| | Buffer | Sprout Social (Trellis) |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_SOCIAL | AI_SOCIAL |
| Pricing | Free; Essentials $6/channel/mo; Team $12/channel/mo | Enterprise (custom, demo-based) |
| Best for | Founders, creators, small marketing teams and agencies | Enterprise and mid-market social/marketing teams (JetBlue, Ipsy; 30,000+ brands) |
| Differentiator | Per-channel pricing and a genuinely free AI tier make it the default low-cost AI social tool for startups. | Only social-management incumbent with a single agentic layer spanning the whole platform plus user-built skills (Trellis Studio) - agentic social intelligence at enterprise scale |

## What Buffer does

AI Assistant (OpenAI); content idea library; reply suggestions; scheduling across 11+ networks incl. Bluesky/Threads; analytics; approval workflows; API

## What Sprout Social (Trellis) does

Trellis GA to every customer on every plan (Aug 2026); Trellis Studio (custom AI skill templates, automated recurring workflows); JetBlue/Ipsy evidence (reporting from hours to minutes); brand-risk detection before it spreads; cross-team routing of social feedback; sentiment/theme analysis

## How to choose

Choose **Buffer** if: Founders, creators, small marketing teams and agencies.

Choose **Sprout Social (Trellis)** if: Enterprise and mid-market social/marketing teams (JetBlue, Ipsy; 30,000+ brands).
