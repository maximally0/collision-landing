# FeedHive vs Postwise

**FeedHive:** Social media automation platform (v2.0) with AI content generation, workflows, AI agents, smart scheduling, recycling and analytics.

**Postwise:** AI tool to write, schedule and grow on X, LinkedIn and Threads; generates viral-engineered posts and offers growth/DM automations.

## At a glance

| | FeedHive | Postwise |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_SOCIAL | AI_SOCIAL |
| Pricing | From $15/mo (tiers $15-$69/mo) | 7-day free trial; Basic $37/mo; Unlimited $97/mo (annual) |
| Best for | Founders, marketers and agencies | Creators, founders and marketers growing personal brands on X/LinkedIn |
| Differentiator | Workflow/condition-based automation plus AI agents inside a social scheduler. | Engagement-engineered AI trained on proven posts plus revenue tools (auto-DM, link plugging) inside a social scheduler. |

## What FeedHive does

AI agents, automation workflows, post conditions, content recycling, social inbox, analytics, AI design library, API/CLI, AI-agent integrations.

## What Postwise does

AI viral post & thread generation; one topic to many posts; multi-platform scheduling; GrowthTools follower suite; GhostWriter autopost; auto-send DMs; repost winning content

## How to choose

Choose **FeedHive** if: Founders, marketers and agencies.

Choose **Postwise** if: Creators, founders and marketers growing personal brands on X/LinkedIn.
