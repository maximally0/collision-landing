# Hyper AI vs Simon AI

**Hyper AI:** AI-native marketing agent platform: CMO, paid-ads, social, SEO/AI-search and analytics agents that connect 200+ tools and run 24/7, with multi-client agency workspaces.

**Simon AI:** Agentic marketing platform (ex-Simon Data): marketers set goals, agents turn live customer + real-world signals into adaptive campaigns

## At a glance

| | Hyper AI | Simon AI |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM |
| Pricing | Free tier ($10 credits); paid plans (tiers unverified) | Enterprise (contact sales) |
| Best for | Marketing teams, founders and agencies | Mid-market to enterprise brands (ASOS, SeatGeek, Travel + Leisure, Equinox) |
| Differentiator | Prebuilt expert agents (not DIY automations) with deep ad-platform API execution and multi-client agency mode | AI-first composable CDP + agents = enterprise-scale data-driven personalization, not content generation |

## What Hyper AI does

80+ ad/marketing connectors; hourly/daily/weekly recurring tasks; brand voice + positioning context; multi-client agency mode; MCP for external agents; $10 free credits

## What Simon AI does

Personalization Studio; AI Fields & AI Moments; Insight Agents; Blueprints; adaptive segments; composable CDP on Snowflake; weather/trend/inventory triggers

## How to choose

Choose **Hyper AI** if: Marketing teams, founders and agencies.

Choose **Simon AI** if: Mid-market to enterprise brands (ASOS, SeatGeek, Travel + Leisure, Equinox).
