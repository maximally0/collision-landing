## Where Collision genuinely wins

- **FeedHive:** From $15/mo (tiers $15-$69/mo) (Subscription)

## How to decide

Choose **FeedHive** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts FeedHive doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
