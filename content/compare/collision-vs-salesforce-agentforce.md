Enterprise AI agent platform: autonomous agents across service, sales, marketing and commerce; marketing agents plan/execute campaigns on CRM data

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where Salesforce Agentforce genuinely wins

Salesforce's data moat, Fortune 100 reach and agent ecosystem massively outscale Collision's execution surface (assessment)

## Where Collision genuinely wins

Collision is self-serve, productized growth for founders/startups; Agentforce is an enterprise platform requiring Data Cloud spend (assessment)

## Pricing reality

- **Salesforce Agentforce:** From $2/conversation; Flex Credits ~$0.10/action (~$500/100k); Data Cloud add-on (Usage-based)
- **Collision:** Launch $500–1,000/mo · Growth $2,000–6,000/mo · Scale $8,000–15,000/mo

_Pricing verified 2026-08-14._

## How to decide

Choose **Salesforce Agentforce** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Salesforce Agentforce doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
