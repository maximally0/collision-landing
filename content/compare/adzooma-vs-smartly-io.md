# Adzooma vs Smartly.io

**Adzooma:** AI-powered PPC platform that audits, optimizes and scales Google, Microsoft and Meta campaigns with automated, one-click recommendations.

**Smartly.io:** AI advertising platform unifying creative production, media buying and intelligence across Meta, TikTok, Snap, Pinterest, Google and CTV.

## At a glance

| | Adzooma | Smartly.io |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_ADVERTISING | AI_ADVERTISING |
| Pricing | Free; Pro from ~£69/mo | Custom enterprise (~$5K+/mo) |
| Best for | SMBs, agencies, freelancers and founders running paid ads | Enterprise brands and performance teams (700+ brands, $5B ad spend) |
| Differentiator | Free AI PPC optimization across three ad networks with two-click implementation | Only platform managing creative and media end-to-end for 700+ global brands at 300B creatives/year |

## What Adzooma does

24/7 machine-learning analysis; one-click recommendation application; performance scorecards; automated alerts; AI ad copy; multi-channel reporting

## What Smartly.io does

AI creative production at scale (300B creatives/yr); automated campaign wizardry; cross-platform orchestration; real-time optimization; Brand Pulse measurement

## How to choose

Choose **Adzooma** if: SMBs, agencies, freelancers and founders running paid ads.

Choose **Smartly.io** if: Enterprise brands and performance teams (700+ brands, $5B ad spend).
