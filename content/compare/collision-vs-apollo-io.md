# Collision vs Apollo.io

AI sales platform combining a large B2B contact database, engagement sequences, dialer and AI Assistant; free tier, used by 600K+ companies.

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where Apollo.io genuinely wins

Free access to a huge verified database and mature automation is a powerful moat; Collision cannot match its data scale and will be compared on price.

## Where Collision genuinely wins

assessment - Collision adds content, SEO/AEO and social growth; Apollo is sales-database-centric, so Collision wins on brand-plus-inbound bundling

## Pricing reality

- **Apollo.io:** Free plan; paid from ~$49/user/mo; custom enterprise (subscription + credits)
- **Collision:** Launch $500–1,000/mo · Growth $2,000–6,000/mo · Scale $8,000–15,000/mo

_Pricing verified 2026-08-14._

## How to decide

Choose **Apollo.io** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Apollo.io doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
