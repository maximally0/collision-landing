# Collision vs Artisan

AI employee 'Ava' (AI BDR) finds leads, enriches contacts, sends hyper-personalized email/social outreach and books meetings autonomously.

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where Artisan genuinely wins

## Where Collision genuinely wins

- **Artisan:** Custom (demo-based) (Subscription)

## How to decide

Choose **Artisan** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Artisan doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
