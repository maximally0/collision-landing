# Buffer vs Passionfroot

**Buffer:** Social media scheduling and publishing platform with built-in AI Assistant (OpenAI) for content ideas, drafting and repurposing; per-channel pricing.

**Passionfroot:** AI-native B2B creator-led growth platform (Berlin): Zest AI agent runs creator campaigns end-to-end from brief to payment, powered by a proprietary Creator Graph of B2B creator pricing/performance and a global payment wallet. $15M Series A led by Insight Partners (Jul 2026); profitable with 13x revenue growth in a year.

## At a glance

| | Buffer | Passionfroot |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_SOCIAL | AI_SOCIAL |
| Pricing | Free; Essentials $6/channel/mo; Team $12/channel/mo | Not published (platform + wallet fees) |
| Best for | Founders, creators, small marketing teams and agencies | B2B brands/GTM teams doing creator-led distribution (ElevenLabs, Figma, Replit, Framer, Gamma; 150+ brands) |
| Differentiator | Per-channel pricing and a genuinely free AI tier make it the default low-cost AI social tool for startups. | First AI-native B2B creator platform: proprietary Creator Graph + end-to-end agent execution + global payments, profitable and growing 13x YoY |

## What Buffer does

AI Assistant (OpenAI); content idea library; reply suggestions; scheduling across 11+ networks incl. Bluesky/Threads; analytics; approval workflows; API

## What Passionfroot does

Zest agent (create, execute, monitor campaigns); Creator Graph (pricing/performance data from thousands of campaigns); Passionfroot Wallet (global payouts, spend visibility); finding creators inside and outside the platform; measuring AI-citation impact (in progress)

## How to choose

Choose **Buffer** if: Founders, creators, small marketing teams and agencies.

Choose **Passionfroot** if: B2B brands/GTM teams doing creator-led distribution (ElevenLabs, Figma, Replit, Framer, Gamma; 150+ brands).
