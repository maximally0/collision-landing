# Copy.ai vs The AI CMO

**Copy.ai:** GTM AI platform: autonomous workflows and agents for outbound prospecting, lead processing, content creation, deal coaching; now part of Fullcast

**The AI CMO:** Agentic marketing OS by ROGA AI Ltd: AI specialists plan, produce and send campaigns (email, ads, SMS, push) with approval queue, CDW and built-in SEO/link-building

## At a glance

| | Copy.ai | The AI CMO |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_MARKETING_OS | AI_MARKETING_OS |
| Pricing | Freemium; tiered paid plans | From $299/mo; full platform on every plan |
| Best for | B2B sales and marketing teams, SMB to enterprise | Founders, solo marketers, SMBs, agencies |
| Differentiator | Autonomous GTM workflows spanning sales + marketing (not just copy) with a massive freemium user base | Append-only audit ledger, structural suppression and dedicated warehouse = enterprise trust in an agentic marketing OS |

## What Copy.ai does

Copy Agents; pre-built GTM workflows; Infobase (brand voice/knowledge); CRM/email integrations; 15M+ users; freemium tools

## What The AI CMO does

10k+ campaigns/mo; customer 360 + self-filling data warehouse; studios (ads, email, pages, video); behavior journeys; append-only record; structural suppression; 600+ connectors

## How to choose

Choose **Copy.ai** if: B2B sales and marketing teams, SMB to enterprise.

Choose **The AI CMO** if: Founders, solo marketers, SMBs, agencies.
