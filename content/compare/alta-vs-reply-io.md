# Alta vs Reply.io

**Alta:** AI GTM system of actions: self-optimizing agents execute the full top-of-funnel (prospecting, personalized multi-channel outreach, inbound qualification, AI calling, ad audience sync, pipeline analytics) from first signal to booked meeting

**Reply.io:** AI sales outreach platform: multichannel sequences, email/LinkedIn automation, 1B+ contact data and AI SDR agents that run prospecting.

## At a glance

| | Alta | Reply.io |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_OUTBOUND | AI_OUTBOUND |
| Pricing | Contact sales (not public) | Free trial; from ~$60/user/mo |
| Best for | Mid-market and enterprise GTM teams | Startups, growth teams and agencies running outbound |
| Differentiator | Full top-of-funnel coverage (outbound + inbound + calling + paid alignment + pipeline intelligence) as one self-optimizing system, with enterprise trust certs and logos (Snowflake, Deel, Riverside) | AI SDR agents (e.g., Jason AI) inside a mature multichannel engagement platform - automation-native, not bolted-on. |

## What Alta does

Self-optimizing agents; personalized email/LinkedIn/call outreach; human-quality AI conversations; automatic ad audience sync to Google Ads/Meta; 50+ data source integrations; Slack + CRM integrations; SOC 2 + ISO certified

## What Reply.io does

Multichannel sequences; AI SDR agents; audience suggestions; email/LinkedIn automation; Findy Chrome extension; data enrichment; deliverability tools

## How to choose

Choose **Alta** if: Mid-market and enterprise GTM teams.

Choose **Reply.io** if: Startups, growth teams and agencies running outbound.
