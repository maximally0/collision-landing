# Collision vs HubSpot Breeze

## What each one is

**HubSpot Breeze** is the AI layer inside HubSpot's CRM: a Breeze Copilot plus 20+ agents (content, social, prospecting, customer) that handle marketing, sales and service work on the customer data already living in HubSpot. It ships with Marketing, Sales and Service Hub Pro and above, runs on HubSpot credits, and moved to outcome-based pricing for key agents in April 2026.

**Collision** is AI-native growth intelligence for founders and startups: one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews), email and outbound — approval gates on every action, one memory across every surface. It's the AI you hire to run growth.

## Capability comparison

| Capability | HubSpot Breeze | Collision |
|---|---|---|
| Content creation | Content agents (blogs, podcasts, case studies) | Research–plan–write loop across surfaces |
| Social | Multi-channel social strategy agents | LinkedIn and X as core surfaces |
| Prospecting / outreach | Prospecting and outreach agents | Outbound as a core surface |
| CRM-native data | Runs on live HubSpot CRM data | No CRM dependency; one memory instead |
| Channel-agnostic growth (SEO/AEO/GEO, personal branding, distribution) | Inside the HubSpot ecosystem (assessment) | Core, by design |
| Buyer | Existing HubSpot customers, SMB to enterprise | Founders and startups on any stack |
| Pricing | Included with Hub Pro+; credits (10 per query); outcome-based for some agents since Apr 2026 | $500–15,000/mo  |

## Where Breeze genuinely wins

**CRM-native data.** Breeze's agents run where your customer data already lives — in the CRM, on live workflows, grounded in what your sales team actually knows. No sync, no export, no cold start. For a HubSpot shop, that's the lowest-friction AI you'll ever adopt.

**SMB distribution.** HubSpot's installed base and brand trust reach small businesses at a scale no startup can match (assessment). Breeze is the default choice for anyone already paying for HubSpot — it's bundled, it's familiar, and the sales motion already happened.

**Outcome-based pricing.** Key agents moved to outcome-based pricing in April 2026 — you pay for results, which is a genuinely founder-friendly signal.

## Where Collision genuinely wins

**Channel-agnostic by design.** Breeze lives inside the HubSpot ecosystem (assessment). Collision works on LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound no matter what CRM you use — or don't. Founders building personal brand and outbound pipeline aren't waiting on a HubSpot rollout.

**Surfaces Breeze doesn't serve.** SEO/AEO/GEO depth, personal branding and multi-channel distribution are core to Collision; they sit outside Breeze's content, social and prospecting agents (assessment).

**One memory.** Collision keeps a single source of truth across every surface — not a CRM database, but a living memory of strategy, voice and learnings that compounds. Approval gates sit on every action.

**Independent.** Breeze is an ecosystem product; Collision is the intelligence you own.

**Built to learn.** Every post, every outreach reply, every AI-search citation win feeds back into one memory — so the intelligence gets sharper at your market instead of starting from scratch each week.

## Pricing reality

Breeze: included with Marketing/Sales/Service Hub Pro+; usage runs on HubSpot credits, with outcome-based pricing for key agents since April 2026. Collision: starting at $500–1,000/mo (Launch), $2,000–6,000/mo (Growth), $8,000–15,000/mo (Scale). Pricing verified Aug 2026.

## How to decide in 5 minutes

- Already on HubSpot and want AI that plugs into your CRM data today → Breeze is the honest, low-friction pick.
- Growth depends on LinkedIn, X, AI search, outbound and SEO — or you're not on HubSpot → Collision.
- On HubSpot but need founder brand, outbound and AI-search growth → Collision as the growth intelligence; Breeze stays your CRM layer.
