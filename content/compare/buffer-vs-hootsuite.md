# Buffer vs Hootsuite

**Buffer:** Social media scheduling and publishing platform with built-in AI Assistant (OpenAI) for content ideas, drafting and repurposing; per-channel pricing.

**Hootsuite:** Enterprise social media management suite with OwlyWriter AI and OwlyGPT: AI captions, hashtags, insights and social listening at scale.

## At a glance

| | Buffer | Hootsuite |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_SOCIAL | AI_SOCIAL |
| Pricing | Free; Essentials $6/channel/mo; Team $12/channel/mo | Free trial; plans from ~$99/mo; Enterprise custom |
| Best for | Founders, creators, small marketing teams and agencies | Enterprises, agencies and larger marketing teams |
| Differentiator | Per-channel pricing and a genuinely free AI tier make it the default low-cost AI social tool for startups. | OwlyGPT trained on real-time social trends plus 15+ years of social expertise and enterprise compliance tooling. |

## What Buffer does

AI Assistant (OpenAI); content idea library; reply suggestions; scheduling across 11+ networks incl. Bluesky/Threads; analytics; approval workflows; API

## What Hootsuite does

OwlyWriter AI; OwlyGPT (trend-aware assistant); AI hashtags; social listening; scheduling; analytics; advocacy; compliance/approval workflows

## How to choose

Choose **Buffer** if: Founders, creators, small marketing teams and agencies.

Choose **Hootsuite** if: Enterprises, agencies and larger marketing teams.
