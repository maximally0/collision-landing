# Collision vs Pomo

AI marketing team platform ('your AI marketing team, on shift daily'): learns the business, then coordinates, launches and optimizes campaigns across paid ads, social and AI search (AEO). Built by ex-Google/DeepMind/Meta engineers; Databricks AI Accelerator program.

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.
