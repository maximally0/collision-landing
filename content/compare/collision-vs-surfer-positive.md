## Where Surfer (Positive) genuinely wins

## Where Collision genuinely wins

## How to decide

Choose **Surfer (Positive)** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Surfer (Positive) doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
