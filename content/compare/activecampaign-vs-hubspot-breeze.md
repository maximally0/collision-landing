# ActiveCampaign vs HubSpot Breeze

**ActiveCampaign:** Incumbent marketing automation platform (est. 2003) repositioned as an autonomous marketing platform: Active Intelligence AI agents plan, create, launch and optimize email, SMS and WhatsApp campaigns end-to-end

**HubSpot Breeze:** AI layer inside HubSpot CRM: Breeze Copilot plus 20+ agents (content, social, prospecting, customer) automating marketing, sales and service

## At a glance

| | ActiveCampaign | HubSpot Breeze |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_MARKETING_AUTOMATION | AI_MARKETING_AUTOMATION |
| Pricing | Tiered subscription (Plus/Pro/Enterprise) | Included with Marketing/Sales/Service Hub Pro+; runs on HubSpot credits; outcome-based for some agents |
| Best for | SMB and mid-market marketing teams (180K+ customer base) | SMB to enterprise HubSpot customers (Marketing/Sales/Service Hubs) |
| Differentiator | Incumbent automation vendor with a large SMB install base, a results guarantee and billions of behavioral data points - autonomous marketing layered on an existing customer database rather than greenfield | Agents run natively on existing CRM data and workflows, distributed to HubSpot's large SMB install base |

## What ActiveCampaign does

Autonomous Marketing platform positioning; Wavelength (marketing AI tuned to each business's data, launched Aug 27 2026); results guarantee on autonomous marketing; Google Ads connector for AI-guided campaign creation (Jul 8 2026); first autonomous marketing connector for Anthropic Claude (Nov 2025); MCP server; Feedback Intelligence AI-evaluation acquisition (Feb 3 2026); billions of behavioral data points processed daily

## What HubSpot Breeze does

20+ agents in Breeze Studio; content across blogs/podcasts/case studies; multi-channel social strategies; prospecting/outreach; credits (10/prompt); outcome-based pricing for key agents since Apr 2026

## How to choose

Choose **ActiveCampaign** if: SMB and mid-market marketing teams (180K+ customer base).

Choose **HubSpot Breeze** if: SMB to enterprise HubSpot customers (Marketing/Sales/Service Hubs).
