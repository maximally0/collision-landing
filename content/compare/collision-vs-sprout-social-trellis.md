# Collision vs Sprout Social (Trellis)

AI-powered social intelligence platform (NASDAQ: SPT) with Trellis, a proprietary agentic AI layer now generally available across every plan - spans Listening, Publishing, Smart Inbox and Reporting; Trellis Studio lets teams build custom AI skill templates that turn recurring work into automated workflows.

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where Sprout Social (Trellis) genuinely wins

Sprout's data scale (billions of data points), enterprise logos and public-company resources far exceed Collision's social tooling; Trellis Studio shows user-built agentic workflows (assessment)

## Where Collision genuinely wins

Collision is founder-priced, channel-agnostic growth execution (SEO/AEO/GEO, outbound, LinkedIn); Sprout is enterprise social intelligence/publishing (assessment)

## How to decide

Choose **Sprout Social (Trellis)** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Sprout Social (Trellis) doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
