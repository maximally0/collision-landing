# Adobe GenStudio vs Simon AI

**Adobe GenStudio:** Generative AI app for performance marketing: on-brand copy/image/video variants at scale, activated to paid social/display/CTV with brand governance

**Simon AI:** Agentic marketing platform (ex-Simon Data): marketers set goals, agents turn live customer + real-world signals into adaptive campaigns

## At a glance

| | Adobe GenStudio | Simon AI |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM |
| Pricing | Enterprise quote (Adobe Experience Cloud) | Enterprise (contact sales) |
| Best for | Enterprise marketing teams (retail, ecommerce, financial services, telecom, CPG) | Mid-market to enterprise brands (ASOS, SeatGeek, Travel + Leisure, Equinox) |
| Differentiator | Creative-production scale + brand governance + Adobe ecosystem (CDP, AEM, Workfront) — not autonomous strategy | AI-first composable CDP + agents = enterprise-scale data-driven personalization, not content generation |

## What Adobe GenStudio does

Firefly/Azure OpenAI/Veo models; on-brand copy+image+video variants; activation to paid social/display/CTV; A/B testing; Workfront/AEM/Real-Time CDP integrations

## What Simon AI does

Personalization Studio; AI Fields & AI Moments; Insight Agents; Blueprints; adaptive segments; composable CDP on Snowflake; weather/trend/inventory triggers

## How to choose

Choose **Adobe GenStudio** if: Enterprise marketing teams (retail, ecommerce, financial services, telecom, CPG).

Choose **Simon AI** if: Mid-market to enterprise brands (ASOS, SeatGeek, Travel + Leisure, Equinox).
