# Hyper AI vs Jasper

**Hyper AI:** AI-native marketing agent platform: CMO, paid-ads, social, SEO/AI-search and analytics agents that connect 200+ tools and run 24/7, with multi-client agency workspaces.

**Jasper:** AI marketing platform (ex-AI copywriter): purpose-built agents execute end-to-end workflows - campaigns, GEO/SEO/AEO optimization, content pipelines

## At a glance

| | Hyper AI | Jasper |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM |
| Pricing | Free tier ($10 credits); paid plans (tiers unverified) | Tiered subscription; free trial (Creator/Pro/Business plans) |
| Best for | Marketing teams, founders and agencies | Marketing teams, SMB to enterprise |
| Differentiator | Prebuilt expert agents (not DIY automations) with deep ad-platform API execution and multi-client agency mode | Only major AI marketing platform with native GEO + SEO/AEO + agents + brand governance in one suite |

## What Hyper AI does

80+ ad/marketing connectors; hourly/daily/weekly recurring tasks; brand voice + positioning context; multi-client agency mode; MCP for external agents; $10 free credits

## What Jasper does

Agents library (product launch, social campaigns); GEO citation monitoring; SEO & AEO content; Brand Voice/Style Guide governance; Knowledge Base grounding; APIs/MCP; Semrush integration

## How to choose

Choose **Hyper AI** if: Marketing teams, founders and agencies.

Choose **Jasper** if: Marketing teams, SMB to enterprise.
