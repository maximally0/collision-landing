# Collision vs Jasper

## What each one is

**Jasper** is an AI marketing platform that started life as an AI copywriter in 2021 and now ships purpose-built agents for campaigns, GEO/SEO/AEO optimization and content pipelines. It's brand-governed by design — Brand Voice and Style Guide controls, knowledge-base grounding, APIs and MCP, plus a Semrush integration — and it's big: $131M raised, a $1.5B valuation at its 2022 Series A, and a customer base from SMB to enterprise.

**Collision** is AI-native growth intelligence for founders and startups: one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews), email and outbound — approval gates on every action, one memory across every surface. It's the AI you hire to run growth.

## Capability comparison

| Capability | Jasper | Collision |
|---|---|---|
| Content creation and pipelines | Agents library (product launch, social campaigns); content pipelines | Research–plan–write–distribute loop across surfaces |

| Pricing | Tiered subscription (Creator/Pro/Business), free trial | $500–15,000/mo ( hypothesis) |

## Where Jasper genuinely wins

**Scale and enterprise credibility.** $131M raised, a $1.5B valuation and years of brand trust — Jasper is the platform a large marketing org can hand to a team without a second thought (assessment: that institutional weight outmatches a product on content and GEO depth).

**GEO tooling depth.** Native GEO citation monitoring is a product there, not a feature buried in a roadmap. Teams whose whole job is "get us cited in AI answers, at scale" have a purpose-built instrument.

**Brand governance.** Brand Voice, Style Guide and knowledge-base grounding are first-class controls. For a company that must keep every asset on-brand across hundreds of writers, that's a real moat.

## Where Collision genuinely wins

**The full growth loop.** Jasper is content and campaign creation; it doesn't execute the loop that turns content into pipeline. Collision adds outbound, lead gen, SDR, personal branding, LinkedIn growth and distribution (assessment) — the surfaces where founders actually win deals.

**One memory.** Jasper grounds on a knowledge base; Collision keeps one memory across every surface, so the intelligence learns from LinkedIn what it applies to outbound and AI search. Your source of truth compounds.

**Approval-based, founder-shaped.** Collision puts approval gates on every action and prices for a founder's budget, not an enterprise seat count.

**Built to learn.** Every piece of content, every outbound reply, every citation win feeds back into the same memory — so the intelligence gets sharper at your market instead of starting from scratch each campaign.

## Pricing reality

Jasper runs tiered subscriptions (Creator/Pro/Business) with a free trial; exact prices shift with promos, so check the site. Collision's hypothesis: Launch $500–1,000/mo, Growth $2,000–6,000/mo, Scale $8,000–15,000/mo. Pricing verified Aug 2026.

## How to decide in 5 minutes

- You run a marketing team that needs governed content at scale, with GEO monitoring and enterprise guardrails → Jasper is the proven pick.
- You're a founder who needs content plus the outbound, LinkedIn and AI-search execution that turns it into revenue → Collision.
- You need both today → Jasper for content ops, and watch Collision for the growth loop.
