# Alta vs Smartlead

**Alta:** AI GTM system of actions: self-optimizing agents execute the full top-of-funnel (prospecting, personalized multi-channel outreach, inbound qualification, AI calling, ad audience sync, pipeline analytics) from first signal to booked meeting

**Smartlead:** Cold email platform for outbound at scale: unlimited mailboxes, AI warmup, deliverability controls and AI agent personalization.

## At a glance

| | Alta | Smartlead |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_OUTBOUND | AI_OUTBOUND |
| Pricing | Contact sales (not public) | 14-day free trial; from ~$39/mo (Basic); ~$94/mo (Pro); ~$174/mo (Custom) |
| Best for | Mid-market and enterprise GTM teams | Outbound agencies, sales teams, recruiters and startups |
| Differentiator | Full top-of-funnel coverage (outbound + inbound + calling + paid alignment + pipeline intelligence) as one self-optimizing system, with enterprise trust certs and logos (Snowflake, Deel, Riverside) | Deliverability-first cold email (unlimited mailboxes + AI warmup) that powers most outbound agencies and startups. |

## What Alta does

Self-optimizing agents; personalized email/LinkedIn/call outreach; human-quality AI conversations; automatic ad audience sync to Google Ads/Meta; 50+ data source integrations; Slack + CRM integrations; SOC 2 + ISO certified

## What Smartlead does

Unlimited mailboxes & AI warmup; deliverability controls; AI personalization via SmartAgent; multichannel campaigns; unified inbox; white-label; API

## How to choose

Choose **Alta** if: Mid-market and enterprise GTM teams.

Choose **Smartlead** if: Outbound agencies, sales teams, recruiters and startups.
