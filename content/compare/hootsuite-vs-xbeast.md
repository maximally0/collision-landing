# Hootsuite vs XBeast

**Hootsuite:** Enterprise social media management suite with OwlyWriter AI and OwlyGPT: AI captions, hashtags, insights and social listening at scale.

**XBeast:** AI agent for X/Twitter that writes, schedules, retweets, replies and plugs content in the user's voice on autopilot.

## At a glance

| | Hootsuite | XBeast |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_SOCIAL | AI_SOCIAL |
| Pricing | Free trial; plans from ~$99/mo; Enterprise custom | 7-day free trial; Basic $12/mo; Pro $24/mo; Ultra $72/mo |
| Best for | Enterprises, agencies and larger marketing teams | Creators, founders, indie hackers growing an X presence |
| Differentiator | OwlyGPT trained on real-time social trends plus 15+ years of social expertise and enterprise compliance tooling. | Positioned as an AI agent that fully executes X growth (writes, replies, retweets, plugs), not just a scheduler. |

## What Hootsuite does

OwlyWriter AI; OwlyGPT (trend-aware assistant); AI hashtags; social listening; scheduling; analytics; advocacy; compliance/approval workflows

## What XBeast does

Voice-trained presets; weekly auto-generation with human review; auto-reply, auto-retweet, auto-plug (link/offer on viral posts); YouTube/TikTok to tweet; analytics

## How to choose

Choose **Hootsuite** if: Enterprises, agencies and larger marketing teams.

Choose **XBeast** if: Creators, founders, indie hackers growing an X presence.
