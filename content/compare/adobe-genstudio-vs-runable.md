# Adobe GenStudio vs Runable

**Adobe GenStudio:** Generative AI app for performance marketing: on-brand copy/image/video variants at scale, activated to paid social/display/CTV with brand governance

**Runable:** General-purpose AI agent platform (Bengaluru) that pivoted from AI website/app building into 'Grow': AI agents that find customers, run ad campaigns, manage social media, handle SEO and optimize brand presence in AI chatbot results for small businesses. $21M Series A (Aug 2026); 1.7M registered users; ~$2M annualized revenue run-rate within 3 weeks of launching payments (Mar 2026).

## At a glance

| | Adobe GenStudio | Runable |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM |
| Pricing | Enterprise quote (Adobe Experience Cloud) | Freemium; paid plans live (not fully published) |
| Best for | Enterprise marketing teams (retail, ecommerce, financial services, telecom, CPG) | Non-technical small business owners and solo founders (US, UK, Japan focus) |
| Differentiator | Creative-production scale + brand governance + Adobe ecosystem (CDP, AEM, Workfront) — not autonomous strategy | Huge warm build-side user base (1.7M) + $21M to push the same agent into autonomous growth; the only 'grow' entrant this week with a built-in distribution funnel and India-scale economics |

## What Adobe GenStudio does

Firefly/Azure OpenAI/Veo models; on-brand copy+image+video variants; activation to paid social/display/CTV; A/B testing; Workfront/AEM/Real-Time CDP integrations

## What Runable does

Natural-language build (websites, apps, slides, videos, carousels); deployment + analytics handled; Grow automates bookings, FAQs, repeat orders, social posting/replies, ad campaigns, SEO and AI-chatbot presence; 1.7M registered users; US/UK/Japan/Brazil markets

## How to choose

Choose **Adobe GenStudio** if: Enterprise marketing teams (retail, ecommerce, financial services, telecom, CPG).

Choose **Runable** if: Non-technical small business owners and solo founders (US, UK, Japan focus).
