# Collision vs DOJO AI

Intelligent marketing system for challenger brands: a living knowledge graph (DOJO Graph) models the brand's marketing reality; specialized AI agents act on it across paid, organic, SEO and content, feeding every outcome back into the model.

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where DOJO AI genuinely wins

DOJO has $6M funding, 100+ named customers, 20% MoM growth and a closed-loop learning architecture — a well-resourced full-stack rival to Collision's execution story (assessment).

## Where Collision genuinely wins

Collision is founder-priced and self-serve with outbound, LinkedIn/personal branding and lead gen; DOJO is intelligence-plus-execution aimed at established challenger brands at $499+/mo (assessment).

## Pricing reality

- **DOJO AI:** From $499/mo; 7-day free trial; Starter/Growth/Enterprise tiers (Subscription)
- **Collision:** Launch $500–1,000/mo · Growth $2,000–6,000/mo · Scale $8,000–15,000/mo

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## How to decide

Choose **DOJO AI** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts DOJO AI doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
