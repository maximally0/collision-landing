# Auxia vs Persado

**Auxia:** Agentic marketing platform for enterprise B2C lifecycle teams: Auxia Decisioning personalizes/orchestrates every customer interaction in real time (200B+ decisions delivered, 500M+/day), and Auxia Agent Studio is a command center where AI agents plan, build and launch campaigns across the team's existing ESP/CDP stack

**Persado:** AI message-generation platform for regulated brands: generates, scores, compliance-checks and deploys campaign copy across email/SMS/push/ads

| | Auxia | Persado |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM |
| Pricing | Enterprise (demo/early-access waitlist) | Enterprise (demo/quote) |
| Best for | Enterprise B2C lifecycle marketing teams (Fortune 500) | Enterprise regulated brands (banks, insurers, telecoms, retail) |
| Differentiator | Proven decisioning scale (200B+ decisions; customers Atlassian, Comcast, Docomo) plus a new agentic execution layer - enterprise-grade autonomous marketing backed by real personalization data | Domain-specific motivation AI plus compliance engine for regulated verticals — clears UDAAP/TILA checks out of the box |

## What Auxia does

500M+ decisions/day; marketing context graph (brand voice, campaign history); natural-language agentic workflows across ESPs/CDPs; collaborative approval workspace; localized campaign generation at global scale; model-driven experimentation replacing manual A/B testing

## What Persado does

Brief-Generate-Score-Comply-Deploy-Learn loop; performance score vs 1M+ A/B tests; regulatory checks (UDAAP, TILA, CAN-SPAM); deploys to Salesforce MC, Braze, Adobe Target

## How to choose

Choose **Auxia** if: Enterprise B2C lifecycle marketing teams (Fortune 500).

Choose **Persado** if: Enterprise regulated brands (banks, insurers, telecoms, retail).
