# Buffer vs Predis.ai

**Buffer:** Social media scheduling and publishing platform with built-in AI Assistant (OpenAI) for content ideas, drafting and repurposing; per-channel pricing.

**Predis.ai:** All-in-one AI marketing platform: video ads, UGC, ad copies, social posts; plan, schedule and auto-publish from one calendar (6.4M+ users).

## At a glance

| | Buffer | Predis.ai |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_SOCIAL | AI_SOCIAL |
| Pricing | Free; Essentials $6/channel/mo; Team $12/channel/mo | Free tier; paid from ~$25-$29/mo (up to $99/mo) |
| Best for | Founders, creators, small marketing teams and agencies | SMBs, ecommerce, creators and agencies |
| Differentiator | Per-channel pricing and a genuinely free AI tier make it the default low-cost AI social tool for startups. | Mass scale (6.4M users, 200M+ ads generated) and strong AI video-ad/UGC generation. |

## What Buffer does

AI Assistant (OpenAI); content idea library; reply suggestions; scheduling across 11+ networks incl. Bluesky/Threads; analytics; approval workflows; API

## What Predis.ai does

AI ad/video/UGC generation, social post generation, brand kits, multi-brand workspaces, approvals, calendar, scheduling, ROAS analytics.

## How to choose

Choose **Buffer** if: Founders, creators, small marketing teams and agencies.

Choose **Predis.ai** if: SMBs, ecommerce, creators and agencies.
