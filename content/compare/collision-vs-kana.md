# Collision vs Kana

Agentic marketing platform from Rapt/Krux founders: flexible AI agents for data analysis, audience targeting, campaign orchestration, customer engagement, media planning and AI-chatbot (LLM) optimization. $15M seed led by Mayfield (Feb 2026).

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where Kana genuinely wins

Kana's founder pedigree ($1B Krux acquisition), $15M Mayfield backing and solution breadth give it enterprise credibility and an AEO position Collision must match (assessment)

## Where Collision genuinely wins

Collision is founder-priced, self-serve and execution-first across SEO/AEO/GEO/outbound/LinkedIn; Kana is enterprise data/platform-centric with a services-leaning motion (assessment)

## Pricing reality

- **Kana:** Enterprise (contact) (Enterprise SaaS)
- **Collision:** Launch $500–1,000/mo · Growth $2,000–6,000/mo · Scale $8,000–15,000/mo

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## How to decide

Choose **Kana** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Kana doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
