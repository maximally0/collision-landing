# Collision vs Treasure AI

Enterprise customer-data and agentic-experience platform (formerly Treasure Data, acquired by SoftBank 2018): Intelligent CDP plus AI Marketing Cloud; launched Marketing Super Agent (Jan 2026), a multi-agent orchestration system pitched as "the first enterprise AI that operates like a marketing department" - strategy, creative, activation and real-time optimization in one governed workspace.

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where Treasure AI genuinely wins

Treasure AI's CDP-native data foundation, SoftBank backing and full-lifecycle orchestration are enterprise-grade assets Collision does not have; its rebrand to 'Treasure AI' signals full commitment to the agentic category (assessment)

## Where Collision genuinely wins

Collision serves founders with organic-first growth, outbound and personal branding at accessible price points; Treasure AI is an enterprise CDP-era platform requiring governed data estates (assessment)

Choose **Treasure AI** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Treasure AI doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
