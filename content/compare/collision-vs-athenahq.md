# Collision vs AthenaHQ

Enterprise AI-search visibility platform ("Agents to Win on AI Search"): monitors what AI engines say about a brand, explains why, and turns insights into governed action across content, PR, commerce and optimization. 300+ global brands incl. Coinbase, SoFi, ZoomInfo, PagerDuty, Hearst.

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where AthenaHQ genuinely wins

AthenaHQ has 300+ enterprise brands, ex-Google Search/DeepMind pedigree and agency-channel distribution - deeper enterprise trust and data depth than Collision's GEO tooling (assessment).

## Where Collision genuinely wins

Collision bundles GEO/AEO with outbound, social, personal branding and lead-gen execution for founders/SMBs; AthenaHQ is enterprise measurement-and-governance first (assessment).

## How to decide

Choose **AthenaHQ** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts AthenaHQ doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
