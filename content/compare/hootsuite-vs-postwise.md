# Hootsuite vs Postwise

**Hootsuite:** Enterprise social media management suite with OwlyWriter AI and OwlyGPT: AI captions, hashtags, insights and social listening at scale.

**Postwise:** AI tool to write, schedule and grow on X, LinkedIn and Threads; generates viral-engineered posts and offers growth/DM automations.

## At a glance

| | Hootsuite | Postwise |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_SOCIAL | AI_SOCIAL |
| Pricing | Free trial; plans from ~$99/mo; Enterprise custom | 7-day free trial; Basic $37/mo; Unlimited $97/mo (annual) |
| Best for | Enterprises, agencies and larger marketing teams | Creators, founders and marketers growing personal brands on X/LinkedIn |
| Differentiator | OwlyGPT trained on real-time social trends plus 15+ years of social expertise and enterprise compliance tooling. | Engagement-engineered AI trained on proven posts plus revenue tools (auto-DM, link plugging) inside a social scheduler. |

## What Hootsuite does

OwlyWriter AI; OwlyGPT (trend-aware assistant); AI hashtags; social listening; scheduling; analytics; advocacy; compliance/approval workflows

## What Postwise does

AI viral post & thread generation; one topic to many posts; multi-platform scheduling; GrowthTools follower suite; GhostWriter autopost; auto-send DMs; repost winning content

## How to choose

Choose **Hootsuite** if: Enterprises, agencies and larger marketing teams.

Choose **Postwise** if: Creators, founders and marketers growing personal brands on X/LinkedIn.
