# Jasper vs Persado

**Jasper:** AI marketing platform (ex-AI copywriter): purpose-built agents execute end-to-end workflows - campaigns, GEO/SEO/AEO optimization, content pipelines

**Persado:** AI message-generation platform for regulated brands: generates, scores, compliance-checks and deploys campaign copy across email/SMS/push/ads

## At a glance

| | Jasper | Persado |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM |
| Pricing | Tiered subscription; free trial (Creator/Pro/Business plans) | Enterprise (demo/quote) |
| Best for | Marketing teams, SMB to enterprise | Enterprise regulated brands (banks, insurers, telecoms, retail) |
| Differentiator | Only major AI marketing platform with native GEO + SEO/AEO + agents + brand governance in one suite | Domain-specific motivation AI plus compliance engine for regulated verticals — clears UDAAP/TILA checks out of the box |

## What Jasper does

Agents library (product launch, social campaigns); GEO citation monitoring; SEO & AEO content; Brand Voice/Style Guide governance; Knowledge Base grounding; APIs/MCP; Semrush integration

## What Persado does

Brief-Generate-Score-Comply-Deploy-Learn loop; performance score vs 1M+ A/B tests; regulatory checks (UDAAP, TILA, CAN-SPAM); deploys to Salesforce MC, Braze, Adobe Target

## How to choose

Choose **Jasper** if: Marketing teams, SMB to enterprise.

Choose **Persado** if: Enterprise regulated brands (banks, insurers, telecoms, retail).
