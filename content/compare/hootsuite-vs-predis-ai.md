# Hootsuite vs Predis.ai

**Hootsuite:** Enterprise social media management suite with OwlyWriter AI and OwlyGPT: AI captions, hashtags, insights and social listening at scale.

**Predis.ai:** All-in-one AI marketing platform: video ads, UGC, ad copies, social posts; plan, schedule and auto-publish from one calendar (6.4M+ users).

## At a glance

| | Hootsuite | Predis.ai |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_SOCIAL | AI_SOCIAL |
| Pricing | Free trial; plans from ~$99/mo; Enterprise custom | Free tier; paid from ~$25-$29/mo (up to $99/mo) |
| Best for | Enterprises, agencies and larger marketing teams | SMBs, ecommerce, creators and agencies |
| Differentiator | OwlyGPT trained on real-time social trends plus 15+ years of social expertise and enterprise compliance tooling. | Mass scale (6.4M users, 200M+ ads generated) and strong AI video-ad/UGC generation. |

## What Hootsuite does

OwlyWriter AI; OwlyGPT (trend-aware assistant); AI hashtags; social listening; scheduling; analytics; advocacy; compliance/approval workflows

## What Predis.ai does

AI ad/video/UGC generation, social post generation, brand kits, multi-brand workspaces, approvals, calendar, scheduling, ROAS analytics.

## How to choose

Choose **Hootsuite** if: Enterprises, agencies and larger marketing teams.

Choose **Predis.ai** if: SMBs, ecommerce, creators and agencies.
