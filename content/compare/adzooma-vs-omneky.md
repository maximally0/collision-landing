# Adzooma vs Omneky

**Adzooma:** AI-powered PPC platform that audits, optimizes and scales Google, Microsoft and Meta campaigns with automated, one-click recommendations.

**Omneky:** Autonomous AI advertising platform that writes, designs, launches and optimizes ads across Meta, Google, LinkedIn, TikTok and Reddit — now including ChatGPT (OpenAI self-serve Ads Manager). Manages $1B+ annualized ad spend.

## At a glance

| | Adzooma | Omneky |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_ADVERTISING | AI_ADVERTISING |
| Pricing | Free; Pro from ~£69/mo | 7-day free trial; plans not published (contact) |
| Best for | SMBs, agencies, freelancers and founders running paid ads | DTC/ecommerce, startups to enterprise brands (Stance, HPSO, Brex, Sony, SoftBank, You.com) |
| Differentiator | Free AI PPC optimization across three ad networks with two-click implementation | Cross-channel autonomous ad execution including ChatGPT-native ads (post OpenAI self-serve Ads Manager, May 2026) with $1B+ managed spend |

## What Adzooma does

24/7 machine-learning analysis; one-click recommendation application; performance scorecards; automated alerts; AI ad copy; multi-channel reporting

## What Omneky does

AI ad generation at scale; cross-channel management (Meta/Google/LinkedIn/TikTok/Reddit/ChatGPT); omnichannel analytics; ROAS optimization; $1B+ annualized managed spend

## How to choose

Choose **Adzooma** if: SMBs, agencies, freelancers and founders running paid ads.

Choose **Omneky** if: DTC/ecommerce, startups to enterprise brands (Stance, HPSO, Brex, Sony, SoftBank, You.com).
