# Clay vs Snov.io

**Clay:** AI GTM data platform: unify first/third-party data, 200+ provider marketplace, enrichment, signals/intent, AI workflow builder for outbound.

**Snov.io:** All-in-one lead gen and outreach toolkit: email finder/verifier, B2B database, drip campaigns, LinkedIn automation, AI email writer and CRM.

## At a glance

| | Clay | Snov.io |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_LEAD_GENERATION | AI_LEAD_GENERATION |
| Pricing | From ~$54/mo (usage/credit-based; tiers to ~$446/mo) | Free trial; Starter ~$29/mo up to Ultra ~$554/mo (25% off annual) |
| Best for | Growth, SDR and RevOps teams at startups through enterprises | Bootstrapped founders, SMBs and agencies |
| Differentiator | The go-to AI GTM data layer with best-in-class enrichment and a powerful agentic workflow builder. | One affordable toolkit spanning find, verify, warmup, send and CRM, with AI Studio and MCP integration. |

## What Clay does

Audiences, 200+ data providers, signals and intent, AI agent workflows, enrichment, list building, outreach automation, Chrome extension.

## What Snov.io does

Email finder/verifier; B2B database; multichannel drip campaigns; LinkedIn automation; AI writer + AI Studio; warmup; CRM; MCP for Claude/ChatGPT

## How to choose

Choose **Clay** if: Growth, SDR and RevOps teams at startups through enterprises.

Choose **Snov.io** if: Bootstrapped founders, SMBs and agencies.
