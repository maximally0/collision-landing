# Hyper AI vs Persado

**Hyper AI:** AI-native marketing agent platform: CMO, paid-ads, social, SEO/AI-search and analytics agents that connect 200+ tools and run 24/7, with multi-client agency workspaces.

**Persado:** AI message-generation platform for regulated brands: generates, scores, compliance-checks and deploys campaign copy across email/SMS/push/ads

## At a glance

| | Hyper AI | Persado |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM |
| Pricing | Free tier ($10 credits); paid plans (tiers unverified) | Enterprise (demo/quote) |
| Best for | Marketing teams, founders and agencies | Enterprise regulated brands (banks, insurers, telecoms, retail) |
| Differentiator | Prebuilt expert agents (not DIY automations) with deep ad-platform API execution and multi-client agency mode | Domain-specific motivation AI plus compliance engine for regulated verticals — clears UDAAP/TILA checks out of the box |

## What Hyper AI does

80+ ad/marketing connectors; hourly/daily/weekly recurring tasks; brand voice + positioning context; multi-client agency mode; MCP for external agents; $10 free credits

## What Persado does

Brief-Generate-Score-Comply-Deploy-Learn loop; performance score vs 1M+ A/B tests; regulatory checks (UDAAP, TILA, CAN-SPAM); deploys to Salesforce MC, Braze, Adobe Target

## How to choose

Choose **Hyper AI** if: Marketing teams, founders and agencies.

Choose **Persado** if: Enterprise regulated brands (banks, insurers, telecoms, retail).
