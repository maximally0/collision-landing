# Collision vs Lapis

Self-improving ads engine (YC Fall 2025): AI agents create, launch and optimize paid campaigns end-to-end — ChatSense (ChatGPT-native ads), OmniSense (Google/Meta/LinkedIn) and RapidDomain (self-optimizing landing pages).

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where Lapis genuinely wins

Lapis' full-cycle paid execution with ChatGPT-ads distribution and self-improving creative is deeper ad tooling than Collision ships, at founder-friendly entry (assessment)

## Where Collision genuinely wins

Collision spans organic growth (SEO/AEO/GEO), outbound, LinkedIn and strategy; Lapis is paid-ads execution plus landing pages (assessment)

## Pricing reality

- **Lapis:** Tiered plans with credits (Starter/Growth/Enterprise managed tier); pricing not fully published (Subscription + credits)
- **Collision:** Launch $500–1,000/mo · Growth $2,000–6,000/mo · Scale $8,000–15,000/mo

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## How to decide

Choose **Lapis** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Lapis doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
