# AiSDR vs Regie.ai

**AiSDR:** AI SDR agent that finds prospects in real time, writes personalized email/LinkedIn/text outreach and books qualified meetings that show up.

**Regie.ai:** AI-native sales engagement platform: AI agents source leads, write personalized outreach and sequence follow-ups; RegieOne unifies it.

## At a glance

| | AiSDR | Regie.ai |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_OUTBOUND | AI_OUTBOUND |
| Pricing | From ~$250/mo; flat plans up to ~$900/mo | AI Agents packages from $35K/yr; RegieOne free with annual contract |
| Best for | B2B SaaS growth teams (50-300 employees, $2M+ ARR) | B2B sales teams and SDR orgs (mid-market/enterprise) |
| Differentiator | Pure-play AI SDR (YC-backed) with strong booked-meeting performance claims and HubSpot-native workflow. | World's first AI-native sales engagement platform with patented agentic AI and 300% YoY ARR growth. |

## What AiSDR does

AI prospecting and qualification, personalized emails/LinkedIn/texts, campaign planning, performance monitoring, inbound handling, CRM sync.

## What Regie.ai does

AI Agents; audience builder; personalized content generation; signal-based prioritization; AI dialer (9-line parallel); RegieOne SEP; enterprise integrations

## How to choose

Choose **AiSDR** if: B2B SaaS growth teams (50-300 employees, $2M+ ARR).

Choose **Regie.ai** if: B2B sales teams and SDR orgs (mid-market/enterprise).
