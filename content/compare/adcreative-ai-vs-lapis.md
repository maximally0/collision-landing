# AdCreative.ai vs Lapis

**AdCreative.ai:** AI ad creative generator producing high-converting banners, video ads and copy with predictive conversion scoring, wired into ad platforms.

**Lapis:** Self-improving ads engine (YC Fall 2025): AI agents create, launch and optimize paid campaigns end-to-end — ChatSense (ChatGPT-native ads), OmniSense (Google/Meta/LinkedIn) and RapidDomain (self-optimizing landing pages).

## At a glance

| | AdCreative.ai | Lapis |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_ADVERTISING | AI_ADVERTISING |
| Pricing | $39-$999/mo (Starter/Professional/Ultimate) | Tiered plans with credits (Starter/Growth/Enterprise managed tier); pricing not fully published |
| Best for | Founders, startups, SMBs and agencies running paid ads | DTC and SaaS marketing teams (1,500+ teams; 30+ enterprises incl. Fortune 500) |
| Differentiator | Predictive conversion scoring of creatives before launch, trained on performance data | YC F25 self-improving ads engine covering the full lifecycle incl. ChatGPT-native ads (ChatSense) and self-optimizing landing pages (RapidDomain); 1,500+ teams, 30+ enterprises |

## What AdCreative.ai does

Banner/video ad generation; AI copy; pre-launch conversion score prediction; brand kit training; 20+ platform formats; creative refresh suggestions

## What Lapis does

End-to-end campaign lifecycle; ChatGPT sponsored-result ads; AI ad copy + static creative; per-ad custom landing pages; self-improving loop from real interaction data; brand-from-site learning; A/B testing at scale; competitor ad tracking; Brand Radar 10,000+ ad library; 10 languages; 20x performance claims

## How to choose

Choose **AdCreative.ai** if: Founders, startups, SMBs and agencies running paid ads.

Choose **Lapis** if: DTC and SaaS marketing teams (1,500+ teams; 30+ enterprises incl. Fortune 500).
