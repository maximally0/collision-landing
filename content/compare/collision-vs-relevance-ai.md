# Collision vs Relevance AI

No-code 'AI workforce' platform: build teams of AI agents (sales, research, support, marketing) that execute business tasks autonomously.

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where Relevance AI genuinely wins

Enterprise traction (Canva, KPMG, Databricks), $37M funding, deep template breadth.

## Where Collision genuinely wins

Collision ships outcomes (SEO/content/outbound executed); Relevance is a DIY agent platform requiring setup. Assessment.

## Pricing reality

- **Relevance AI:** Enterprise custom; usage-based credits (Subscription)
- **Collision:** Launch $500–1,000/mo · Growth $2,000–6,000/mo · Scale $8,000–15,000/mo (pre-launch hypothesis pricing)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-14. Collision pricing is pre-launch positioning, not market-validated._

## How to decide

Choose **Relevance AI** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Relevance AI doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
