# FeedHive vs XBeast

**FeedHive:** Social media automation platform (v2.0) with AI content generation, workflows, AI agents, smart scheduling, recycling and analytics.

**XBeast:** AI agent for X/Twitter that writes, schedules, retweets, replies and plugs content in the user's voice on autopilot.

## At a glance

| | FeedHive | XBeast |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_SOCIAL | AI_SOCIAL |
| Pricing | From $15/mo (tiers $15-$69/mo) | 7-day free trial; Basic $12/mo; Pro $24/mo; Ultra $72/mo |
| Best for | Founders, marketers and agencies | Creators, founders, indie hackers growing an X presence |
| Differentiator | Workflow/condition-based automation plus AI agents inside a social scheduler. | Positioned as an AI agent that fully executes X growth (writes, replies, retweets, plugs), not just a scheduler. |

## What FeedHive does

AI agents, automation workflows, post conditions, content recycling, social inbox, analytics, AI design library, API/CLI, AI-agent integrations.

## What XBeast does

Voice-trained presets; weekly auto-generation with human review; auto-reply, auto-retweet, auto-plug (link/offer on viral posts); YouTube/TikTok to tweet; analytics

## How to choose

Choose **FeedHive** if: Founders, marketers and agencies.

Choose **XBeast** if: Creators, founders, indie hackers growing an X presence.
