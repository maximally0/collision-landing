# Adzooma vs Lapis

**Adzooma:** AI-powered PPC platform that audits, optimizes and scales Google, Microsoft and Meta campaigns with automated, one-click recommendations.

**Lapis:** Self-improving ads engine (YC Fall 2025): AI agents create, launch and optimize paid campaigns end-to-end — ChatSense (ChatGPT-native ads), OmniSense (Google/Meta/LinkedIn) and RapidDomain (self-optimizing landing pages).

## At a glance

| | Adzooma | Lapis |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_ADVERTISING | AI_ADVERTISING |
| Pricing | Free; Pro from ~£69/mo | Tiered plans with credits (Starter/Growth/Enterprise managed tier); pricing not fully published |
| Best for | SMBs, agencies, freelancers and founders running paid ads | DTC and SaaS marketing teams (1,500+ teams; 30+ enterprises incl. Fortune 500) |
| Differentiator | Free AI PPC optimization across three ad networks with two-click implementation | YC F25 self-improving ads engine covering the full lifecycle incl. ChatGPT-native ads (ChatSense) and self-optimizing landing pages (RapidDomain); 1,500+ teams, 30+ enterprises |

## What Adzooma does

24/7 machine-learning analysis; one-click recommendation application; performance scorecards; automated alerts; AI ad copy; multi-channel reporting

## What Lapis does

End-to-end campaign lifecycle; ChatGPT sponsored-result ads; AI ad copy + static creative; per-ad custom landing pages; self-improving loop from real interaction data; brand-from-site learning; A/B testing at scale; competitor ad tracking; Brand Radar 10,000+ ad library; 10 languages; 20x performance claims

## How to choose

Choose **Adzooma** if: SMBs, agencies, freelancers and founders running paid ads.

Choose **Lapis** if: DTC and SaaS marketing teams (1,500+ teams; 30+ enterprises incl. Fortune 500).
