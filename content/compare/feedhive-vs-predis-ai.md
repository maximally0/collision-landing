# FeedHive vs Predis.ai

**FeedHive:** Social media automation platform (v2.0) with AI content generation, workflows, AI agents, smart scheduling, recycling and analytics.

**Predis.ai:** All-in-one AI marketing platform: video ads, UGC, ad copies, social posts; plan, schedule and auto-publish from one calendar (6.4M+ users).

## At a glance

| | FeedHive | Predis.ai |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_SOCIAL | AI_SOCIAL |
| Pricing | From $15/mo (tiers $15-$69/mo) | Free tier; paid from ~$25-$29/mo (up to $99/mo) |
| Best for | Founders, marketers and agencies | SMBs, ecommerce, creators and agencies |
| Differentiator | Workflow/condition-based automation plus AI agents inside a social scheduler. | Mass scale (6.4M users, 200M+ ads generated) and strong AI video-ad/UGC generation. |

## What FeedHive does

AI agents, automation workflows, post conditions, content recycling, social inbox, analytics, AI design library, API/CLI, AI-agent integrations.

## What Predis.ai does

AI ad/video/UGC generation, social post generation, brand kits, multi-brand workspaces, approvals, calendar, scheduling, ROAS analytics.

## How to choose

Choose **FeedHive** if: Founders, marketers and agencies.

Choose **Predis.ai** if: SMBs, ecommerce, creators and agencies.
