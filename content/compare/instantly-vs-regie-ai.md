# Instantly vs Regie.ai

**Instantly:** Sales engagement + lead intelligence: AI sales/reply/lead-finder agents, lead database, email warmup/deliverability, outreach and CRM.

**Regie.ai:** AI-native sales engagement platform: AI agents source leads, write personalized outreach and sequence follow-ups; RegieOne unifies it.

## At a glance

| | Instantly | Regie.ai |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_OUTBOUND | AI_OUTBOUND |
| Pricing | From $47/mo (tiers to ~$358/mo) | AI Agents packages from $35K/yr; RegieOne free with annual contract |
| Best for | Founders, SMBs and agencies running cold email/outbound | B2B sales teams and SDR orgs (mid-market/enterprise) |
| Differentiator | Owns cold-email deliverability infrastructure (unlimited accounts + warmup) and now ships AI SDR agents. | World's first AI-native sales engagement platform with patented agentic AI and 300% YoY ARR growth. |

## What Instantly does

AI agents (sales/reply/lead-gen), lead database, email verification, warmup, deliverability, outreach automations, website visitor ID.

## What Regie.ai does

AI Agents; audience builder; personalized content generation; signal-based prioritization; AI dialer (9-line parallel); RegieOne SEP; enterprise integrations

## How to choose

Choose **Instantly** if: Founders, SMBs and agencies running cold email/outbound.

Choose **Regie.ai** if: B2B sales teams and SDR orgs (mid-market/enterprise).
