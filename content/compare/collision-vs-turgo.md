# Collision vs Turgo

Autonomous marketing execution platform ('autonomous GTM platform', product of Growstack Inc.) that deploys 5 AI employees - AI Inbound Marketer, AI Outbound Rep, AI Calling Agent, AI Media Buyer, AI Marketing Ops - to run the full B2B revenue cycle from first lead signal to booked meeting across email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, voice, paid media and CRM

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where Turgo genuinely wins

Turgo's 5-AI-employee full-funnel loop including voice calling and paid media covers execution breadth Collision must match; India cost base and ISO certifications help it sell into enterprise (assessment)

## Where Collision genuinely wins

Collision is channel-agnostic strategy plus execution with deeper SEO/GEO and personal-branding focus; Turgo is outbound/GTM-centric and young (30+ customers) (assessment)

## Pricing reality

- **Turgo:** Credit-based plans (500 free credits); self-serve and enterprise tiers (Usage-based credits + subscription)
- **Collision:** Launch $500–1,000/mo · Growth $2,000–6,000/mo · Scale $8,000–15,000/mo

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## How to decide

Choose **Turgo** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Turgo doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
