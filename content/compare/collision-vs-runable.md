# Collision vs Runable

General-purpose AI agent platform (Bengaluru) that pivoted from AI website/app building into 'Grow': AI agents that find customers, run ad campaigns, manage social media, handle SEO and optimize brand presence in AI chatbot results for small businesses. $21M Series A (Aug 2026); 1.7M registered users; ~$2M annualized revenue run-rate within 3 weeks of launching payments (Mar 2026).

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where Runable genuinely wins

Runable has $21M (Susquehanna + Nexus), 1.7M users and a built-in funnel from its build product; it cites general-purpose agents (Manus, Genspark) as rivals, meaning marketing depth will be bought, not built (assessment)

## Where Collision genuinely wins

Collision has channel depth (SEO/AEO/GEO, LinkedIn growth, multi-platform outbound) and strategy-led execution; Runable's Grow arm is day-one and shares one general-purpose agent with its build product (assessment)

## Pricing reality

- **Runable:** Freemium; paid plans live (not fully published) (Freemium subscription)
- **Collision:** Launch $500–1,000/mo · Growth $2,000–6,000/mo · Scale $8,000–15,000/mo

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## How to decide

Choose **Runable** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Runable doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
