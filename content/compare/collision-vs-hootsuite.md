- **Hootsuite:** Free trial; plans from ~$99/mo; Enterprise custom (subscription)
- **Collision:** Launch $500–1,000/mo · Growth $2,000–6,000/mo · Scale $8,000–15,000/mo

## How to decide

Choose **Hootsuite** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Hootsuite doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
