# Hootsuite vs Typefully

**Hootsuite:** Enterprise social media management suite with OwlyWriter AI and OwlyGPT: AI captions, hashtags, insights and social listening at scale.

**Typefully:** AI-powered writing and scheduling tool for X, LinkedIn, Bluesky and Threads; distraction-free editor with AI writing assistance and analytics.

## At a glance

| | Hootsuite | Typefully |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_SOCIAL | AI_SOCIAL |
| Pricing | Free trial; plans from ~$99/mo; Enterprise custom | Free plan; Starter ~$14/mo; Team ~$29/mo; Agency/Enterprise up to $199/mo |
| Best for | Enterprises, agencies and larger marketing teams | Individual creators, founders and small teams building audiences |
| Differentiator | OwlyGPT trained on real-time social trends plus 15+ years of social expertise and enterprise compliance tooling. | Creator-first writing experience with native support for emerging networks (Bluesky, Threads) and 10,000+ customers. |

## What Hootsuite does

OwlyWriter AI; OwlyGPT (trend-aware assistant); AI hashtags; social listening; scheduling; analytics; advocacy; compliance/approval workflows

## What Typefully does

AI ideas & rewrites; distraction-free composer; real-time platform previews; cross-platform scheduling; analytics; social blog repurposing; team plans

## How to choose

Choose **Hootsuite** if: Enterprises, agencies and larger marketing teams.

Choose **Typefully** if: Individual creators, founders and small teams building audiences.
