# ActiveCampaign vs Gradial

**ActiveCampaign:** Incumbent marketing automation platform (est. 2003) repositioned as an autonomous marketing platform: Active Intelligence AI agents plan, create, launch and optimize email, SMS and WhatsApp campaigns end-to-end

**Gradial:** Agentic AI operating system ('system of work') for enterprise marketing: AI agents run the full marketing operations workflow - authoring, QA, brand compliance, accessibility, asset tagging, assembly - across existing stacks (Adobe, Salesforce, ServiceNow, Databricks), with GEO-driven execution that ships fixes (not just recommendations) when AI-search gaps surface. $65M Series C (Jun 2026) at $675M valuation.

## At a glance

| | ActiveCampaign | Gradial |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_MARKETING_AUTOMATION | AI_MARKETING_AUTOMATION |
| Pricing | Tiered subscription (Plus/Pro/Enterprise) | Enterprise (demo/quote) |

| Differentiator | Incumbent automation vendor with a large SMB install base, a results guarantee and billions of behavioral data points - autonomous marketing layered on an existing customer database rather than greenfield | Only agentic marketing platform that ships fixes (not recommendations) when GEO data shows AI-search gaps; compliance-first design for regulated enterprises; $675M valuation with Fortune-500 roster (AWS, T-Mobile, Kaiser, U.S. Bank) |

## What ActiveCampaign does

Autonomous Marketing platform positioning; Wavelength (marketing AI tuned to each business's data, launched Aug 27 2026); results guarantee on autonomous marketing; Google Ads connector for AI-guided campaign creation (Jul 8 2026); first autonomous marketing connector for Anthropic Claude (Nov 2025); MCP server; Feedback Intelligence AI-evaluation acquisition (Feb 3 2026); billions of behavioral data points processed daily

## What Gradial does

AI agents running authoring-to-assembly workflows; brand-compliance and QA enforcement; GEO-driven execution (surfaces AI-search gaps and ships fixes directly); HubSpot email operations; Slack + GEO Sentiment; customer-journey simulation; accessibility/translation/localization automation; campaign execution time reduced 80-90% per T-Mobile

## How to choose

Choose **ActiveCampaign** if: SMB and mid-market marketing teams (180K+ customer base).

Choose **Gradial** if: Enterprise marketing and digital operations teams (AWS, T-Mobile, Kaiser Foundation Health Plan, U.S. Bank, Prudential, Vanguard).
