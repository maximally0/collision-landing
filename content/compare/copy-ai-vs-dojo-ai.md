# Copy.ai vs DOJO AI

**Copy.ai:** GTM AI platform: autonomous workflows and agents for outbound prospecting, lead processing, content creation, deal coaching; now part of Fullcast

**DOJO AI:** Intelligent marketing system for challenger brands: a living knowledge graph (DOJO Graph) models the brand's marketing reality; specialized AI agents act on it across paid, organic, SEO and content, feeding every outcome back into the model.

## At a glance

| | Copy.ai | DOJO AI |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_MARKETING_OS | AI_MARKETING_OS |
| Pricing | Freemium; tiered paid plans | From $499/mo; 7-day free trial; Starter/Growth/Enterprise tiers |
| Best for | B2B sales and marketing teams, SMB to enterprise | Challenger brands and mid-market marketing teams (CoinDesk, MorningStar, PensionBee, Broadvoice, Refine Labs) |
| Differentiator | Autonomous GTM workflows spanning sales + marketing (not just copy) with a massive freemium user base | Knowledge-graph-first marketing intelligence where agents execute (not just report) and every outcome feeds back into the model — 'most AI resets, DOJO compounds' |

## What Copy.ai does

Copy Agents; pre-built GTM workflows; Infobase (brand voice/knowledge); CRM/email integrations; 15M+ users; freemium tools

## What DOJO AI does

DOJO Graph living knowledge graph; proactive agentic workflows (not prompt-driven); brand/demand/creative intelligence; unlimited competitive monitoring; revenue intelligence; queryable dashboards; 20% MoM growth; 100+ brands

## How to choose

Choose **Copy.ai** if: B2B sales and marketing teams, SMB to enterprise.

Choose **DOJO AI** if: Challenger brands and mid-market marketing teams (CoinDesk, MorningStar, PensionBee, Broadvoice, Refine Labs).
