# Auxia vs Runable

**Auxia:** Agentic marketing platform for enterprise B2C lifecycle teams: Auxia Decisioning personalizes/orchestrates every customer interaction in real time (200B+ decisions delivered, 500M+/day), and Auxia Agent Studio is a command center where AI agents plan, build and launch campaigns across the team's existing ESP/CDP stack

**Runable:** General-purpose AI agent platform (Bengaluru) that pivoted from AI website/app building into 'Grow': AI agents that find customers, run ad campaigns, manage social media, handle SEO and optimize brand presence in AI chatbot results for small businesses. $21M Series A (Aug 2026); 1.7M registered users; ~$2M annualized revenue run-rate within 3 weeks of launching payments (Mar 2026).

## What Auxia does

500M+ decisions/day; marketing context graph (brand voice, campaign history); natural-language agentic workflows across ESPs/CDPs; collaborative approval workspace; localized campaign generation at global scale; model-driven experimentation replacing manual A/B testing

## What Runable does

Natural-language build (websites, apps, slides, videos, carousels); deployment + analytics handled; Grow automates bookings, FAQs, repeat orders, social posting/replies, ad campaigns, SEO and AI-chatbot presence; 1.7M registered users; US/UK/Japan/Brazil markets

## How to choose

Choose **Auxia** if: Enterprise B2C lifecycle marketing teams (Fortune 500).

Choose **Runable** if: Non-technical small business owners and solo founders (US, UK, Japan focus).
