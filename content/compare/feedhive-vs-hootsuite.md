# FeedHive vs Hootsuite

**FeedHive:** Social media automation platform (v2.0) with AI content generation, workflows, AI agents, smart scheduling, recycling and analytics.

**Hootsuite:** Enterprise social media management suite with OwlyWriter AI and OwlyGPT: AI captions, hashtags, insights and social listening at scale.

## At a glance

| | FeedHive | Hootsuite |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_SOCIAL | AI_SOCIAL |
| Pricing | From $15/mo (tiers $15-$69/mo) | Free trial; plans from ~$99/mo; Enterprise custom |
| Best for | Founders, marketers and agencies | Enterprises, agencies and larger marketing teams |
| Differentiator | Workflow/condition-based automation plus AI agents inside a social scheduler. | OwlyGPT trained on real-time social trends plus 15+ years of social expertise and enterprise compliance tooling. |

## What FeedHive does

AI agents, automation workflows, post conditions, content recycling, social inbox, analytics, AI design library, API/CLI, AI-agent integrations.

## What Hootsuite does

OwlyWriter AI; OwlyGPT (trend-aware assistant); AI hashtags; social listening; scheduling; analytics; advocacy; compliance/approval workflows

## How to choose

Choose **FeedHive** if: Founders, marketers and agencies.

Choose **Hootsuite** if: Enterprises, agencies and larger marketing teams.
