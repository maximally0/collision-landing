# Buffer vs XBeast

**Buffer:** Social media scheduling and publishing platform with built-in AI Assistant (OpenAI) for content ideas, drafting and repurposing; per-channel pricing.

**XBeast:** AI agent for X/Twitter that writes, schedules, retweets, replies and plugs content in the user's voice on autopilot.

## At a glance

| | Buffer | XBeast |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_SOCIAL | AI_SOCIAL |
| Pricing | Free; Essentials $6/channel/mo; Team $12/channel/mo | 7-day free trial; Basic $12/mo; Pro $24/mo; Ultra $72/mo |
| Best for | Founders, creators, small marketing teams and agencies | Creators, founders, indie hackers growing an X presence |
| Differentiator | Per-channel pricing and a genuinely free AI tier make it the default low-cost AI social tool for startups. | Positioned as an AI agent that fully executes X growth (writes, replies, retweets, plugs), not just a scheduler. |

## What Buffer does

AI Assistant (OpenAI); content idea library; reply suggestions; scheduling across 11+ networks incl. Bluesky/Threads; analytics; approval workflows; API

## What XBeast does

Voice-trained presets; weekly auto-generation with human review; auto-reply, auto-retweet, auto-plug (link/offer on viral posts); YouTube/TikTok to tweet; analytics

## How to choose

Choose **Buffer** if: Founders, creators, small marketing teams and agencies.

Choose **XBeast** if: Creators, founders, indie hackers growing an X presence.
