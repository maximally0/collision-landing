# Buffer vs FeedHive

**Buffer:** Social media scheduling and publishing platform with built-in AI Assistant (OpenAI) for content ideas, drafting and repurposing; per-channel pricing.

**FeedHive:** Social media automation platform (v2.0) with AI content generation, workflows, AI agents, smart scheduling, recycling and analytics.

## At a glance

| | Buffer | FeedHive |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_SOCIAL | AI_SOCIAL |
| Pricing | Free; Essentials $6/channel/mo; Team $12/channel/mo | From $15/mo (tiers $15-$69/mo) |
| Best for | Founders, creators, small marketing teams and agencies | Founders, marketers and agencies |
| Differentiator | Per-channel pricing and a genuinely free AI tier make it the default low-cost AI social tool for startups. | Workflow/condition-based automation plus AI agents inside a social scheduler. |

## What Buffer does

AI Assistant (OpenAI); content idea library; reply suggestions; scheduling across 11+ networks incl. Bluesky/Threads; analytics; approval workflows; API

## What FeedHive does

AI agents, automation workflows, post conditions, content recycling, social inbox, analytics, AI design library, API/CLI, AI-agent integrations.

## How to choose

Choose **Buffer** if: Founders, creators, small marketing teams and agencies.

Choose **FeedHive** if: Founders, marketers and agencies.
