# Collision vs AutoBacklinks

AI agent for link building (launched Aug 26 2026, Berlin): discovers relevant sites from the user's website and competitors, filters by SEO metrics, finds and verifies contact emails, and sends personalized outreach sequences — replacing the manual Ahrefs+Hunter+Instantly stack.

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where AutoBacklinks genuinely wins

Day-one product with no traction or funding; link-building agents face deliverability ceilings and quality-control limits that agencies solve with relationships (assessment)

## Where Collision genuinely wins

Collision bundles link building inside a full-stack growth system (content, SEO/AEO/GEO, social, outbound, strategy); AutoBacklinks is a single-function agent (assessment)

## Pricing reality

- **AutoBacklinks:** Free trial; paid from $79/mo (agency/high-volume plans above) (Subscription + monthly credits)

## How to decide

Choose **AutoBacklinks** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts AutoBacklinks doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
