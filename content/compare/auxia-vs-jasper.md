# Auxia vs Jasper

**Auxia:** Agentic marketing platform for enterprise B2C lifecycle teams: Auxia Decisioning personalizes/orchestrates every customer interaction in real time (200B+ decisions delivered, 500M+/day), and Auxia Agent Studio is a command center where AI agents plan, build and launch campaigns across the team's existing ESP/CDP stack

**Jasper:** AI marketing platform (ex-AI copywriter): purpose-built agents execute end-to-end workflows - campaigns, GEO/SEO/AEO optimization, content pipelines

## At a glance

| | Auxia | Jasper |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM | AI_MARKETING_PLATFORM |
| Pricing | Enterprise (demo/early-access waitlist) | Tiered subscription; free trial (Creator/Pro/Business plans) |
| Best for | Enterprise B2C lifecycle marketing teams (Fortune 500) | Marketing teams, SMB to enterprise |
| Differentiator | Proven decisioning scale (200B+ decisions; customers Atlassian, Comcast, Docomo) plus a new agentic execution layer - enterprise-grade autonomous marketing backed by real personalization data | Only major AI marketing platform with native GEO + SEO/AEO + agents + brand governance in one suite |

## What Auxia does

500M+ decisions/day; marketing context graph (brand voice, campaign history); natural-language agentic workflows across ESPs/CDPs; collaborative approval workspace; localized campaign generation at global scale; model-driven experimentation replacing manual A/B testing

## What Jasper does

Agents library (product launch, social campaigns); GEO citation monitoring; SEO & AEO content; Brand Voice/Style Guide governance; Knowledge Base grounding; APIs/MCP; Semrush integration

## How to choose

Choose **Auxia** if: Enterprise B2C lifecycle marketing teams (Fortune 500).

Choose **Jasper** if: Marketing teams, SMB to enterprise.
