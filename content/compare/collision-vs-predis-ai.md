# Collision vs Predis.ai

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where Predis.ai genuinely wins

Predis has enormous reach and proven AI ad/video generation for social-first brands.

## Where Collision genuinely wins

Collision covers strategy, SEO, outbound and LinkedIn; Predis is social/ads content. assessment.

## Pricing reality

- **Predis.ai:** Free tier; paid from ~$25-$29/mo (up to $99/mo) (Freemium)
- **Collision:** Launch $500–1,000/mo · Growth $2,000–6,000/mo · Scale $8,000–15,000/mo (pre-launch hypothesis pricing)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-14. Collision pricing is pre-launch positioning, not market-validated._

## How to decide

Choose **Predis.ai** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Predis.ai doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
