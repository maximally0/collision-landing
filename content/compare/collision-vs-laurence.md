## Where Laurence genuinely wins

Laurence's quant-style profit optimization on live ad spend and its founder-friendly 'no agencies, no wasted clicks' pitch are credible, well-funded (Susa/Box/YC; OpenAI and Amazon angels) and already deployed on real DTC budgets (assessment)

## Where Collision genuinely wins

Collision covers organic growth (SEO/AEO/GEO, content, outbound, LinkedIn) plus strategy, so it is not dependent on ad-spend take; Laurence is Amazon-PPC-first today (assessment)
