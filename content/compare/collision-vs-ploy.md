# Collision vs Ploy

AI marketing platform that turns a company's website into its growth engine: swarms of marketing and web-design agents design pages, write copy, launch campaigns (ABM, SEO, paid ads) and optimize, with built-in AEO. YC P26; $27M seed (Jun 2026). Founded by ex-Webflow co-founder/CTO Bryant Chou.

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where Ploy genuinely wins

Ploy has $27M seed, an ex-Webflow CTO and deep YC-batch adoption - better resourced and faster-scaling than most Collision competitors in the founder wedge (assessment).

## Where Collision genuinely wins

Collision spans the full funnel beyond the website (outbound, LinkedIn, social distribution, lead gen, personal branding); Ploy is website-centric (assessment).

## Pricing reality

- **Ploy:** Free; Starter $50/mo; Pro/Enterprise (usage credits) (Freemium subscription (credits))
- **Collision:** Launch $500–1,000/mo · Growth $2,000–6,000/mo · Scale $8,000–15,000/mo

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## How to decide

Choose **Ploy** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Ploy doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
