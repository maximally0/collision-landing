# AdCreative.ai vs Laurence

**AdCreative.ai:** AI ad creative generator producing high-converting banners, video ads and copy with predictive conversion scoring, wired into ad platforms.

**Laurence:** Autonomous performance marketing for e-commerce (YC Winter 2026): automates advertising the way quant firms trade markets, starting with Amazon PPC where it autonomously executes tens of millions of dollars of ad spend with 15-40% net-profit improvements within a month of onboarding; closed beta expanding to ChatGPT, Google, Meta, TikTok and Walmart.

## What AdCreative.ai does

Banner/video ad generation; AI copy; pre-launch conversion score prediction; brand kit training; 20+ platform formats; creative refresh suggestions

## What Laurence does

15-40% net profit improvement within ~30 days (company-reported); tens of millions of dollars of Amazon ad spend under management; systematic portfolio-style allocation; instant free audit

## How to choose

Choose **AdCreative.ai** if: Founders, startups, SMBs and agencies running paid ads.

Choose **Laurence** if: E-commerce brands of all sizes (health supplements, pet electronics, home cleaning products).
