# Collision vs Zapier (Agents)

## How to decide

Choose **Zapier (Agents)** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Zapier (Agents) doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
