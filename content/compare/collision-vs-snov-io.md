# Collision vs Snov.io

All-in-one lead gen and outreach toolkit: email finder/verifier, B2B database, drip campaigns, LinkedIn automation, AI email writer and CRM.

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where Snov.io genuinely wins

Low-cost full-stack convenience and pay-as-you-go credits make it an easy default for bootstrapped founders before they buy a platform like Collision.

## Where Collision genuinely wins

assessment - Collision adds content, SEO and social strategy; Snov.io is execution tooling, so Collision can bundle the strategy layer on top

## Pricing reality

- **Snov.io:** Free trial; Starter ~$29/mo up to Ultra ~$554/mo (25% off annual) (subscription + credits)
- **Collision:** Launch $500–1,000/mo · Growth $2,000–6,000/mo · Scale $8,000–15,000/mo (pre-launch hypothesis pricing)

_Pricing verified 2026-08-14. Collision pricing is pre-launch positioning, not market-validated._

## How to decide

Choose **Snov.io** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Snov.io doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
