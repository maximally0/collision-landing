# Collision vs Mega

AI-powered growth engine that replaces marketing agencies for SMBs: a network of AI agents executes SEO, GEO, paid ads and website management end-to-end, continuously optimizing and reporting. ~$10M revenue in 10 months; $11.5M Series A (Mar 2026).

Collision is positioned as the AI you hire to run growth — one intelligence that researches, plans, writes, distributes and learns across LinkedIn, X, SEO, AI search, email and outbound, with approval gates on every action.

## Where Mega genuinely wins

Mega has Goodwater/a16z backing, ~$10M revenue in 10 months and a proven zero-login 'never manages it' promise that out-executes Collision on hands-off SMB growth (assessment)

## Where Collision genuinely wins

Collision adds personal branding, LinkedIn growth, multi-platform outbound depth and founder strategy; Mega is SEO/ads/website-centric today (assessment)

## Pricing reality

- **Mega:** From $699/mo (SEO), $1,399/mo (ads), $2,099/mo all-in-one (per Tam Radar) (Subscription)
- **Collision:** Launch $500–1,000/mo · Growth $2,000–6,000/mo · Scale $8,000–15,000/mo

_Pricing verified 2026-08-30._

## How to decide

Choose **Mega** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Mega doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
