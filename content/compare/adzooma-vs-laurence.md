# Adzooma vs Laurence

**Adzooma:** AI-powered PPC platform that audits, optimizes and scales Google, Microsoft and Meta campaigns with automated, one-click recommendations.

**Laurence:** Autonomous performance marketing for e-commerce (YC Winter 2026): automates advertising the way quant firms trade markets, starting with Amazon PPC where it autonomously executes tens of millions of dollars of ad spend with 15-40% net-profit improvements within a month of onboarding; closed beta expanding to ChatGPT, Google, Meta, TikTok and Walmart.

24/7 machine-learning analysis; one-click recommendation application; performance scorecards; automated alerts; AI ad copy; multi-channel reporting

## What Laurence does

15-40% net profit improvement within ~30 days (company-reported); tens of millions of dollars of Amazon ad spend under management; systematic portfolio-style allocation; instant free audit
