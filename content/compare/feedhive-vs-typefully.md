# FeedHive vs Typefully

**FeedHive:** Social media automation platform (v2.0) with AI content generation, workflows, AI agents, smart scheduling, recycling and analytics.

**Typefully:** AI-powered writing and scheduling tool for X, LinkedIn, Bluesky and Threads; distraction-free editor with AI writing assistance and analytics.

## At a glance

| | FeedHive | Typefully |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_SOCIAL | AI_SOCIAL |
| Pricing | From $15/mo (tiers $15-$69/mo) | Free plan; Starter ~$14/mo; Team ~$29/mo; Agency/Enterprise up to $199/mo |
| Best for | Founders, marketers and agencies | Individual creators, founders and small teams building audiences |
| Differentiator | Workflow/condition-based automation plus AI agents inside a social scheduler. | Creator-first writing experience with native support for emerging networks (Bluesky, Threads) and 10,000+ customers. |

## What FeedHive does

AI agents, automation workflows, post conditions, content recycling, social inbox, analytics, AI design library, API/CLI, AI-agent integrations.

## What Typefully does

AI ideas & rewrites; distraction-free composer; real-time platform previews; cross-platform scheduling; analytics; social blog repurposing; team plans

## How to choose

Choose **FeedHive** if: Founders, marketers and agencies.

Choose **Typefully** if: Individual creators, founders and small teams building audiences.
