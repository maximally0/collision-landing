## Where XBeast genuinely wins

XBeast's hands-off engagement automation is deeper on X than Collision likely offers; cheap ($12/mo) and fast for solo founders to adopt.

assessment - Collision covers LinkedIn, multi-platform and content strategy; XBeast is X-only, so Collision bundles broader value

## How to decide

Choose **XBeast** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts XBeast doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
