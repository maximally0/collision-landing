# Buffer vs Postwise

**Buffer:** Social media scheduling and publishing platform with built-in AI Assistant (OpenAI) for content ideas, drafting and repurposing; per-channel pricing.

**Postwise:** AI tool to write, schedule and grow on X, LinkedIn and Threads; generates viral-engineered posts and offers growth/DM automations.

## At a glance

| | Buffer | Postwise |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_SOCIAL | AI_SOCIAL |
| Pricing | Free; Essentials $6/channel/mo; Team $12/channel/mo | 7-day free trial; Basic $37/mo; Unlimited $97/mo (annual) |
| Best for | Founders, creators, small marketing teams and agencies | Creators, founders and marketers growing personal brands on X/LinkedIn |
| Differentiator | Per-channel pricing and a genuinely free AI tier make it the default low-cost AI social tool for startups. | Engagement-engineered AI trained on proven posts plus revenue tools (auto-DM, link plugging) inside a social scheduler. |

## What Buffer does

AI Assistant (OpenAI); content idea library; reply suggestions; scheduling across 11+ networks incl. Bluesky/Threads; analytics; approval workflows; API

## What Postwise does

AI viral post & thread generation; one topic to many posts; multi-platform scheduling; GrowthTools follower suite; GhostWriter autopost; auto-send DMs; repost winning content

## How to choose

Choose **Buffer** if: Founders, creators, small marketing teams and agencies.

Choose **Postwise** if: Creators, founders and marketers growing personal brands on X/LinkedIn.
