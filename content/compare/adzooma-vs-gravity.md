# Adzooma vs Gravity

**Adzooma:** AI-powered PPC platform that audits, optimizes and scales Google, Microsoft and Meta campaigns with automated, one-click recommendations.

**Gravity:** Full-stack ad network for AI (founded 2025): demand platform for advertisers, supply platform for AI developers, and an exchange connecting both - placing sponsored, context-matched ads inside ChatGPT and other AI assistants; forward-looking agent-to-agent product feeds product catalogs to AI shopping agents.

## At a glance

| | Adzooma | Gravity |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_ADVERTISING | AI_ADVERTISING |
| Pricing | Free; Pro from ~£69/mo | CPM (rates undisclosed) |
| Best for | SMBs, agencies, freelancers and founders running paid ads | Advertisers (Best Buy, Target, Vercel, MongoDB) and AI platform developers seeking monetization |
| Differentiator | Free AI PPC optimization across three ad networks with two-click implementation | Independent exchange connecting AI demand and supply with real-time auction and first-party measurement, plus first-mover positioning on agent-to-agent commerce (paid information entering agent recommendations, eventually approved transactions) |

## What Adzooma does

24/7 machine-learning analysis; one-click recommendation application; performance scorecards; automated alerts; AI ad copy; multi-channel reporting

## What Gravity does

Millions of ads/day served (company-reported); CPM billing; first-party tracking pixels (no cross-site data sharing); placement in ChatGPT, Codebuff, EcoGPT, Daimon, Magneta, Runable, CTO.new; specialized 1B-param model cutting ad-serving p99 latency ~5.7x and inference cost ~10x (Inference.net case study)

## How to choose

Choose **Adzooma** if: SMBs, agencies, freelancers and founders running paid ads.

Choose **Gravity** if: Advertisers (Best Buy, Target, Vercel, MongoDB) and AI platform developers seeking monetization.
