# Collision vs Pathmonk

- **Pathmonk:** Brand plans from $690/mo; agency plans from $899/mo (Subscription)

## How to decide

Choose **Pathmonk** if their scope matches your motion and you're happy with the capabilities above. Choose **Collision** if you want the whole growth loop — including the parts Pathmonk doesn't cover — under one intelligence with one memory.
