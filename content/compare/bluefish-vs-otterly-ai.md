# Bluefish vs Otterly.ai

**Bluefish:** Agentic Marketing Platform (AMP) for Fortune 500 brands: visibility, influence and measurement of how brands are portrayed by AI tools (ChatGPT, Google AI, Claude, Perplexity, Amazon Rufus). ~10% of the Fortune 500 are customers.

**Otterly.ai:** AEO platform monitoring brand presence across ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews, Gemini and Copilot, with prompt research and AI search analytics.

## At a glance

| | Bluefish | Otterly.ai |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_AEO | AI_AEO |
| Pricing | Enterprise custom | From $29/mo (tiers $29-$489/mo) |
| Best for | Fortune 500 marketing leaders (Adidas, American Express, Hearst, LVMH, Ulta; 12+ verticals incl. financial services, pharma, beauty, luxury, CPG) | Startups, marketers and agencies (40,000+ marketing pros) |
| Differentiator | Enterprise agentic marketing platform for brand portrayal and influence in AI answers - not a monitoring dashboard; Salesforce Ventures and Amex Ventures backed | Dedicated multi-LLM monitoring with prompt-level feedback, Gartner Cool Vendor, G2 4.8. |

## What Bluefish does

Millions of AI prompts/responses processed daily; multi-LLM monitoring (ChatGPT, Google AI, Claude, Perplexity, Amazon Rufus); brand portrayal control; enterprise governance; 80-90 employees

## What Otterly.ai does

Multi-engine tracking (ChatGPT, Perplexity, AIO, Gemini, Copilot), prompt research, AI search analytics, optimization workflows, competitor tracking.

## How to choose

Choose **Bluefish** if: Fortune 500 marketing leaders (Adidas, American Express, Hearst, LVMH, Ulta; 12+ verticals incl. financial services, pharma, beauty, luxury, CPG).

Choose **Otterly.ai** if: Startups, marketers and agencies (40,000+ marketing pros).
