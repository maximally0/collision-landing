# Ahrefs Letaido vs Semrush

**Ahrefs Letaido:** AI marketing agent workspace built by Ahrefs (bootstrapped SEO data incumbent): builds and runs marketing tools, reports, dashboards, scheduled automations and apps with unrestricted access to Ahrefs data endpoints. Launched Aug 12 2026.

**Semrush:** Online visibility management platform (SEO, content, advertising, social, competitive research) with native GEO (LLM Optimizer, AI Overview tracking). Acquired by Adobe for $1.9B (closed Apr 2026) as its brand-visibility engine for the agentic AI era. ~$443.6M revenue (2025).

## At a glance

| | Ahrefs Letaido | Semrush |
|---|---|---|
| Category | AI_SEO | AI_SEO |
| Pricing | $99/mo flat incl $50 AI credits (top-ups as used); separate Ahrefs plan required for full data access | Freemium; Pro from ~$139.95/mo; enterprise custom |
| Best for | Marketing teams, SEO/content/growth teams, agencies, freelancers, ecommerce, SaaS | Marketing teams, SEO professionals, agencies, SMB to enterprise (Amazon, JPMorganChase, TikTok) |
| Differentiator | Only agent with unrestricted internal Ahrefs endpoint access (data beyond the public API/MCP, no API-unit cost) plus purpose-built Ahrefs expertise and a durable cloud workspace | Deepest SEO data estate plus native GEO tooling, now inside Adobe's marketing stack — data and distribution no standalone GEO vendor matches |

## What Ahrefs Letaido does

$99/mo incl $50 AI credits (75% passes through to model providers); 300+ models (Claude/GPT/Gemini); pre-built marketing skills maintained by Ahrefs; 30+ connectors (Slack, HubSpot, Notion, WordPress, Semrush, Mailchimp, Stripe, Gong); scheduled agent runs/webhooks/triggers; persistent workspace + DB + Console + public site; zero Ahrefs API-unit consumption; unlimited team seats

## What Semrush does

25+ tools; LLM Optimizer and AI Overview/ChatGPT/Claude/Copilot/Grok/Perplexity tracking; position tracking; keyword/backlink/site audits; content marketing suite; social management; API; enterprise segment 33% YoY ARR growth

## How to choose

Choose **Ahrefs Letaido** if: Marketing teams, SEO/content/growth teams, agencies, freelancers, ecommerce, SaaS.

Choose **Semrush** if: Marketing teams, SEO professionals, agencies, SMB to enterprise (Amazon, JPMorganChase, TikTok).
