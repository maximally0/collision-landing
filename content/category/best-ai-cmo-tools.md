An AI CMO is a system of AI agents that does what a marketing leader does: research the market, set strategy, produce work, ship it across channels, measure it, and decide what to do next. In 2026 it's crowded — everyone from bootstrapped solo founders to Lightspeed-backed teams sells the same "marketing team in a box" story. The rankings below exist because "AI CMO" now means anything from a content drafting layer to a real send-infrastructure system — and the wrong choice is expensive.

## Methodology — fully disclosed

We scored each product on six criteria, 10 points each, for a maximum of 60. Every fact is from Collision's competitor intelligence database (77 companies, primary-source verified). Pricing reflects **published pricing verified August 2026** — unpublished pricing scores low on that criterion by design. Scores marked as judgment are assessments.

| Criterion | What it measures | Max |
|---|---|---|
| Capability breadth | How much of research → strategy → execution → measurement → optimization the product covers | 10 |
| Autonomy depth | How much of the loop runs without a human in the middle | 10 |
| AI-search focus | SEO, AEO and GEO depth — Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews | 10 |
| Integrations | Channels and tools actually connected | 10 |
| Pricing value | Entry price vs what you get; unverified pricing scores low | 10 |
| Founder fit | Whether the product is built for founders/startups or for teams/enterprise | 10 |

## FAQ

**Are these really CMOs?** The honest answer: they're the execution layer of a CMO. None replaces human taste and relationships; the best ones replace the coordination cost — the stack of ten tools and freelancers that never share context.

**What's the real price range?** Verified published pricing runs from $66/month (Okara) to $499/month (NoimosAI's top tier), with The AI CMO from $299/month and enterprise suites (Simon AI, Salesforce, Adobe) priced by contract. Collision's $500+/month pricing is the published starting point.

**Do I still need humans?** Yes — for taste, relationships and judgment. For most founders: an AI CMO running the loop, with a trusted human bought a la carte on top.

**Should I buy software?** Only with eyes open: you're trading early risk for early positioning. Collision earns this ranking on capability — the only full-loop system here — with published starting pricing. That's the honest trade.
