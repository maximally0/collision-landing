# AI PR

AI PR automates media research, press releases, journalist outreach and coverage tracking.

## Why it matters now

AI systems now answer a growing share of search queries. The brands that appear in those answers get the traffic. The category below is the 2026 landscape of tools competing for that surface.

## The landscape (verified data)

- **[Meltwater](/alternatives/meltwater)** — Scale (27K orgs, 1.3B docs/day) plus AI features and an MCP connector bridging media intelligence into AI agen
- **[Muck Rack](/alternatives/muck-rack)** — Largest verified journalist database plus agentic media-list workflow (10% avg pitch open/click lift in beta)
- **[PRophet](/alternatives/prophet)** — Predictive (not just generative) AI for PR - forecasts journalist interest and sentiment pre-pitch
- **[Signal AI](/alternatives/signal-ai)** — AI-native (founded on NLP/ML) risk+reputation intelligence now majority-backed by Battery Ventures
- **[PressPulse AI](/alternatives/presspulse-ai)** — True AI agent for HARO-style PR lead-gen versus manual query sifting

## How to evaluate a purchase

1. **Does it draft, or does it execute?** Ask what you'd still do yourself after buying it.
2. **Does it remember?** One memory across surfaces beats per-task context.
3. **Who approves?** Approval gates are a feature, not a concession.
4. **Does it learn?** Does the system measure results and change its next move?
5. **What does it replace?** A real system replaces coordination cost, not just one tool.
