# Shopify + Collision

Shopify runs storefronts for many of Collision's customers. Collision supports store growth — content, campaigns and distribution around the store.

## What this unlocks

Collision works where your team already works. With Shopify connected, growth stops being a separate dashboard and becomes part of the loop you're already in.

## Common workflows

- Keep growth drafts and approvals inside Shopify so nothing ships without a human check
- Push research, competitor alerts and performance reports where the team actually reads them
- Coordinate content, outbound and campaigns with the tools your stack already uses
