# HubSpot + Collision

HubSpot holds your CRM and marketing data. Collision connects so campaigns, leads and outbound stay in sync with what HubSpot already knows.

## What this unlocks

Collision works where your team already works. With HubSpot connected, growth stops being a separate dashboard and becomes part of the loop you're already in.

## Common workflows

- Keep growth drafts and approvals inside HubSpot so nothing ships without a human check
- Push research, competitor alerts and performance reports where the team actually reads them
- Coordinate content, outbound and campaigns with the tools your stack already uses
