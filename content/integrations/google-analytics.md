# Google Analytics + Collision

Analytics shows what's working. Collision uses GA4 data to measure which content and channels drive traffic and conversions.

## What this unlocks

Collision works where your team already works. With Google Analytics connected, growth stops being a separate dashboard and becomes part of the loop you're already in.

## Common workflows

- Keep growth drafts and approvals inside Google Analytics so nothing ships without a human check
- Push research, competitor alerts and performance reports where the team actually reads them
- Coordinate content, outbound and campaigns with the tools your stack already uses
