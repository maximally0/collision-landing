# Gmail + Collision

Collision connects to Gmail to run email campaigns and outbound from a real inbox — with human approval on every send.

## What this unlocks

Collision works where your team already works. With Gmail connected, growth stops being a separate dashboard and becomes part of the loop you're already in.

## Common workflows

- Keep growth drafts and approvals inside Gmail so nothing ships without a human check
- Push research, competitor alerts and performance reports where the team actually reads them
- Coordinate content, outbound and campaigns with the tools your stack already uses
