# Slack + Collision

Slack is where most teams talk. Collision connects to Slack so growth work — drafts, approvals, reports, competitor alerts — shows up where the team already is.

## What this unlocks

Collision works where your team already works. With Slack connected, growth stops being a separate dashboard and becomes part of the loop you're already in.

## Common workflows

- Keep growth drafts and approvals inside Slack so nothing ships without a human check
- Push research, competitor alerts and performance reports where the team actually reads them
- Coordinate content, outbound and campaigns with the tools your stack already uses
