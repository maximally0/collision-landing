# Notion + Collision

Notion is where many startups keep their docs and roadmap. Collision can drop research, strategy and content plans into your workspace.

## What this unlocks

Collision works where your team already works. With Notion connected, growth stops being a separate dashboard and becomes part of the loop you're already in.

## Common workflows

- Keep growth drafts and approvals inside Notion so nothing ships without a human check
- Push research, competitor alerts and performance reports where the team actually reads them
- Coordinate content, outbound and campaigns with the tools your stack already uses
