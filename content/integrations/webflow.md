# Webflow + Collision

Webflow is the visual CMS of choice for many startups. Collision syncs written and SEO content into your Webflow site.

## What this unlocks

Collision works where your team already works. With Webflow connected, growth stops being a separate dashboard and becomes part of the loop you're already in.

## Common workflows

- Keep growth drafts and approvals inside Webflow so nothing ships without a human check
- Push research, competitor alerts and performance reports where the team actually reads them
- Coordinate content, outbound and campaigns with the tools your stack already uses
