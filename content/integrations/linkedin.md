# LinkedIn + Collision

LinkedIn is a primary growth surface for founders. Collision plans, drafts and schedules LinkedIn content with approval gates before anything posts.

## What this unlocks

Collision works where your team already works. With LinkedIn connected, growth stops being a separate dashboard and becomes part of the loop you're already in.

## Common workflows

- Keep growth drafts and approvals inside LinkedIn so nothing ships without a human check
- Push research, competitor alerts and performance reports where the team actually reads them
- Coordinate content, outbound and campaigns with the tools your stack already uses
