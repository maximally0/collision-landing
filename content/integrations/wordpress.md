# WordPress + Collision

WordPress powers a large share of the web. Collision publishes SEO-optimized content straight to WordPress — drafts approved, posts shipped.

## What this unlocks

Collision works where your team already works. With WordPress connected, growth stops being a separate dashboard and becomes part of the loop you're already in.

## Common workflows

- Keep growth drafts and approvals inside WordPress so nothing ships without a human check
- Push research, competitor alerts and performance reports where the team actually reads them
- Coordinate content, outbound and campaigns with the tools your stack already uses
