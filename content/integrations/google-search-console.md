# Google Search Console + Collision

Search Console tells you what Google actually sees. Collision reads query and performance data to decide what content to create or fix next.

## What this unlocks

Collision works where your team already works. With Google Search Console connected, growth stops being a separate dashboard and becomes part of the loop you're already in.

## Common workflows

- Keep growth drafts and approvals inside Google Search Console so nothing ships without a human check
- Push research, competitor alerts and performance reports where the team actually reads them
- Coordinate content, outbound and campaigns with the tools your stack already uses
